Overview

Under the Mountain is the Goblin-town deck of The Hobbit set: the goblin horde under the Misty Mountains, amassing one Army and grinding the opponent out with it. Every card in the list feeds a single loop — put +1/+1 counters on your Goblin Army, and let The Great Goblin tax the opponent 2 life for every trigger. Amass sources are everywhere: creatures (Goblin-town Flunkies, Misty Mountains Raider, Fearsome Goblin Pair), spells (Rage into the Valley, Gathering of Darkness, Down, Down to Goblin-town), artifacts (Goblin Plate Mail), and a whole sacrifice-recursion layer that re-fires those triggers from the graveyard.

The engine’s spine is Rhovanion Rampager plus Azog, Moria’s Ruin. The Rampager eats your other creatures to grow, and when it dies it amasses X where X is its power. Azog destroys a creature — often your own enormous Rampager — and amasses X again, drawing a card if the creature was yours. Along the Crooked Way and Gathering of Darkness pull creatures back out of the graveyard, and every creature card that leaves the graveyard amasses 1 more. Each of those triggers is 2 damage from The Great Goblin, and each death also exiles the top card of your library for you to play.

The blue splash is the draw engine, not the beatdown: Uncover the Moon-Letters draws X cards for every noncreature spell (and the discards feed the recursion layer), Plunder the Trollshaws draws with flashback, and The Lord of the Eagles flashes in as a cheap 7/7 finisher, discounted by the power of your Dreaded Bat-Clouds. The lands are part of the kill: Goblin-town sacrifices itself to pump the Army, and Rogue’s Passage opens the road.

The set is scheduled for Arena on 2026-08-14 (arena_id is still empty in the database, so the import list works from that date).

Curve and mana

22 lands, 38 nonlands. The curve tops at 2-drop: 11 (Flunkies, Bothersome, Bolg’s Company, Plate Mail, Gollum, Plunder), 19 three-drops (The Great Goblin, Azog, Fearsome Goblin Pair, Rampager, Rage into the Valley, Along the Crooked Way, Down, Down to Goblin-town), then 4 four-drops (Gathering of Darkness, Uncover the Moon-Letters) and 3 five-drops (Bat-Cloud x2, Misty Mountains Raider), with the single nine-drop Eagles as the flash closer. Black is the core color (28 black pips), red supports (16), and blue is a light splash (5) — ten blue sources keep Uncover and Plunder reliable while the Eagles only ever comes down late. Ten lands enter tapped, so sequence aggressively: tapped fixing first, untapped shock/fast lands on the turns you need to cast your engine on curve.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Turns 1-2 are about assembling the pair: a goblin that amasses (Flunkies, or Bothersome Noisemaker for the spell chain) and The Great Goblin on turn 3. Any early amass creates the 0/0 Army token, so a turn-2 Flunkies into a turn-3 Great Goblin plus Rage into the Valley leaves you with a 3/3 Army, a ping of 4, and a card drawn. Do not be afraid to attack with the Army early — it is also a Goblin, so its death triggers The Great Goblin’s exile-card ability.

Mid game

The Rampager turns on. Attack with it, sacrifice a goblin (Flunkies, a spent Fearsome Goblin Pair, a chump), and the Rampager grows by its power. When it dies — blocking, removal, or Azog — it amasses its full power onto the Army. Azog is the elegant play: destroy your own 8/8 Rampager, amass 8, draw a card, and let The Great Goblin ping. Then Along the Crooked Way or Gathering of Darkness brings the Rampager back for another cycle, each one amassing again.

The combo turn

The kill turn stacks triggers: with The Great Goblin and Bothersome Noisemaker on board, cast Uncover the Moon-Letters (draw 4, discard 2, amass 1, ping 2), then Rage into the Valley (draw, amass 2, ping 2), then Gathering of Darkness (return a creature, amass 3, ping 2) — six damage and eight counters before combat. Sacrifice the Fearsome Goblin Pair to Bolg’s Company for mana (amass 4, ping 2) and replay a creature; the Army is now a 12/12 or larger. Activate Rogue’s Passage and swing for lethal. If the Army is not the path, the same chain alone is the burn win: four noncreature spells is eight damage, and the drains from Down, Down to Goblin-town and Gollum the Abandoned finish the last points.

Win condition 2

The Great Goblin’s burn is a real alternate kill. Every amass trigger is 2 damage, so the deck does not need combat to win: Bothersome Noisemaker plus any spell chain, Fearsome Goblin Pair deaths, and the Along the Crooked Way recursion loop each re-fire the ping. Down, Down to Goblin-town’s final chapters drain 1 per turn, and Gollum the Abandoned drains 2 and exiles graveyard threats every time it recurs. The Lord of the Eagles is the flying backstop — flash it in after combat to threaten lethal in the air.

Mulligan guide

Keep any hand with 2-3 lands (prefer untapped or a mix), a two-drop, and a path to The Great Goblin. A hand with four tapped lands and no two-drop is a mulligan. Hands with Rampager plus a sacrifice target or Azog are premium. Rogue’s Passage is fine to keep with any Army plan; Cavern of Souls is best when you need The Great Goblin to resolve against blue decks. Boiling Rock Prison and the guildgates are the weakest lands — trim them mentally when judging color fixing.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • The Army is a Goblin: it counts for Bolg’s Company’s haste condition, Bolg’s Company can sacrifice it for {B}{R}, Goblin-town can pump it, and its death triggers The Great Goblin. Keep it alive unless the exchange wins the game.
  • Azog can target your own creature: destroying your own Rhovanion Rampager amasses X, draws a card, and — because it is a destruction, not a sacrifice — it works even if the opponent has no creatures.
  • Along the Crooked Way’s amass fires for every creature card leaving your graveyard — including the Rampager being returned to hand, so the recursion cycle amasses twice per loop.
  • Uncover the Moon-Letters’ discard is a cost, not a may: always discard creatures (or lands) to feed Along the Crooked Way and Gathering of Darkness.
  • Dreaded Bat-Cloud discounts by any death that turn — your own sacrifices count, so it is often a 1/2-mana 4/3 deathtouch flyer.
  • The Lord of the Eagles counts total flying power of creatures you control, not just your own turns — flash it during the opponent’s declare-blockers step if they attack into your Bat-Clouds.
  • Gollum the Abandoned cannot block — it is a raider, not a roadblock.
  • Down, Down to Goblin-town’s chapter I forces a discard; chapter III/IV drain on resolution, so the saga always chips even against removal-heavy decks.
