Scale and Coin

Grixis (UBR) Smaug Dragon × Treasure — The Hobbit set identity deck

hobbit

Attribute Value

Core mechanic Treasure generation × artifact-ETB draw × dragon payoffs

Secondary win Dragon swarm — The Misty Mountains Cold’s 6/6 Dragon, Magda’s 4/4 haste Dragon tokens, Smaug Wicked Worm and Rapacious Dragon beat down

Overview

Scale and Coin is the Grixis Smaug deck. Every spell either makes a Treasure, draws off the Treasures being made, or is a Dragon that profits from them. The engine loops two abilities: Fateful Discovery — “whenever an artifact you control enters, draw a card” — turns every Treasure token into a cantrip, and Smaug the Magnificent — a 4/3 flying haste Dragon that creates a Treasure each upkeep and then deals damage equal to your Treasure count to any target whenever it attacks.

The red shell is the Smaug identity: The Misty Mountains Cold (a saga whose four chapters each make a Treasure and then become a 6/6 Dragon), Desolation of Smaug (a 3-damage sweep that also ramps four mana into a Dragon), Last Light of Durin’s Day (a Mountain-quest that tutors a Dragon into play), and Magda, the Hoardmaster (crimes make Treasures; three Treasures make a 4/4 haste Dragon). Blue is the draw engine — Fateful Discovery, Stock Up, Collector’s Vault — and black brings The Sackville-Bagginses, Nowhere to Run and Smaug, Wicked Worm, who converts your own Treasure casts into cards.

The deck wins by out-drawing the opponent while the Treasure count climbs, then pointing Smaug’s attack trigger — or a wave of Dragon tokens — at the opponent’s face. It is a value-tempo combo deck: it plays like a ramp-draw engine for the first turns and like a burn deck the moment Smaug or a 6/6 Dragon token lands.

Curve and mana

Twenty-five lands: seventeen basics (7 Mountain, 6 Island, 6 Swamp) plus eight nonbasics (3 The Lonely Mountain, 1 Cavern of Souls, 2 Izzet Guildgate). The red base comes down early for Magda and Sarkhan; the two guildgates and Cavern cover the blue splash while Swamps support black. The curve is engine-shaped: six two-drop plays (Magda, Sarkhan, Sackville-Bagginses, Collector’s Vault, Gleaming Barrier, Last Light), four three-drops (Dori, Misty saga, Stock Up), and the four-to-five mana payoff cluster (Smaug the Magnificent, Desolation of Smaug, Wicked Worm, Rapacious Dragon, Fateful Discovery). Treasures effectively advance the curve by a full turn — a turn-three Dori or Misty saga makes turn-four Smaug routine.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Lead with Magda (crimes feed her Treasure), Sarkhan (behold a Dragon from hand for a Treasure), The Sackville-Bagginses, or Collector’s Vault. Gleaming Barrier stalls aggro while setting up a death-Treasure. Do not churn Treasures for mana unless the play matters — the Treasure count is both your mana and your damage. Play toward Fateful Discovery on four or five: from that point every Treasure ETB replaces itself.

Mid game

Resolve The Misty Mountains Cold or Dori to start the Treasure train, then Fateful Discovery. Each subsequent Treasure — Smaug’s upkeep trigger, Magda’s crime Treasure, Sackville’s draw-Treasure, a dying Gleaming Barrier, Collector’s Vault — draws a card. This is where the deck pulls ahead: Treasure count climbs and the hand refills every turn, so removal and counters stay stocked. Nowhere to Run clears hexproof/ward threats at flash speed.

The combo turn

With Fateful Discovery and Magda on board and a Treasure count of five or more: commit a crime (Nowhere to Run on their creature) for a tapped Treasure — that ETB draws a card. Sacrifice three Treasures to Magda for a 4/4 haste Dragon, whose entry grows Sarkhan and (if Sarkhan is out) makes him a flying Dragon too. Cast Smaug the Magnificent, then attack: he deals damage equal to the remaining Treasures to any target — removal or face. Desolation of Smaug is the reset button: it sweeps their non-Dragon board, then its four floating mana cast Wicked Worm or Rapacious Dragon the same turn.

Win condition 2

The Dragon swarm. The Misty Mountains Cold resolves fully into a 6/6 flying Dragon; Magda converts three Treasures into haste Dragons on demand; Smaug, Wicked Worm is a 5/5 flyer that can enter with a Treasure per opposing artifact; Rapacious Dragon brings two Treasures and a 3/3 flyer. Multiple evasive Dragons end games that Smaug’s ping can’t reach — especially against lifegain, where the attack-trigger damage plus flyer beats outlasts incidental life.

Mulligan guide

Keep any hand with two or three lands and a two-drop engine (Magda, Sarkhan, Sackville-Bagginses, Collector’s Vault). Hands with Smaug the Magnificent plus any Treasure source are strong. Ship one-landers, hands with three or more five-drops, and hands with no red source. Against aggressive decks, prioritize Gleaming Barrier and Nowhere to Run over the slow draw pieces; against control, keep Spell Pierce and lean on Cavern of Souls naming Dragon.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Smaug’s attack trigger deals damage equal to your Treasure count at the moment the trigger resolves — sacrifice Treasures for mana after declaring attackers, not before, if you want maximum damage.
  • Fateful Discovery triggers on artifacts entering, not tokens specifically: Treasure tokens, Gleaming Barrier, Collector’s Vault, and Cavern-free artifact creatures all draw. The Misty Mountains Cold’s four chapter triggers each draw a card with Fateful Discovery out.
  • The Misty Mountains Cold checks for four or more Treasures after each chapter trigger, then sacrifices itself for a 6/6 Dragon — it needs four Treasures, not four counters, so time your Treasure spends carefully if you want the Dragon rather than the mana.
  • Magda’s Treasure trigger fires once per turn regardless of how many crimes you commit — one crime per turn is enough.
  • “Commit a crime” means targeting the opponent, anything they control, or cards in their graveyard — Nowhere to Run, removal, and even a counterspell aimed at their spell are crimes.
  • Desolation of Smaug damages non-Dragon creatures — your Dragon tokens and Dragons survive while everything else on both sides takes 3. The four mana it adds can only cast Dragon spells.
  • The Sackville-Bagginses’ token-sacrifice ping includes Treasure tokens sacrificed for mana — cracking a Treasure at the right moment doubles as a Shock.
  • Cavern of Souls must name Dragon before it taps for the uncounterable any-color mana; it only protects Dragon creature spells.
  • Wicked Worm draws when you cast a spell with mana from a Treasure — mana from Cavern, basic lands or treasures all mix, so this triggers only if Treasure mana was actually spent on that spell.

Matchups

  • Aggro (fliers, lifegain, cats): Gleaming Barrier and Nowhere to Run hold the ground until Desolation of Smaug resets the board; the deck’s draw engine finds answers faster than aggro refills. Smaug’s ping doubles as removal for their biggest threat.
  • Midrange (landfall, counters): the Treasure-draw engine out-values them — every Treasure replaces itself and every Dragon is bigger than their curve; Desolation clears their developed board and ramps into a Dragon on the same turn.
  • Control: Cavern of Souls (naming Dragon), Spell Pierce and Wicked Worm’s treasure-cast draw keep gas flowing; the saga and enchantment engines (Misty Mountains Cold, Fateful Discovery, Last Light of Durin’s Day) are hard for sweepers to answer permanently.
  • Combo and big mana: the deck is the aggressor — haste Smaug and Magda Dragons pressure before their engine comes online, and Nowhere to Run plus Spell Pierce disrupt key pieces.
