Storyforge

Naya (R/W/G) Storied Dwarves — Equipment engine with a green artifact splash

dwarves

Overview

Storyforge is a red-white Dwarf legendary core with a small green artifact engine bolted on, built around the Hobbit set’s Storied mechanic: once you control three or more artifacts, legendaries or Sagas, every legendary Dwarf in the deck flips on at the same time. The cheap side of the deck provides those three pieces for free — Axe equipment tokens from Iron Hills Blacksmith and Dáin Ironfoot, Dwarven Mattock, the legendary creatures themselves — so Storied is normally live by turn three.

The engine is looped rather than linear. Kíli the Resourceful and Giott, King of the Dwarves both convert Dwarf and Equipment entries into cards, Dáin’s Company digs four deep for the next Dwarf or Equipment, and Elvish Archivist turns every artifact you drop into two +1/+1 counters while Warleader’s Call pays you a card. Everything you draw is either another artifact, another legend, or a way to make the artifacts hit harder.

Curve and mana

The deck curves out at 1-2 and stops there on purpose: 4 one-drops, 23 two-drops, 3 three-drops and 6 four-drops. Red is the primary colour (Dáin Ironfoot, Dwarven Mauler, Leyline Axe, Erode is white), white supplies the legendary Dwarf core, and green is a three-card splash for Elvish Archivist and Sandstorm Salvager off Gathering Place, Training Compound and the two Forests. Fifteen sources produce white, twelve produce red, and seven produce green — enough for a splash that only wants to see one green card a game.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Open on Dwarven Mauler or a two-drop legend rather than on an Equipment: the storied count is what turns the deck on, and the legends are themselves artifacts-or-legendaries. Iron Hills Blacksmith is the best turn two — it enters with an Axe, attacks for two with double strike, and gives you a free artifact for the Storied check. Kíli the Resourceful on two is the value line: hold the first Dwarf or Equipment of the turn and it draws you a card.

Mid game

Turn three to five is where the deck separates from a vanilla tribal deck. Dáin’s Company re-fills the hand and finds the missing piece, Giott and Kíli make every subsequent permanent a cantrip, and Elvish Archivist grows into the biggest creature on the board if the artifact half of the deck shows up. Keep one Equipment unattached if you are about to cast Thorin, Mountain-king — it attaches everything on entry, so every extra Equipment is extra damage.

The combo turn

The killing turn is assembled from three pieces that are each fine on their own:

  1. An Equipment on a Dwarf — Dwarven Mattock (+2/+2, ward) or Leyline Axe (double strike and trample).
  2. Dáin Ironfoot, whose attack trigger gives every equipped attacking creature double strike.
  3. Dwalin, Weaponmaster, whose attack trigger puts a hone counter on each Equipment ({+1}/{+0} per counter).

With Mattock on a Dwarven Mauler and Dáin Ironfoot attacking, a single 2/1 becomes a 5/4 double striker for eight damage, and Dwalin’s hone counters scale every Equipment you control, including the Axe tokens the Blacksmith and Dáin hand you. Kíli’s Storied ability makes the first equip of that turn free, so the whole package costs nothing extra to assemble and Fíli’s +1/+1 is applied on top of it.

Win condition 2

When the combat route is bottled up, the deck wins off tokens. The Lonely Mountain activates for {4}{R} minus {1} for each Equipment you control — with three Equipment that is {1}{R} for a 2/2 Dwarf, every turn, forever. Fíli the Pathfinder makes a 2/2 token off every nontoken Dwarf entry, Sandstorm Salvager makes a 3/3 Golem artifact token and then puts +1/+1 counters and trample on the whole token team, and Warleader’s Call turns each of those entries into a point of damage to each opponent. Leyline Axe and Dwarven Mattock sit on whichever token lives, so the tokens are not simply chump blockers.

Mulligan guide

Keep any hand with two or three lands, one one-drop or Blacksmith, and one Equipment. A hand with Leyline Axe plus The Lonely Mountain is a keep even on four lands: the Axe begins on the battlefield, which turns on the untapped Mountain and puts you one permanent away from Storied before you cast anything. Ship hands that are all four-drops, and ship hands with three or more colourless lands (Gathering Place and Training Compound only produce coloured mana once you control a basic land).

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Leyline Axe starts on the battlefield when it is in your opening hand. That is a free Storied piece and it leaves The Lonely Mountain untapped on turn one.
  • Dwarven Mauler discounts equip abilities that target it — it does not make them free, but it makes Leyline Axe’s and Mattock’s {3} equips cost {1}.
  • Dáin Ironfoot’s Axe token counts toward Storied and is the cheapest equip in the deck at {2}, which Dwarven Mauler reduces to nothing.
  • Kíli the Resourceful triggers only once per turn: sequence a Dwarf first, then an Equipment, so the single draw lands when you want it.
  • Giott, King of the Dwarves triggers on your own entry as well as on each Dwarf and Equipment, so he is a three-card dig engine by himself once the board is assembled.
  • Thorin, Mountain-king’s trigger deals damage equal to the attached creature’s power to a target creature, so it is removal, not reach: point it at the biggest blocker before combat.
  • Elvish Archivist has two separate once-per-turn triggers — artifacts give counters, enchantments draw cards. Warleader’s Call entering is a card, and each subsequent Equipment is two counters.
  • Gathering Place and Training Compound produce coloured mana only if the land entered this turn or you control a basic land. Play them behind a basic whenever possible; otherwise they are a {C} source for that turn.
  • Erode is unconditional, but the opponent fetches a basic land. Use it on the creature that actually kills you, not on the first thing that attacks.
  • Sandstorm Salvager’s Golem is an artifact creature token: it counts for Storied, it is a legal target for its own pump ability, and it carries Equipment.
