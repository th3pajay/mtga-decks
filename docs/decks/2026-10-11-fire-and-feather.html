Overview

The Hobbit set hides a spellslinger engine inside three cards. Gandalf, Goblins’ Bane is a three-mana 2/3 that reads: whenever you cast a noncreature spell, he gets +1/+1 and deals 1 damage to each opponent. Glamdring, Foe-hammer is a two-mana Equipment with no power bonus at all — instead it reduces the cost of every instant and sorcery you cast by the equipped creature’s power. Equip Glamdring to Gandalf and the two halves lock together: the Equipment is cheaper than any lord, and the discount scales with the very creature the spells are feeding.

The rest of the deck exists to (a) turn the discount on and (b) keep the spell stream flowing. An Unexpected Party names Wizard and gives every Gandalf plus two plus two — eight Wizards in the deck, and each point of power on the equipped creature is another point off every spell. Flameshape, Gandalf’s own Adventure, exiles the top two cards and lets you play them while you control a Wizard; since Gandalf is a Wizard, he turns himself on. Uncover the Moon-Letters trades each noncreature cast for cards, and Riddles in the Dark tops the hand up in piles an opponent has to split for you.

The pressure comes from the air. Eagle of the Great Shelf is a 2/5 flier that adds a counter-per-other-creature every attack, and the Lake-town recruit creatures — Long Lake Nuisance and Esgaroth Garrison — draw a card, discard a card and leave a 1/1 white Soldier behind when the discard is not a land. That Soldier is another body for the Eagle and another point of power for the Garrison, and the draw is another chance to find Gandalf or Glamdring. The two Restless manlands finish the job: Restless Anchorage becomes a 2/3 flying Bird, Restless Spire a 2/1 first striker, so even a stalled board keeps attacking.

Twenty-four lands, four of them fetches and two of them manlands, is enough to hit four lands on turn four in 64 percent of games. The average nonland cost sits at three, but the deck rarely taps out for it: Glamdring’s discount means the expensive half of the curve is paid for by the creature holding the sword, and the Adventures provide a second spell for the same card.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Open on Bilbo, Luckwearer or hold one mana for Bilbo’s Gambit. The goal for the first three turns is a Gandalf, Goblins’ Bane on the battlefield and a Glamdring in hand or on the stack — nothing else matters as much, because every turn after that the pair converts one card into a point of damage to the opponent. Gaze in Wonder is the best Turn-one play against a deck that tries to race: tapping their two best creatures is often a full turn of tempo.

Mid game and the combo turn

Sequence the turn so the ping happens as many times as possible. Cast the Adventure half first when it is cheaper — Flameshape for one red, then Gandalf — because the Adventure is a noncreature spell that triggers him. With Uncover the Moon-Letters on the battlefield, cast spells from smallest to largest: each one draws X where X is the mana actually spent, so a discounted four-mana spell still draws four and the discards feed the graveyard for Moment of Glory’s flashback. Once Glamdring is attached to a four- or five-power Wizard, Riddles in the Dark, Bilbo’s Gambit and Moment of Glory all cost one or less, and the loop becomes: cast, ping, draw, discard, cast again.

Win condition 2

When the ground stalls, switch to the air. Eagle of the Great Shelf plus two or three Soldiers makes every attack a one-shot kill attempt, and An Unexpected Party naming Bird instead of Wizard turns Long Lake Nuisance, Velvetwing Butterflies and the Restless Anchorage Bird into a real clock — the naming choice is the deck’s main decision point, and against a removal-heavy opponent Bird is usually right because it spreads the bonus across more bodies over more turns. At the Door is the third option: name Dwarf after making X Dwarf tokens for an instant anthem.

Mulligan guide

Keep any two- to four-land hand that has a Gandalf, Goblins’ Bane or a Glamdring, and keep hands with an Evolving Wilds plus any two colours, because the fetch repairs the rest. Pitch hands with no blue source and no Gandalf — the deck cannot function without either; pitch hands that need two of the three shock lands untapped with only one land; and pitch hands with three or more five-plus drops and no early interaction.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • An Unexpected Party’s type is chosen as it enters, so decide before the first ping of the turn: Wizard grows the engine, Bird or Dwarf grows the clock.
  • Glamdring’s discount is fixed by the equipped creature’s power at the moment of casting, so equip first, then cast. An Unexpected Party naming Wizard raises the discount by two on every Wizard.
  • Flameshape needs a Wizard to play the exiled cards. Gandalf, Goblins’ Bane is a Wizard, so he satisfies his own Adventure; if he dies before you play them, they stay exiled face down.
  • Uncover the Moon-Letters draws X where X is mana spent, not mana value, so a discounted spell draws less. Cast the big spells before Glamdring’s discount eats them.
  • Moment of Glory’s flashback is a sorcery cast from the graveyard; it gives the counter to each other creature only when cast from a graveyard, so the first copy is a single counter and the flashback is a team pump.
  • Bilbo’s Gambit returns target spell to its owner’s hand. The gift clause is optional but the Treasure is a real cost — give it when the lock matters more than the mana.
  • Recruit discards, so a discarded nonland card makes a Soldier: with Esgaroth Garrison out, every recruit is also a power increase.
  • Restless Anchorage enters tapped; do not count it as a land drop you can use, and remember it stops being a creature at end of turn, so it cannot block after attacking.

Matchups

Opponent Plan
Aggro Trade early with Bilbo’s Gambit and Gaze in Wonder, then race with the ping, which does not care about blockers. First-strike Restless Spire blocks well
Midrange Do not fight their best creature: keep Glamdring’s discount online, cast two spells a turn and let Gandalf grow past their blockers
Control Hold An Unexpected Party until you can also cast a spell the same turn, and hold Bilbo’s Gambit for their sweeper — the gift clause means they cannot cast anything else that turn
Ramp Race. The ping plus flying damage is faster than a seven-mana payoff, and Flameshape’s exiled cards keep the pressure on after a wipe
