R/U/G/W — Warp × Convoke × Delirium

At a glance

Format Standard (60 cards)
Colors Red, Blue, Green, White
Archetype Midrange combo (enter-the-battlefield burn)
Engine Warp recursion plus enter-the-battlefield damage, doubled and then doubled again
Primary win Weftstalker Ardent and Shocking Sharpshooter pings, amplified by Starfield Vocalist, Collective Inferno and The Rollercrusher Ride
Secondary win Delirium tramplers on extra combats, with Rogue’s Passage for the final swing
Land that wins Fountainport, which manufactures a fresh creature every turn, and Rogue’s Passage, which removes the last blocker

Overview

Warpfeed Chorus is a damage engine disguised as a midrange deck. Nearly every creature in the list does something the moment it enters the battlefield, and two of them convert that moment into damage. Warp turns those one-shot entries into a repeatable resource: the same creature arrives twice, once at its cheap warp cost and once later out of exile, and each arrival re-triggers the board.

Starfield Vocalist is the multiplier: every permanent entry that already triggered a permanent you control triggers an additional time. Collective Inferno (naming Drix) doubles every damage source you control, and The Rollercrusher Ride doubles noncombat damage again once delirium is live. Delirium is the fuel gauge rather than a side theme. Codecracker Hound, Fear of Missing Out, artifact creatures and the enchantments fill the graveyard with distinct card types, so the doubling turns on around turn four or five and stays on. Convoke pays for the six-mana finishers with the board the engine was already building.

Approach

Game plan phases

Phase Turns What you are doing
Establish 1-3 Deploy Shocking Sharpshooter or Weftstalker Ardent, then add artifact creatures. Every entry is a point of damage.
Multiply 3-5 Land Starfield Vocalist. From here each entry pings twice and each Warp cast pings twice on both halves.
Double 4-6 Turn delirium on, deploy The Rollercrusher Ride, then Collective Inferno naming Drix. Pings now read 4 damage each.
Convert 5+ Either burn them out with the engine, or switch to tramplers plus an extra combat and finish with Rogue’s Passage.

Curve and mana

Mana value 1 2 3 4 5
Spells 0 12 15 7 2

Thirty-six spells average 2.97 mana over 24 lands: seven Mountain, seven Forest, four Island, three Plains, plus the two colorless engine lands. Red is primary (pingers, Inferno, Rollercrusher), green supplies delirium payoffs and convoke bodies, blue carries the multiplier and the graveyard filter, and white is a light splash. Because the top of the curve is convoke, the deck effectively stops at four.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Open on a pinger. Shocking Sharpshooter on two is the ideal start because it costs a single red and trades with nothing, but Weftstalker Ardent on three with warp mana held up is stronger against removal: cast it for R at the end of their turn, ping, and keep the real card in exile. Pinnacle Emissary is the best turn-three follow-up because the Drone it makes on cast is a second entry, and both entries feed the pingers.

Do not crack Fountainport early. The Fish is worth more as a pinger trigger than as a blocker, and holding three mana open on their turn is how you punish a tapped-out opponent.

Mid game

Starfield Vocalist is the pivot. Before it, the deck does one damage per entry; after it, two. Warp creatures are the fuel: cast Weftstalker Ardent for R, ping twice, let it exile, then cast it again for 2R later for two more pings. Codecracker Hound warps on the same pattern and stocks the graveyard while it does it, which is how delirium arrives without dedicated milling.

All-Fates Stalker is your interaction. Warped for 1W it exiles their best creature until the Stalker leaves, which usually means permanently, and as a Drix it also benefits from Collective Inferno’s chosen type.

The combo turn

The kill wants three pieces on board: a pinger, Starfield Vocalist, and Collective Inferno naming Drix. Add The Rollercrusher Ride and each entry deals four damage to each opponent; with two pingers it is eight per entry. Then cast anything. A single Warped creature is two entries, and every token, Drone and Treasure-maker is another trigger. Convoke makes the six-mana pieces free: tap Drones, Fish and spent attackers to cast Collective Inferno, then untap and keep pinging. Selfless Safewright is the insurance policy, flashed in with convoke to give the board hexproof and indestructible for the turn.

Win condition two

Delirium beatdown. Wildfire Wickerfolk is a hasty 3/2 that becomes a 4/3 trampler, Omnivorous Flytrap distributes and then doubles counters, and Fear of Missing Out untaps a creature and grants an additional combat phase once four card types are in the yard. Pair it with Beastie Beatdown to clear the one blocker that matters, then use Rogue’s Passage on the biggest trampler. This line matters most against decks that gain life or that answer the pingers, because it does not care how much damage the engine has already done.

Mulligan guide

Keep any hand with a pinger plus two lands. Keep one-pinger hands with Pinnacle Emissary or Codecracker Hound as the second body. Ship hands with no red source, and ship hands that are all four-drops, even if the four-drops are good, because this deck wins by stacking small entries and loses when it does nothing until turn four.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Warp exiles at the beginning of the next end step. If the creature is removed in response, it still exiles, so a Warped creature dodges sorcery-speed removal but not instant removal aimed at the exile line.
  • Starfield Vocalist triggers on the entry, not the cast. Tokens count: the Fountainport Fish, the Pinnacle Emissary Drone and every Treasure-making entry are triggers.
  • Collective Inferno checks the source’s type at the time damage is dealt. Name Drix when you have Weftstalkers or All-Fates Stalkers to convert; name Robot when your damage comes from Drones.
  • The Rollercrusher Ride needs delirium at the moment damage is dealt, so sequence Codecracker Hound and Fear of Missing Out before the first big ping turn. Delirium counts card types, not cards, and a single artifact creature covers artifact and creature at once.
  • Violent Urge gives double strike, so put it on the trampler, never on a pinger.

Matchups

Opponent Plan
Fast aggro Trade early with Shocking Sharpshooter and Beastie Beatdown, then stabilise on Wildfire Wickerfolk and Omnivorous Flytrap. The pingers block well enough at three toughness.
Control Warp is your answer to sorcery-speed interaction: hold creatures in exile and re-cast them after they tap out. Do not commit two pingers to the same turn.
Midrange value Your engine out-grinds theirs as long as Starfield Vocalist lives. All-Fates Stalker on their key threat is usually the whole game.
Lifegain Switch to the delirium combat plan early; the burn plan alone will not get there against repeated lifegain.
Combo Race with pings. You are faster when a pinger lands on turn two, and All-Fates Stalker buys the turn you need.
