Overview

Banner Fall fuses token creation with the Tarkir Mobilize mechanic. Every Mobilize creature attacks and creates tapped-and-attacking 1/1 red Warrior tokens that are sacrificed at the beginning of the next end step. That forced sacrifice is not a drawback — it is the engine. The warriors die every single end step, and the deck is built so that every death deals damage: Garna, Bloodfist of Keld pings each opponent for 1 when a non-attacking creature dies, Venerated Stormsinger drains 1 and gains 1 life per death, and Vengeful Bloodwitch repeats that drain. One attacking Avenger with a stocked graveyard means four, five or more warriors dying on end step, which is 12-15 damage to each opponent before your next turn even starts.

The token creation half of the fusion supplies the board and the fodder: Dragon Fodder and Goblin Surprise make Goblins that attack and feed Corrupted Conviction (sacrifice a creature, draw two) and Betrayer’s Bargain (sacrifice a creature, deal 5). Every token is a card, a removal spell or a damage trigger — nothing is wasted. Great Train Heist adds a second combat phase, doubling the Mobilize triggers, and Devastating Onslaught turns one Mobilizer into a horde of haste copies that all die at once, converting the whole board into one enormous drain. The average mana value is 2.5 and the deck is aggressive enough to win on board alone while the drain engine grinds in the background.

Approach

Game plan phases

Phase What happens
Turns 1-3 Curve out: Stadium Headliner or Shock Brigade attacks on turn 2, creating the first warriors; Dragon Fodder and Corrupted Conviction turn early tokens into cards
Turns 3-5 Engine online: Garna and Venerated Stormsinger hit the board, so every end step converts dead warriors into damage and life; Avenger of the Fallen starts counting the graveyard
Turns 5-7 The combo turn: Devastating Onslaught copies a Mobilizer with haste, Great Train Heist adds a second combat, and one end step resolves 15-25 death triggers for lethal damage
Late game Grind or finish: Restless Vents loots the board into Avenger fuel, Rogue’s Passage delivers the unblockable Avenger swing, and the drain clock never stops

Curve and mana

22 lands, 38 nonlands: ten one- and two-mana cards (Stadium Headliner, Shock Brigade, Corrupted Conviction, Dragon Fodder), a strong four-mana top end (Garna, Stormsinger, Avenger) and a 2.5 average mana value. Red and black split the pips almost evenly; the mana base is nine dual sources plus four basics, with Cavern of Souls and Rogue’s Passage as colorless utility. Sequence the tapped lands (Raucous Theater, Restless Vents) early and keep Blood Crypt and Blazemire Verge for the curve.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Stadium Headliner on turn 1 attacks on turn 2 for a warrior; Shock Brigade with menace usually connects for two more. Dragon Fodder fills the board, and the first Corrupted Conviction converts a token into two cards. Prioritize attacking with Mobilizers over trading — the warriors they create are worth more dying on your end step than the Mobilizer is worth in combat. Discard creature cards to Restless Vents liberally; every one makes Avenger’s next Mobilize bigger.

Mid game

Garna and Venerated Stormsinger are the priority plays from turn 4 on. Once both are down, a single Shock Brigade attack is 3 damage from the warrior, 1 from Garna and 1 from the Stormsinger as it dies, plus 2 life — and the attack damage itself. Start counting: with two Mobilizers attacking, each end step is 6-8 damage to each opponent. Use Betrayer’s Bargain on anything that blocks, and hold Corrupted Conviction for end-of-turn or in response to removal, trading a token for two cards.

The combo turn

Cast Great Train Heist during the first combat phase to create a second combat; every Mobilizer attacks twice, doubling the warrior wave. Then Devastating Onslaught with X equal to your graveyard count (or as much mana as you have): the copies enter with haste, attack, and each triggers Mobilize. With X 3-4 and a stocked graveyard, one end step resolves 15-25 deaths — Garna pings each opponent for 1 per death, Venerated Stormsinger drains 1 per death, and Vengeful Bloodwitch drains 1 per death. That is 30-60 damage spread across the table in a single end step, with life gain to match. The Onslaught copies themselves die into the same drain.

Win condition 2

If the combo pieces do not come together, the drain clock wins anyway. Every turn, Mobilize warriors die and ping for 3 apiece; Restless Vents loots and attacks; Corrupted Conviction keeps the hand full. The deck simply out-values and out-drains attrition decks. For the closing swing, Rogue’s Passage makes Avenger of the Fallen unblockable — a deathtouch 2/4 hitting face while X warriors flood in, then the end step drains for the rest.

Mulligan guide

Keep any hand with two or three lands including at least one red and one black source, and a one- or two-drop play (Stadium Headliner, Shock Brigade, Dragon Fodder or Corrupted Conviction). Hands with only colorless lands and no colored source are risky. A hand with a Mobilizer and a Corrupted Conviction is premium. Mulligan hands with no early play or more than two tapped lands. Garna and Stormsinger are worth keeping on four lands even without early creatures.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Mobilize warriors are created tapped and attacking, then sacrificed at the beginning of the next end step — they are no longer attacking then, so Garna pings rather than draws for them; combat-trade deaths (while still attacking) draw cards instead.
  • Garna’s ping, Stormsinger’s drain and Bloodwitch’s drain are separate triggers, so one dead warrior is 3 damage to each opponent and 2 life for you with all three out.
  • Great Train Heist’s extra combat requires casting it during a combat phase; warriors from the first combat stay on the board (tapped and attacking) through the second and all die together at the end step.
  • Devastating Onslaught copies enter as tokens, so they are not legendary duplicates of Garna issues — but do not copy a creature with a legend rule problem; copy Mobilizers.
  • Stadium Headliner’s sacrifice ability counts all creatures you control — with a warrior wave on board it deals 6-10 damage to a blocker, then the sacrificed Headliner itself drains.
  • Cavern of Souls mana only pays for creature spells of the chosen type; name Warrior for the core.
  • Betrayer’s Bargain exiles the creature if it would die this turn, which is relevant against graveyard decks.
  • Restless Vents enters tapped; animate it only when the activation is worth the mana, usually to loot or to close with menace.

Matchups

Aggro: the drain engine races their clock — every death gains life while pinging them, and menace attackers force bad blocks. Control: Cavern of Souls makes the Warrior core uncounterable and Corrupted Conviction turns their removal into two cards for you; grind them through card advantage. Midrange: the board fills faster than theirs and Devastating Onslaught turns one trade into a 20-point end step; save Betrayer’s Bargain for their key threat.
