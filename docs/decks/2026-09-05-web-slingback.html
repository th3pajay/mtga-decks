Green-white | Web-slinging — bounce a tapped creature, cast Spiders for cheap, and overrun

Overview

Web-slinging is an alternative cost: “You may cast this spell for its web-slinging cost if you also return a tapped creature you control to its owner’s hand.” Every creature that attacked, or that tapped for mana, becomes payment for a discount. Spider-Man, Web-Slinger (normally {2}{W}) slings for {W}; Silk, Web Weaver ({2}{G}{W}) slings for {1}{G}{W}; Spider-Man India ({3}{G}{W}) slings for {1}{G}{W}; Spider-Man, Brooklyn Visionary ({4}{G}) slings for {2}{G}. The deck is built so that bouncing and recasting is a feature, not a cost: green mana dorks tap for mana and then become the toll, attackers tap in combat and then become the toll, and every recast fires the deck’s payoffs.

The engine is a cast-and-enter loop. Mary Jane Watson draws a card whenever a Spider enters (once per turn); Aunt May gains life and puts a +1/+1 counter on every Spider that enters; Silk, Web Weaver creates a 1/1 Human Citizen for every creature spell cast and later turns the whole team into an overrun with its {3}{G}{W} activation; Spider-Man India puts a +1/+1 counter on a creature and gives it flying whenever you cast a creature spell. A single web-slinging turn with three creatures to return chains all four engines at once.

Approach

Game plan in three phases

  1. Set up the sling line (turns 1-3): Aunt May or Llanowar Elves on turn 1, Mary Jane Watson and a second dork or Spider on turn 2, then Spider-Man, Web-Slinger or Get Lost on turn 3. Creatures that attack or tap for mana become the web-slinging toll.
  2. Chain the recasts (turns 4-6): attack with the team, then cast discounted Spiders by returning tapped creatures. Each cast draws (Mary Jane), gains life and counters (Aunt May), makes a Citizen (Silk) and spreads counters (India). Silk and Spider-UK are the best follow-up plays on the same turn they untap into the engine.
  3. Overrun (turn 5+): with five or six creatures on board, activate Silk’s {3}{G}{W} for +2/+2 and vigilance, animate Restless Prairie for an extra +1/+1 to everything as it attacks, and swing. Web-Warriors and Origin of Spider-Man make the board big enough to kill through blockers.

Curve and mana

24 lands: 11 basics (7 Forest, 4 Plains) plus 4 Hushwood Verge, 4 Restless Prairie, 4 Gathering Place and 1 Three Tree City. Green and white appear at near-equal weight and most key spells are hybrid {G/W}, so the base is forgiving; Plains count is kept at 4 because only white removal (Get Lost) and the saga demand early white. The nonland curve is 7 one-drops, 9 twos, 7 threes, 7 fours, 6 fives (about 3.0 average), which puts the first web-slinging chain on turns 3-4 and the overrun by turn 6.

Gameplay strategy

Early game (turns 1-3)

  • Turn 1: Aunt May or Llanowar Elves. Aunt May is the preferred engine start (every later Spider gains a counter); the Elf is the preferred sling toll (it has already made mana).
  • Turn 2: Mary Jane Watson first so Spider entries draw, then a second dork or Spider. Twitching Doll taps for any color and leaves a nest counter each time, so it both ramps and pays for itself later.
  • Turn 3: Spider-Man, Web-Slinger or Get Lost. Do not bounce the dork on the turn you need its mana; sling only when the creature being returned has already done its job.

Mid game (turns 4-6)

  • Attack with whatever the board has: attackers come back tapped, which is exactly the condition web-slinging needs.
  • Second main phase: cast Silk, Web Weaver or Spider-UK by returning a tapped attacker. If Silk is already out, every creature spell in the chain makes a Citizen; if Mary Jane is out, the entering Spider draws; Aunt May grows it.
  • Order the cast triggers for value: Mary Jane draws once per turn, so make the first Spider enter before casting the rest of the chain.
  • Spider-Man, Brooklyn Visionary smooths lands on entry; Web-Warriors turns a wide board into a big one in a single cast.

The combo turn

  1. Attack with four or more creatures; keep one dork back untapped if you need its mana for the chain.
  2. In the second main phase, return tapped attackers one at a time: each web-slinging cast is cheaper than the spell’s normal cost, so three casts cost about five mana total.
  3. Each cast makes a Citizen (Silk), draws (Mary Jane, once), gains life and a counter on the entering Spider (Aunt May) and grows a creature (India).
  4. Cast Wall Crawl or Web-Warriors if available, then activate Silk’s {3}{G}{W}: the team gets +2/+2 and vigilance.
  5. Animate Restless Prairie and attack: the Llama adds +1/+1 to everything as it swings. With a half-dozen creatures this is lethal.

Win condition 2: the double-strike Spider

Origin of Spider-Man is a two-mana saga that builds its own finisher. Chapter I makes a 2/1 Spider token, chapter II puts a +1/+1 counter on a creature and turns it into a legendary Spider Hero (which keeps it inside every Spider payoff, including Mary Jane and Aunt May), and chapter III grants double strike. Point it at a Spider that Aunt May and India have been growing, or at a Web-Warriors-pumped 5/5, and the single unblocked hit is 10+ damage. Overprotect adds trample, hexproof and indestructible to make the swing unstoppable.

Mulligan guide

  • Keep: two to four lands with at least one white and one green source, a one- or two-drop engine (Aunt May, Mary Jane, Elf, Web-Slinger), and preferably one Spider to start the loop.
  • Trim: hands with five or more lands, all-toll hands with no actual web-slingers, and single-color hands that cannot cast both an engine and a Spider by turn 3.
  • Restless Prairie and Gathering Place enter tapped or conditionally, so count them as slow mana when judging a two-land hand.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • The returned creature must be tapped as you cast the spell; a creature that attacked this turn qualifies, and so does a dork you tapped for mana. Vigilance is not a problem because the deck grants it only through Silk’s overrun, on the turn you are attacking with it.
  • Web-slinging is an alternative cost, not a reduction: you pay the sling cost plus any additional costs, and you cannot combine it with other alternative costs.
  • Mary Jane Watson triggers once per turn no matter how many Spiders enter, so time the first Spider of your chain before the flood of casts.
  • Silk’s Citizen tokens are Humans, not Spiders, so they do not draw with Mary Jane or grow with Aunt May, but they do feed Wall Crawl, Web-Warriors, Silk’s overrun and Three Tree City only if you choose Spider for the city (it counts Spiders).
  • Aunt May’s counter trigger puts the counter on the Spider that entered; Spider-UK’s end-step draw needs two enters in the turn, which a standard chain exceeds.
  • Get Lost gives the opponent two Map tokens; use it on their engine rather than their small creatures to avoid the ramp.

Matchups

  • Aggro (fliers, lifegain, cat, landfall): Aunt May and Silk outsize their early creatures; Twitching Doll and Spiders-Man chump or block with reach; Get Lost answers their key threat and the deck’s lifegain (Aunt May, Spider-UK) buys the extra turn to overrun.
  • Midrange: win the board race with Web-Warriors and Wall Crawl; the recast chain out-values one-for-one removal because every bounced creature is replayed with a discount.
  • Control: hold Get Lost for their planeswalker, deploy Silk and India together so a single cast turn rebuilds the board after a sweeper, and lean on the Origin double-strike line to close before they stabilize.
