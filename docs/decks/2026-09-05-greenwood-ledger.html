Sultai (Blue / Black / Green) — Elf-legend lore engine from The Hobbit set

Size 60 cards

Overview

Greenwood Ledger is the Elvenking’s library as a deck: every card either is a legendary Elf, puts cards into your graveyard, or rewards you for having a full graveyard and a full hand. The engine is a closed loop. Cantankerous Keepers and the Silvan Rally adventure mill cards — Keepers then puts all Elf cards from the mill into your hand, Silvan Rally puts up to two lands into your hand — and every legendary Elf you cast off that supply makes Thranduil, the Elvenking draw two more cards. The graveyard is not a graveyard here; it is a second hand that Elvish Regrower, Silvan Reveler, Lluwen, and Morcant’s Loyalist keep feeding back into play.

The set identity is the elf half of The Hobbit: Thranduil the Elvenking, Elrond Moon-Reader, the Sindarin Liege, and Galion are the core, and the support slots are Hobbit-set lore pieces (Master’s Councillors, Mirkwood Nurturer) alongside green-blue-black Elf druids and warriors from the wider Standard elf pool.

The set lands on Arena 2026-08-14; the import list works from that date.

Curve and mana

Twenty-four lands: fifteen basics (7 Forest, 5 Island, 3 Swamp) plus nine nonbasics (2 Restless Vinestalk, 1 Rogue’s Passage, 4 Lush Oasis, 2 Jungle Hollow). The mana is Sultai in practice but green-first: the two-drop cluster (Woodland Weavemaster, Lluwen, Master’s Councillors) and the three-drop lords (Elvish Archdruid, Morcant’s Loyalist, Mirkwood Pathmaker, Formidable Speaker) need {G} and {U} on time, while black is a shallow third color carried by 3 Swamps, 2 Jungle Hollows, and the hybrid Lluwen. The curve is engine-shaped: nine two-drops, twelve three-drops, nine four-plus legends, and the flexible affinity on Cantankerous Keepers. Elvish Archdruid and Woodland Weavemaster give the extra mana the five-mana Thranduil and six-mana Keepers want by turn four or five.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Lead with a one- or two-drop: Woodland Weavemaster, Lluwen (mills four immediately), Master’s Councillors, or Wood Elves for the Forest. The goal of the first turns is to fill the graveyard and reach three mana. Silvan Reveler loots lands into play; Lluwen starts the mill the Keepers and Thranduil will exploit. Do not be afraid to discard — this deck’s graveyard is a resource and Elvish Regrower, Silvan Reveler, and Morcant’s Loyalist all reach back into it.

Mid game

Resolve Thranduil, Sindarin Liege for the anthem and start making landfall Elf tokens, then land Cantankerous Keepers. Keepers typically mills four and returns two or three Elf cards to hand, which is a one-card refill. Cast a legendary Elf (Elrond, Galion, a second Sindarin Liege, or Thranduil himself) and Thranduil, the Elvenking draws two more. Every draw triggers Master’s Councillors’ mill — aim it at the opponent once your own graveyard is already fat.

The combo turn

The loop, once Thranduil the Elvenking and a few Elves are on board:

  1. Cast Cantankerous Keepers (cheap from affinity). It mills four and puts every Elf card from the mill into your hand.
  2. Cast the Elves you just found. Each legendary Elf entering makes Thranduil draw two, then discard one; the discard keeps the graveyard stocked.
  3. Elrond, Moon-Reader draws once more off the first creature-ability activation, and Silvan Rally or Lluwen keeps the library turnover going.
  4. Attack: Galion sets a creature’s base power and toughness equal to his own (4/4 plus lords and counters), Mirkwood Pathmaker is as big as your land count, and Rogue’s Passage makes the chosen attacker unblockable. Restless Vinestalk adds a 5/5 trample attacker on the same turn.

Win condition 2

Master’s Councillors mills three cards from target player’s library every time you draw your second card in a turn. This deck routinely draws two or more cards per turn from Thranduil, Elrond, Silvan Reveler, and Keepers — point the mill at the opponent and their library disappears a chunk per turn, while your recursion loop keeps your own side stocked. In a long game the Councillors also become a large vigilance creature: +2/+0 for every graveyard with seven or more cards, and with two self-milling decks in a match there are almost always two such graveyards.

Mulligan guide

Keep any hand with two or three lands and a two-drop (Weavemaster, Lluwen, Wood Elves, Master’s Councillors) or three lands plus a three-drop lord. Keep hands with Thranduil the Elvenking only if you also have fixing for all three colors. Ship one-landers, hands with three top-end cards and no early play, and hands with no green source. Against aggressive decks, prioritize the early bodies (Weavemaster, Wood Elves, Master’s Councillors); against control, keep the mill and recursion pieces and play the long game.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Thranduil, the Elvenking triggers on every other legendary Elf entering — including a second copy of himself, Elrond, Galion, the Sindarin Liege, and Lluwen.
  • Thranduil also gains all activated abilities of Elf cards in your graveyard: a milled Woodland Weavemaster lets Thranduil tap for Elf-cast mana, and a milled Formidable Speaker lets him tutor. Elrond’s draw-on-activation makes the first such activation each turn draw a card.
  • Cantankerous Keepers’ mill resolves before its “put Elf cards into your hand” clause: non-Elf cards milled stay in the graveyard, where Regrower and Silvan Reveler can reach them.
  • Silvan Rally (the Sindarin Liege adventure) mills four and takes up to two land cards — it never fetches lands from the library, only from the milled four.
  • Master’s Councillors’ mill targets any player: mill yourself early to fuel Thranduil and Keepers, switch to the opponent once your graveyard is loaded.
  • Mirkwood Nurturer returns one of your own permanents to hand — re-trigger Wood Elves for another Forest, Silvan Reveler for another loot, or a legendary Elf for another Thranduil draw.
  • Silvan Reveler’s discarded land enters the battlefield tapped: that is a second landfall trigger on the same turn.
  • Elrond’s blink ({5}{U}{U}) can exile a tapped land and return it next end step for a double landfall, or save an Elf from removal.
  • Restless Vinestalk’s attack trigger sets another target creature’s base stats to 3/3 — do not point it at your own Galion target on the turn you want the big number.

Matchups

  • Aggro (fliers, lifegain, cats): Master’s Councillors and Woodland Weavemaster block early, Morcant’s Loyalist and the token Elves chump, and the recursion engine out-grinds. Aim the Councillors’ mill at the opponent once stabilized.
  • Midrange (landfall, counters): the draw-and-mill engine generates more cards than any midrange deck; Galion plus Rogue’s Passage ends through blockers.
  • Control: the deck rebuilds after every sweeper — Keepers refills from the graveyard, and every legendary Elf draws two. Play at instant speed with Elrond’s blink to dodge exile.
  • Combo and big mana: this deck is the value engine — keep milling the opponent with Master’s Councillors while racing their combo with an unblockable Galion.
