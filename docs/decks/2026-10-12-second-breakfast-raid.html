Overview

The Hobbit set hands the burglar cards an engine that is easy to miss: recruit. Great Gilded Boat says “whenever you attack, recruit” — draw a card, discard a card, and if the discarded card is not a land, create a 1/1 white Human Soldier. The card is technically a 4/4 Vehicle for three mana. In practice it is a free card every combat plus a free body roughly two times out of three, because this deck is built with very few lands to discard and a full grip of spells.

The rest of the company exists to make those Soldiers lethal. An Unexpected Party is an enchantment that names a creature type and gives creatures of that type plus two plus two — name Soldier and every recruit token attacks as a 3/3. Dwarven Provisioner pumps the whole team for four mana. The Arkenstone gives every creature plus one plus one and draws a card every end step. Celebrate the Mountain-king recruits again while removing an opponent’s best permanent for as long as it stays on the battlefield.

The Notary Hobbits is the value bomb: five mana buys three bodies, since the entry trigger copies them twice as non-legendary tokens, and each Halfling taps for colorless mana while the company is assembled. Bilbo, Luckwearer and Bilbo Baggins, Burglar keep the hand full — one loots on damage and cannot be blocked, the other is a cantrip — and Bilbo’s loot is exactly the discard that recruit wants.

The alternative finish is the air force. Eagle’s Rescue is an Aura that grants plus two plus two and flying, and it can be re-bought from the graveyard for four mana onto any creature with power 1 or less — which is every token in the deck. Velvetwing Butterflies is a natural flier, and The Lord of the Eagles is an eight-power flash threat whose cost drops by the total power of your creatures with flying, so a board of Soldiers wearing Eagle’s Rescue makes it nearly free.

9 1 The Lord of the Eagles (usually cast for three or four with a flier board)

Twenty-four lands: twenty-two colored sources plus two Soulstone Sanctuary, and Urban Retreat is the only land that always enters tapped. The Verge lands make the colors conditional on basics, so the eight basics are working lands, not filler. The deepest single pip is The Notary Hobbits at two green, which is why the Forest count is three and a green Verge leads most openers. The curve peaks at four, which the deck reaches on curve in roughly two out of three games.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Lead with Bofur, Reliable Guardian or Bilbo, Luckwearer. Bofur blocks profitably with lifelink and its Adventure buys a turn against removal later; Bilbo is unblockable, so it always connects and always loots, which is the discard recruit is looking for. Against a fast opponent, hold the mana for Concerted Care rather than tapping out.

Mid game

Turn three is Great Gilded Boat. The attack trigger is the deck’s engine, so attack every turn even into a worse board: the card and the possible Soldier are worth more than the three damage you give up. Add Lake-town Toymaker on four; the turn it lands is usually a turn you have drawn two or more cards, which turns a 1/1 token into a 4/1 first striker.

The combo turn

Once Great Gilded Boat and Celebrate the Mountain-king are both out, a single attack triggers both: draw a card and discard a card twice, make one or two Soldiers, and exile an opponent’s blocker for as long as the enchantment lives. Sequence it like this: attack with everything, resolve the exile first to clear a blocker, then resolve the recruits to add two attackers after blocks are made. An Unexpected Party plus two to Soldiers plus one Dwarven Provisioner activation on the same turn adds five power to every token.

Win condition two

Eagle’s Rescue is the pivot. Four mana buys plus two plus two and flying on a token, and the graveyard ability re-buys it for another four onto anything with power 1 or less — every token in the deck, and The Lord of the Eagles is reduced by the total power of your fliers, so two enchanted tokens cut its cost by six. The line is: build three or four tokens, land one Eagle’s Rescue, then flash the 8/8 end of turn and attack with everything.

Urban Retreat, three basics, Great Gilded Boat, Bilbo, Luckwearer Keep — the tapped land costs a turn, not the game
Any hand with The Arkenstone and four lands Keep only if it also has a two-drop; the Arkenstone is a payoff, not a plan

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Recruit is a may-effect on the discard, not the draw. If the discarded card is a land, you get the card but no Soldier; the deck runs light on lands it wants to keep, and every loot effect is a chance to bin a spell that the next recruit turns into a body.
  • An Unexpected Party is typed at entry. Choose Soldier for the swarm, Halfling when The Notary Hobbits and both Bilbos are out, and remember the choice is permanent for that enchantment.
  • The Notary Hobbits enters as three permanents. The copies are not legendary, so they stay when a second copy of the original would have to be sacrificed — and each one taps for colorless, which pays for a Dwarven Provisioner activation.
  • The Arkenstone triggers on every end step, including the turn it enters. Its Adventure side, Seek the Heart, finds a legendary creature, and The Notary Hobbits is the best legal target.
  • Eagle’s Rescue from the graveyard has a restriction. The target must have power 1 or less; enchanting a 1/1 token and then pumping it is legal, enchanting a 3/3 Soldier that already got the anthem is not.
  • Soulstone Sanctuary is still a land after it animates. Crew, anthem effects and the recruit tokens all still work, and a sweeper that hits creatures does not kill it.
  • Celebrate the Mountain-king returns the exiled permanent when it leaves. Do not sacrifice it to crew the Boat unless the exile already did its job.

Matchups

Opponent Plan
Aggro Trade Bofur early, keep the lifelink alive with Concerted Care, and race with the Boat’s card advantage. Celebrate the Mountain-king exiling their best attacker is often the whole game
Control Do not over-commit into a sweeper. Hold The Notary Hobbits until after the first board wipe; Soulstone Sanctuary and the fliers rebuild fastest, and An Unexpected Party on a fresh token wave is a one-turn kill
Midrange Value race. The Arkenstone and the Boat both draw every turn, so grind with tokens and keep Eagle’s Rescue for whichever creature can attack through
Combo Pressure early with unblockable Bilbo and the Boat, then point Celebrate the Mountain-king at the combo piece — it exiles the permanent for as long as the enchantment stays. Seek the Heart into The Lord of the Eagles gives a flash clock that does not need a full turn
