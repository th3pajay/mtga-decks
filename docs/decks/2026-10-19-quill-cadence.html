Reality Fracture is Standard-legal but still carries no arena_id in the card data, so the import list below becomes playable on Arena once the set is enabled there. Nothing in this list is castable on Arena today.

Overview

The deck is a burn engine disguised as an aggro deck. Ingris Stingerquill is the whole idea: a three-mana 1/4 flier whose trigger reads “whenever a creature you control attacks, that creature deals 1 damage to each opponent”. That is not a combat-damage payoff, so it does not care about blocks, toughness, or whether the attacker survives. Four attacking one-drops are four damage to each opponent even if all four are blocked.

Everything else exists to put bodies on the board and make them attack the turn they arrive. Samut, Hazoret’s Champion grants the team haste for two mana. Gallia, the Merrymaker is a hasty two-drop who gives haste to every creature carrying a +1/+1 counter and hands a counter to anything that entered this turn. Emergency Phytomedic and Vigorbloom Vanguard enter prepared, so they hand you a free Seed Suture — a +1/+1 counter and a life — the exact counter Gallia and Vanguard’s vigilance static want.

The second engine is the Way cycle. Way of the Warlord and Way of the Pyromancer each empower Jace on entry, which means they arrive as a five-loyalty or two-loyalty Jace token. Warlord gives every planeswalker you control a drain ability; Pyromancer gives them a mana ability. Repurposed Enforcer attacks for empower X equal to your board, Campus Crier empowers from the graveyard, and Arcane Amphisbaena empowers on entry, so the loyalty count keeps climbing without spending cards.

The last piece is a land. Hall of Echoes copies a creature for five mana and ignores the legend rule that turn, so it becomes a second Ingris: five attackers then deal ten damage per opponent per attack step.

Approach

Plan by phases:

  1. Turns 1 and 2: land a prepared body and a haste enabler. Emergency Phytomedic on one is the ideal opener because the free Seed Suture immediately makes your next attacker bigger. Samut or Gallia on two is the follow-up that makes the deck function.
  2. Turn 3: Ingris Stingerquill. From this point every attack step converts bodies into damage regardless of blocks. If Ingris is answered, Repurposed Enforcer plus Way of the Warlord is the pivot to the planeswalker plan.
  3. Turns 4 and 5: stack the board and get paid. Cast Seed Suture copies before combat, put counters on the creatures that will attack, and hold Gallia’s activated ability for anything that entered this turn.
  4. Closing: Hall of Echoes on five mana copying Ingris, or a Way of the Warlord on a fat Jace token to finish through a stalled board.

Curve and mana

Twenty-six of the thirty-six spells cost two, four cost one (Emergency Phytomedic), and six cost three (Ingris and Way of the Warlord). Nothing costs four or more. The land count is 24: ten conditional duals, twelve basics and Hall of Echoes.

Twenty-four lands with twelve unconditional sources is deliberate: the duals carry the four-color manabase, but the deck never wants to miss its third land, because Ingris on three is the turn the deck starts winning. Black comes from seven sources (Stingerquill Annex, Formidable Commons, Swamp) and is only needed for Ingris, so a single black source is enough. Red sits at nine sources (Mountain, Stingerquill Annex, Dedicated Commons) because Samut, Gallia, both Ways and Ingris all want it; white and green also sit at nine, which is why the deck can afford one colorless land.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Lead with Emergency Phytomedic over Vigorbloom Vanguard when a two-drop follows: the free Seed Suture grows the creature that will actually attack, so put the counter there, not on the Phytomedic. Against one-toughness blockers, Arcane Amphisbaena is the better two-drop — deathtouch trades up and it still empowers on entry. Play basics first; a dual only arrives untapped once a Jace token exists.

Mid game and the combo turn

The deck’s “combo turn” is any turn where a haste enabler and three or more bodies are on the board. Order of operations:

  1. Seed Suture copies first, counters on the creatures with the best combat math.
  2. Samut or Gallia, then attack with everything.
  3. Ingris’s trigger resolves per attacker, so even a chump attack with a 1/1 is a point to each opponent.
  4. Post-combat, Repurposed Enforcer’s attack trigger has already empowered Jace for your board size; spend that loyalty on Way of the Warlord’s drain in the following turns.

With Gallia in play, remember her activated ability targets a creature that entered this turn, so a Cadet made by Ingris’s {4} activation can attack immediately and still carry a counter.

Win condition two

Way of the Warlord arrives as a five-loyalty Jace token and gives every planeswalker you control “-4: this planeswalker deals 2 damage to up to one target creature or planeswalker and 2 damage to target player” — two points a turn per planeswalker, through blockers. Way of the Pyromancer pays for those activations with “+1: Add {R}”, and Campus Crier’s graveyard empower tops the loyalty back up. Slower than the Sphinx, but it beats a board stall.

Mulligan guide

Keep hands with two or three lands, at least one unconditional colored source, and a two-drop. Samut or Gallia plus any two creatures is a keep. A hand of only conditional duals with no one- or two-drop is a mulligan even at four lands, because every dual enters tapped and the deck falls a full turn behind. Ingris is not a reason to keep a bad hand.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Prepared is one-shot here. Neither prepared creature re-prepares, so treat Seed Suture as a single free spell per body. Do not hold the creature back hoping for a second copy.
  • Vigorbloom Vanguard’s vigilance static and Gallia’s haste static both key off +1/+1 counters, which makes Seed Suture and Gallia’s activated ability double-duty.
  • Ingris is a 1/4 flier: it blocks well and attacks badly. Its {4} activation is the way it contributes to the attack step, by making a Cadet and hasting the team.
  • Hall of Echoes copies until end of turn and waives the legend rule only for that turn, so the second Ingris disappears at end of turn. Use the copy turn to attack, not to set up.
  • Hall of Echoes produces only colorless mana; it cannot pay for Ingris’s black or red pips.
  • Jace tokens die at zero loyalty. Way of the Warlord’s drain costs four, so do not spend the token below four loyalty on the surveil ability unless the card is worth more than the drain.

Matchups

  • Sweeper control: do not commit four creatures into untapped four mana. Keep Campus Crier in the graveyard and pivot to the Way of the Warlord drain, which needs no board.
  • Spot-removal midrange: expect Ingris to die. Repurposed Enforcer and the Cadet tokens keep the attack triggers relevant, and Hall of Echoes copies whichever creature survived.
  • Faster aggro or combo: Amphisbaena’s deathtouch and Vanguard’s vigilance trade profitably, Seed Suture’s life gain buys a turn, and every attacker is worth a point even when blocked.
