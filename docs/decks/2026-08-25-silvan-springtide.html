Black / Green / Blue / White Elf Legends x Landfall Swarm — The Hobbit set identity deck

Secondary win One-shot unblockable beater: Mirkwood Pathmaker or Thranduil, the Elvenking (who re-uses Elf activated abilities from the graveyard) finished through Rogue’s Passage

Overview

Silvan Springtide is the Elf legends engine of The Hobbit set wired into a landfall chain. The core sentence is on Thranduil, the Elvenking: “Whenever another legendary Elf you control enters, draw two cards, then discard a card” — and the deck plays thirteen legendary Elves, so every legend that resolves refills the hand. His second line, “Thranduil has all activated abilities of all Elf cards in your graveyard,” turns him into a one-creature machine late: tap for X mana like Woodland Weavemaster, make a creature unblockable like Elvenking’s Harper, or pump +3/+3 like Guardian of the Halls — all from the graveyard.

The second engine is landfall. Thranduil, Sindarin Liege makes a 1/1 Elf on every land you control entering; Thranduil’s Company lets you play an extra land each turn and puts two +1/+1 counters down with each landfall; Wood Elves, Elven Passage, and Down in the Valley manufacture extra land drops. Bard, King of Dale then doubles every token created and every draw beyond the first per turn — the Thranduil draw-two becomes draw four, and a three-land turn becomes six Elf tokens.

The deck is a combo deck in the sense that the engines compound: one legendary Elf entering draws cards, those cards are lands and more Elves, those lands trigger landfall twice over, and those tokens get doubled and anthemed into a lethal board.

Approach

Game plan phases

Turns 5-7 The combo turn: Bard, King of Dale doubles tokens and draws; chain legendary Elves and landfall triggers; swing with Galion and stacked anthems
Late game One-shot: Pathmaker or Thranduil goes unblockable via Harper, Key to the Side-Door, or Rogue’s Passage, pumped by Elvenking’s Halls and Bard the Bowman counters

Curve and mana

23 lands: 2 Elvenking’s Halls, 2 Elven Passage, 2 Restless Vinestalk, 1 Rogue’s Passage, 1 Three Tree City, 2 Breeding Pool, 1 Undercity Sewers, 1 Underground Mortuary, 1 Shadowy Backstreet, 1 Meticulous Archive, 1 Uncharted Haven, 4 Forest, 2 Island, 1 Swamp, 1 Plains. Green sources 12, blue 10, black 6, white 6 — black and white are splashes for Thranduil, the Elvenking and the Bard family.

Nonland curve: 1-drop x1, 2-drop x5, 3-drop x14, 4-drop x10, 5-drop x4, 6-drop x3. Average CMC 3.54. The deck wants green and blue early — Weavemaster or Guardian on turn 2, Wood Elves or Elrond on turn 3 — then a legend on turn 4 to start the engine.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Lead with Woodland Weavemaster or Guardian of the Halls on turn 2. Turn 3 is Wood Elves (fetch a Forest — this is also your first landfall trigger), Elrond, or Mirkwood Pathmaker. Thranduil, Sindarin Liege is the best turn-4 play: it is an anthem immediately and every land drop after it makes a token. Play lands in the right order — Elvenking’s Halls and the surveil duals enter tapped, so sequence untapped sources (Breeding Pool, basics, Elven Passage) first when the curve demands it.

Mid game

Resolve Thranduil, the Elvenking and start chaining: every other legendary Elf that enters draws two, and Thranduil’s Company plus Elven Passage plus Wood Elves keep landfall firing. Elven Passage is a subtle engine piece — fetch a basic, behold an Elf to untap it, and you effectively bank a second land drop. Cantankerous Keepers is the setup card: its self-mill puts Weavemaster, Harper, and Guardian into the graveyard, turning Thranduil into a mana dork, an unblockable threat, and a pump machine all at once.

The combo turn

  1. Have Thranduil, the Elvenking and Thranduil, Sindarin Liege on the battlefield; ideally Bard, King of Dale as well.
  2. Chain landfall: play a land, then Elven Passage or Thranduil’s Company’s extra land, then a fetched Forest from Wood Elves. Each land makes a Liege token — doubled to two by Bard — and Thranduil’s Company counters.
  3. Chain legends: each legendary Elf (Liege copies, Elrond, Bard the Bowman, Galion) draws two cards, doubled to four by Bard, King of Dale. The hand refills faster than it drains.
  4. Resolve The Arkenstone (Seek the Heart first to tutor the missing legend), Bard’s Company, and Down in the Valley’s final chapters to stack +1/+1 anthems.
  5. Swing: Galion sets a token’s base power to 4/4 on attack, Bard’s Company and the anthems push everything to 5/5 and up — lethal across a dozen attackers.

A typical kill: five lands down, Liege plus Thranduil plus Bard on board. Two extra land drops make four 1/1s that become four 4/4s by combat under Galion and the anthem stack, while the legend chain keeps the hand full.

Win condition 2

If the swarm is answered, the one-shot wins. Mirkwood Pathmaker’s power equals your land count — with the ramp package that is 8 to 12. Thranduil, the Elvenking is the more explosive version: with Weavemaster in the graveyard he taps for mana equal to his power (5/6 base, plus counters from Elvenking’s Halls, Bard the Bowman, and Thranduil’s Company), with Guardian in the graveyard he pumps himself +3/+3 repeatedly, and with Harper in the graveyard he can’t be blocked. Elvenking’s Halls adds +2/+2 at sorcery speed from the mana base itself, Key to the Side-Door or Rogue’s Passage punch the final creature through, and Bard the Bowman’s lifelink counters keep the race safe. Restless Vinestalk is the spare finisher: a 5/5 trample land that also shrinks a blocker to 3/3 when it attacks.

Mulligan guide

Keep: two to three lands with a two- or three-drop Elf and either a legend or a landfall piece — Weavemaster, Wood Elves, or Elrond openers are all strong. Hands with Liege plus any land-tutor are excellent. Mulligan one-land hands and hands with five or more lands and nothing to do. Black or white sources are required only from turn 4-5 on: a hand with only green and blue is keepable, but a hand that cannot cast Weavemaster, Wood Elves, or Elrond by turn 3 is not.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Thranduil, the Elvenking’s draw triggers only on another legendary Elf entering — he does not draw for himself, and two Thranduils on the battlefield die to the legend rule; play extra copies as discard fodder and Key to the Side-Door fuel.
  • Thranduil inherits activated abilities from Elf cards in the graveyard, not triggered abilities — Bard the Bowman’s counter trigger and Bard’s Company’s recruit do not transfer, but Weavemaster’s tap, Harper’s unblockable, Guardian’s pump, and Elrond’s flicker all do.
  • Woodland Weavemaster’s mana ability references “this creature’s power” — when Thranduil inherits it, X is Thranduil’s power, and the mana is restricted to Elf spells and Elf-source abilities.
  • Bard, King of Dale doubles tokens and draws as replacement effects — Liege landfall tokens and Thranduil’s draw two are both doubled; the first draw of each draw step is exempt.
  • Elrond’s draw-on-activation fires once per turn across all creatures — time it with Weavemaster’s tap or Thranduil’s inherited ability.
  • Down in the Valley’s chapter II grants landfall to the Saga itself — both it and Liege trigger on the same land drop.
  • Wood Elves can fetch Breeding Pool (a Forest), but not Elvenking’s Halls or the surveil duals — fetch basics or the shock depending on what color is missing.
  • Cantankerous Keepers mills four and returns Elf cards among them to hand — non-Elf cards milled (like instants and lands) stay in the graveyard and fuel Thranduil.
  • Elvenking’s Halls’ pump is sorcery-speed and sacrifices the land — count the lands you can afford to lose before the one-shot turn.
  • Three Tree City’s big tap requires {2} plus the land — with six Elves it is six mana of any one color, mostly for Elf spells; do not rely on it for the early Bard, King of Dale.

Matchups

Opponent Plan
Aggro Elrond’s removal-light hand is slow, so lean on Weavemaster chump-blocking, Bard the Bowman lifelink counters, and Quarrel fights; go wide faster than they do
Midrange The anthem stack outclasses their creatures; use Galion to race and Cantankerous Keepers to refill; save Confusticate for their haymaker
Control Seek the Heart and the legend chain out-grind them; Bard, King of Dale doubles every draw; the one-shot win dodges sweepers by killing in a single unblockable hit
Combo Race: prioritize Thranduil, the Elvenking and Bard, King of Dale — the double-draw engine finds the kill first; Confusticate and Bebother buys the turn
