Hammerhone Saga

Mardu (Red / White / Black) — Storied Dwarves x Equipment, built on The Hobbit set identity

Overview

Hammerhone Saga is a two-loop Dwarf deck. The first loop is Storied: the list is loaded with cheap artifacts, legendary Dwarves and Sagas so three or more are on the battlefield by turn three, which switches the whole Dwarf suite on for the rest of the game. The second loop is the hone counter: Dwalin, Weaponmaster puts a hone counter on every Equipment you control whenever it enters or attacks, and each counter is a permanent +1/+0 for whoever carries that Equipment. The rack gets better every turn instead of staying a fixed stat bonus.

The loops feed each other. Kíli the Resourceful and Óin the Brave only cash in once you have an enduring story, and they need artifacts on board to get there, while every Equipment you cast both pays toward Storied and carries a hone counter. Dáin Ironfoot and Iron Hills Blacksmith each mint an Equipment artifact token named Axe on entry, so one creature can push the artifact count over three by itself and simultaneously shave mana off the win-condition land. The black splash is a single card, Crude Bent Blade: an Equipment that makes an opponent sacrifice a creature, and at the same time a Storied artifact, a hone counter target and the removal spell the deck otherwise lacks.

The deck wins wide rather than tall. The Lonely Mountain turns surplus Equipment into 2/2 red Dwarf tokens at a discount, and An Unexpected Party turns those tokens into 4/4 attackers that also carry ward once Thorin Oakenshield is out. When the token plan is answered, Thorin, Mountain-king is the pivot: he hands the whole rack to one Dwarf and turns its power into a removal spell.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Open on whichever two-drop builds the most board. Dáin’s Company is the best turn-two play because it looks at four cards and takes a Dwarf or an Equipment; Iron Hills Blacksmith is the fastest because it leaves an Axe token, which counts toward Storied immediately. Dwalin, Weaponmaster is the play when an Equipment is already down, since every later attack adds a hone counter to the whole rack. Never hold Equipment back to protect a creature that has not arrived: the rack is the engine, and empty Equipment still counts for Storied.

Mid game

Turns three to five are about crossing the Storied threshold and holding it. The magic number is three artifacts, legendaries or Sagas. Two Equipment plus one legendary Dwarf gets there, an Axe token counts, and the Sagas count on their own.

Once the story is enduring, Kíli equips for zero on the first activation each turn and draws off every other Dwarf or Equipment that enters, and Óin attacks with haste. The Lonely Mountain’s token activation drops by one mana per Equipment, so with four Equipment out it is effectively free each turn. Hold Crude Bent Blade for the biggest blocker the opponent has committed rather than firing it early; it is one of only two clean removal effects in the list.

The combo turn

The big swing is Thorin, Mountain-king in the four slot with four or more Equipment on the battlefield:

  1. Cast Thorin, Mountain-king. His trigger attaches any number of Equipment you control to one target creature you control.
  2. Stack the rack on the best carrier. Mattock, Spear, Sting’s hone counters and a couple of Axe tokens routinely make a 10 power Dwarf that also has ward and double strike from another body.
  3. The same trigger makes the equipped creature deal damage equal to its power to up to one target creature, so the carrier doubles as a removal spell. Point it at the best blocker before attackers are declared.
  4. Attack with the carrier, Dáin Ironfoot holding double strike and Thorin Oakenshield’s trample carrying the excess through.

Sequence it after combat if the opponent has instant-speed removal, so the trigger cannot be answered before damage is assigned.

Win condition 2

The second win is attrition by tokens, and it is the plan when the rack is answered. Dáin Ironfoot and Iron Hills Blacksmith make Axe Equipment tokens, Dwarven Shortsword makes a real 2/2 Dwarf, and The Lonely Mountain makes a 2/2 red Dwarf every turn for one or two mana. An Unexpected Party then turns the board into 4/4s by naming Dwarf, and Thorin Oakenshield gives it ward {1}.

Order matters: cast the Adventure face of An Unexpected Party at sorcery speed to make tokens, then cast the enchantment face later from exile once the board is wide. The enchantment is the payoff and the Adventure is the setup, and six mana is not available on one turn, so plan a two-turn sequence.

Mulligan guide

Hand shape Verdict
Two lands plus Dáin’s Company or Iron Hills Blacksmith Keep. Two mana is where the deck lives
One land with Dwalin and an Equipment Mulligan. The deck needs three mana by turn three to keep the engine turning
Two lands with no Dwarf and no Equipment Mulligan. Both loops need a body or a rack piece on turn two
Three lands with Dwalin, Dáin Ironfoot and Crude Bent Blade Keep. Hone engine, artifact count and removal in one hand

Treat a Crude Bent Blade in the opener as a reason to keep rather than a bonus.

Key interactions and edge cases

Interaction Ruling detail
Axe tokens count for everything The Axe from Dáin Ironfoot and Iron Hills Blacksmith is a colorless Equipment artifact token. It counts toward Storied, receives hone counters from Dwalin, reduces The Lonely Mountain’s activation and can be attached by Thorin, Mountain-king. It is not a creature, so it cannot attack or block
Kíli’s free equip and draw The discount applies to the first equip each turn and only while you have an enduring story. The draw fires once per turn, on the first other Dwarf or Equipment that enters, so an Axe made before Kíli lands does not draw
Thorin, Mountain-king needs a target His trigger requires a target creature you control as well as the Equipment. Casting him into an empty board is a 3/4 trampler with no trigger
Ward is not protection from sweepers Thorin Oakenshield’s and Dwarven Mattock’s ward {1} taxes targeted removal only. Neither stops a sweeper, and Sting is at its worst against creature-light control
Stone by Sunlight is modal Destroy a creature with power four or greater, or make one of your own creatures an indestructible artifact. The second mode blanks damage-based removal and adds an artifact toward Storied at instant speed
No evasion in the list The deck attacks on the ground. Trample, double strike and Crude Bent Blade’s sacrifice are the only ways through a stalled board

Matchups

Opponent Plan
Aggro, including fliers and lifegain counters Race with An Unexpected Party and the Axe tokens, but keep Stone by Sunlight and Crude Bent Blade for the biggest threat. Dáin’s Company’s lifegain and Mattock’s +2/+2 and ward {1} are the stabilizers
Midrange The strong matchup. The rack outgrows one-for-one removal, and Thorin, Mountain-king removes the best blocker while attacking
Control The weak matchup. Ward {1} does not stop sweepers and the deck has no card advantage engine beyond Kíli. Commit two bodies at a time and rebuild with land tokens
Combo Crude Bent Blade and Stone by Sunlight are the only interaction. Point the damage trigger at their key creature and race with double strike

Iterations

One tuning round was run against the viability battery (three 500-game simulations per round). No gate failed in either round; round two was taken because the first list passed inside the noise band.
