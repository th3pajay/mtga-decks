Overview

The Hobbit’s Lake-town commons and uncommons are secretly a single engine: recruit. Every recruit spell draws a card, discards a card and - if the discarded card was not a land - leaves a 1/1 white Human Soldier behind. Because recruit happens on your own turn, the recruit draw plus the draw step is already your second card of the turn, which switches on every “whenever you draw your second card each turn” payoff in the set.

Ravenhill Rally is built on that loop and nothing else. Lake-town Lookout, Patient Instructor and Bard’s Company are the recruit sources. Bard the Bowman and Lakeshore Apothecary are the second-draw payoffs that turn each recruit into a permanent +1/+1 counter (Bard the Bowman even hands out lifelink). Ravenhill Flock converts every one of those draws into a flying +1/+1 counter instead. Belladonna Took pays you off for the soldiers themselves - first token gains life, second token draws a card, third token adds a counter to the whole team - so the token stream feeds the draw stream, and the draw stream feeds the counter stream.

The engine has two multipliers. Bard, King of Dale doubles both halves at once: each recruit makes two Soldiers instead of one, and every draw beyond your first each draw step draws two cards instead. Wizard’s Staff makes a single creature’s triggered ability trigger twice, which turns an attacking Bard’s Company into two recruits (two cards, two Soldiers) every combat. One turn with either card out is usually enough to leave a board that cannot be raced.

The green splash is not filler. Radagast of Rhosgobel discounts the first creature spell of each turn by two and gives it flash, so the recruit creatures can be deployed on the opponent’s turn for an instant-speed draw-two, and the four-mana Company costs two. Abandoned Campground, Lush Portico and Hedge Maze provide the green and the fixing without giving up untapped white-blue sources.

Approach

Game plan phases

Phase What happens
Turns 1-2 Lake-town Lookout, Lakeshore Apothecary, Belladonna Took or The Queen of Dale. Land a recruit body early; do not trade the Lookout, its death trigger is a recruit
Turns 2-4 Patient Instructor and Bard the Bowman start the loop. Every recruit is a card, a Soldier and, with a payoff out, a +1/+1 counter
Turns 4-6 Bard’s Company lands (at flash speed with Radagast or any Human) and the team becomes 2/2s and 3/3s. Wizard’s Staff on the Company turns each attack into two recruits
Turn 5-6 Bard, King of Dale doubles tokens and non-first draws. From here the Soldier count grows four at a time: two per recruit, doubled
Combo turn With four or more creatures, kick The Eagles Are Coming! to return the whole team, then rebuild it - every creature that re-enters re-triggers its recruit
Late game Ravenhill Flock, now a 4/4-plus flier from all the draws, and Fountainport Fish close the game in the air

Twenty-four lands plus two surveil lands and fourteen recruit sources is enough to hit the fourth land on time; the deck’s average nonland cost of 2.94 means it is usually double-spelling by turn four. Green is a splash of exactly two cards plus the Portico/Hedge Maze pair, so never keep a hand that needs Forest plus two blue sources.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Open on a one-drop recruit body and hold Patient Instructor until you can use the recruit. The lookahead is simple: a recruit on your turn is card neutral but board positive, and with Bard the Bowman or Lakeshore Apothecary on the battlefield it is also a +1/+1 counter and, for the Bowman, lifelink. Belladonna Took wants tokens, so do not fire The Eagles Are Coming! early just to cycle it.

Mid game and the combo turn

The deck’s explosive turn is The Eagles Are Coming! with kicker. With four creatures, the kicked spell returns all four to hand, and at the beginning of the next upkeep you get four 4/4 white Bird Soldiers with flying. That is sixteen power in the air for one card, and every creature you replay afterwards re-triggers recruit. Sequence it as the last action of your turn after attacking: the returned creatures are safe from sorcery-speed removal, the Birds arrive on the opponent’s upkeep, and the rebuild turn is a fresh wave of draws and Soldiers. Bard, King of Dale turns that rebuild into four Soldiers per Company attack instead of two.

Win condition 2

The ground swarm is the primary kill: Bard’s Company plus Belladonna Took counters plus Bard, King of Dale doubling means a four-Soldier board becomes twenty power in two turns. When the opponent has a stalled ground board, switch to the air - Ravenhill Flock grows on every draw, The Eagles Are Coming! makes 4/4 fliers, and Fountainport adds Fish to the count for Belladonna Took.

Mulligan guide

Keep any hand with two to four lands and at least one one- or two-mana body. Keep hands that have Meticulous Archive or Lush Portico even if the colours look awkward, because they are two colours on one card. Pitch hands that need both a Forest and double blue, hands with no creature, and hands with three or more four-drops and no early play.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Recruit is a draw and a discard: the discard is a cost you can aim. Discard surplus lands to keep the Soldier, or discard a creature when you already have a board, and remember Bard, King of Dale changes the draw to two cards - but you still only discard one.
  • Wizard’s Staff’s trigger doubling applies to triggered abilities of the equipped creature, so the best hosts are Bard’s Company (attack recruit twice), Belladonna Took (each token trigger twice) and Bard the Bowman (each second draw counters twice).
  • Bard, King of Dale’s token doubling applies to every token, including The Eagles Are Coming! Birds (eight 4/4 fliers, not four) and Fountainport Fish.
  • The Eagles Are Coming! returns creatures you own, not just creatures you control, so it can rescue a stolen creature.
  • Magnificent End costs {3} less when it targets a tapped creature, so attack first, then kill the blocker for one mana.
  • Sound the Trumpets only recruits if the countered spell’s mana value was 2 or less, so it is a tempo play, not a hard answer to a five-drop.

Matchups

Opponent Plan
Aggro Wall up with Patient Instructor and Lakeshore Apothecary, trade the Lookout for a recruit, then stabilise with Bard the Bowman lifelink counters and Magnificent End
Midrange Go wide first, keep Bard’s Company for flash, and use The Eagles Are Coming! to blank a sorcery-speed sweeper by returning the team
Control Deploy at flash speed with Radagast, hold Sound the Trumpets for their cheapest game-ending spell, and win with the Bird Soldier alpha strike rather than the ground
Ramp Race. The anthem plus doubled Soldiers is faster than most ramp payoffs; do not trade recruits for value, just attack
