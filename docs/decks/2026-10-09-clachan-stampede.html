Overview

Clachan Stampede fuses three mechanics into one board: Behold supplies the cheap Kithkin engine, Mount supplies the token bodies and the evasive finishers, and Morbid turns every chump block and sacrifice into permanent +1/+1 counters.

The engine runs on creature entry. Kinsbaile Aspirant is a one-mana Kithkin that gains +1/+1 every time another creature enters, and Thoughtweft Lieutenant hands out +1/+1 and trample whenever a Kithkin arrives. Clachan Festival makes two Kithkin tokens on entry and can keep making them for {4}{W}. Behold matters because these Kithkin feed Champion of the Clachan (behold a Kithkin, exile it, and every other Kithkin gets +1/+1) and Celestial Reunion (choose Kithkin, behold two, and tutor any cheap creature straight onto the battlefield).

Mounts convert that wide board into damage. Saddle taps spare bodies, so tokens are never wasted, and every saddled attack creates more material: Bridled Bighorn makes 1/1 Sheep, Seraphic Steed makes 3/3 flying Angels, and Ornery Tumblewagg doubles the counters you have been collecting.

Morbid is the back end. Because the deck trades tokens constantly and Bulwark Ox can sacrifice itself, creatures die almost every turn. That turns on Needletooth Pack (two +1/+1 counters on a target every end step), Cackling Prowler (grows every end step) and Slumbering Cerberus (untaps every end step), so a stalled board still grows.

Approach

Game plan phases

  1. Turns 1 to 3 — establish the engine. Land Kinsbaile Aspirant, District Mascot, Thoughtweft Lieutenant and Seraphic Steed. Trade nothing yet; just build a board that grows for free.
  2. Turns 3 to 5 — go wide and saddle. Clachan Festival and Brigid’s Command flood the board with Kithkin, then tap them to saddle Bridled Bighorn, Congregation Gryff and Seraphic Steed. Every saddled attack adds a token or a counter.
  3. Turns 5 to 7 — convert. Needletooth Pack and Abandoned Air Temple turn the accumulated board into an unrecoverable +1/+1 lead; Congregation Gryff closes through the air.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Open on Kinsbaile Aspirant or District Mascot and keep adding bodies. Do not attack into blocks you lose in the first three turns; the deck wins by compounding, not by racing. Thoughtweft Lieutenant is the best turn-two play when you already have a Kithkin, because the trample it hands out is what makes the later swarm unblockable.

Mid game and the combo turn

The “combo turn” is any turn where you can attack, saddle, and cash in on entry triggers:

  1. Tap two spare bodies to saddle Bridled Bighorn — it makes a Sheep while attacking.
  2. Saddle Seraphic Steed for a 3/3 flying Angel, or Congregation Gryff to pump it by the number of Mounts you control.
  3. Cast Clachan Festival or Brigid’s Command so Aspirant, Lieutenant and Champion of the Clachan all trigger at once.
  4. Spend the leftover mana on Abandoned Air Temple to put a counter on every creature.

Because saddling only taps creatures you are not attacking with, the tokens you make each turn are exactly the fuel the next turn needs.

Win condition two

Congregation Gryff is a flying lifelinker that gets +X/+X where X is the number of Mounts you control. With Seraphic Steed, Bridled Bighorn, District Mascot, Bulwark Ox and Ornery Tumblewagg all on the board it swings for a large evasive chunk and gains the life back. Secret Tunnel removes the last blocker by making two Kithkin unblockable, and Abandoned Air Temple finishes stalled games one counter at a time.

Mulligan guide

  • Keep any hand with a one-drop plus two lands, or with a Temple plus a basic.
  • Keep hands with Clachan Festival and a two-drop: the tokens carry the early game by themselves.
  • Mulligan hands that are all four- and five-drops with one land, and hands with all three colors but no cheap creature.
  • A single red source is not needed early; treat the red cards as late-game removal.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Behold costs are optional or payable. Kinsbaile Aspirant reads “behold a Kithkin or pay {2}”, so you never lose tempo when you have no Kithkin. Champion of the Clachan and Celestial Reunion require the Kithkin, so cast them only when you have a token or a spare Kithkin to exile.
  • Champion of the Clachan exiles a Kithkin and returns it when it leaves. Exiling a token is fine; exiling a real Kithkin gives it back later.
  • Celestial Reunion puts the card onto the battlefield when the beheld creature is the chosen type, so choosing Kithkin and tutoring Seraphic Steed still nets a free body when the cost is paid with two Kithkin.
  • Morbid checks “a creature died this turn” at the end step. Chump-block with a token or sacrifice Bulwark Ox on the second main phase to turn it on every turn.
  • Bulwark Ox’s sacrifice ability gives every creature with a counter hexproof and indestructible, which protects the Ornery Tumblewagg and Needletooth Pack payoffs from a sweeper.

Matchups

  • Against aggro: the tokens are fine blockers and Slumbering Cerberus comes back every turn after a trade. Osseous Exhale kills an attacker for two mana.
  • Against control: do not over-extend into a sweeper. Build with Clachan Festival and Abandoned Air Temple, hold a Kithkin in hand for Champion of the Clachan, and win with the flying Gryff.
  • Against midrange: Molten Exhale plus Brigid’s Command fight a big creature; the token swarm out-sizes most ground boards.
