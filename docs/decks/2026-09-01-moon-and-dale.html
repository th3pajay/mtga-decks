Moon and Dale

Bant-Black (WUBG) Elf Legends — The Hobbit set identity deck

At a glance

Attribute Value
Format Standard (MTGA)
Colors White / Blue / Black / Green (WUBG)
Archetype Legendary-Elf combo-value (avg nonland CMC 3.9)
Core mechanic Legendary Elf ETB draw engine x token doubling x landfall
Primary win Thranduil, the Elvenking draws two per legendary Elf entry, Bard, King of Dale doubles every non-first draw, and the draw-2 payoffs turn cards into counters, lifelink and pump for an unblockable alpha strike
Secondary win Elf token swarm: Thranduil, Sindarin Liege landfall tokens doubled by Bard, King of Dale, growing with Thranduil’s Company and Mirkwood Pathmaker
Land win condition Restless Vinestalk man-land + Rogue’s Passage unblockable

Overview

Moon and Dale is the WUBG elf-legends deck. Every nonland card either is a legendary Elf, makes Elf tokens, cares about drawing a second card, or fixes the four-color mana base. The engine is a feedback loop: legendary Elves enter, Thranduil, the Elvenking draws two cards, Bard, King of Dale doubles that draw, and the “second card drawn” payoffs — Bard the Bowman, Ravenhill Flock, Lakeshore Apothecary, Lake-town Toymaker — convert the cards into counters, lifelink, and combat tricks. Bard, King of Dale doubles tokens as well, so the same engine that draws cards also floods the board.

The deck wins by card advantage instead of a single spell. Opponents who answer one legendary Elf die to the next one; opponents who race lose to the life buffer from Bard the Bowman’s lifelink and the wall of Elf tokens. The set’s identity pieces (Thranduil, Elrond, Galion, the Sindarin Liege) are the core, and the fresh support slots are all Hobbit-set cards.

Curve and mana

Twenty-five lands: twelve basics (5 Forest, 4 Island, 2 Plains, 1 Swamp) and thirteen fixing lands. Black is a splash for Thranduil, the Elvenking (one Swamp plus Multiversal Passage and Tarnation Vista cover it), so the mana plays like a Bant deck with a black pip. The curve peaks at four (Sindarin Liege, Galion, Thranduil’s Company, Ravenhill Flock) with a top end of six (Bard, King of Dale, Boughside Wanderers, Lake-town Mariners, Thranduil’s Decree). Wood Elves and the landfall payoffs smooth the early turns; most games reach six mana on turn six without acceleration.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Play a two-drop: Wood Elves for ramp, Lakeshore Apothecary for the eventual draw triggers, or The Queen of Dale against spell-heavy decks. On turn three, Elrond or Bard the Bowman. The goal is a board and a draw trigger ready for when Thranduil arrives. Do not hold lands back — the deck’s clock is land count (Pathmaker, Sindarin Liege tokens, Thranduil’s Company).

Mid game

Resolve Thranduil, Sindarin Liege, then start playing legendary Elves. Each one draws two cards with Thranduil, the Elvenking on board; with Bard, King of Dale that becomes four cards drawn (discard one). Bard the Bowman puts a counter and lifelink on something every time you hit your second draw; Ravenhill Flock grows every draw. Thranduil’s Company turns the extra land drops into +1/+1 counters with vigilance. Use Thranduil’s Decree to protect the engine or stop a sweeper.

The combo turn

The signature sequence: with Thranduil, the Elvenking and Bard, King of Dale on board, cast any other legendary Elf (Sindarin Liege, Elrond, Galion). Thranduil draws two, Bard doubles it to four. Bard the Bowman pumps a creature with +1/+1 and lifelink; Ravenhill Flock and Lakeshore Apothecary grow. Then attack: Galion sets a creature’s base power and toughness to his own, Rogue’s Passage makes it unblockable, and the life total disappears. Against creature decks, the same draw burst finds Troll Negotiations or Lake-town Mariners’ Gone Fishing to clear the path.

Win condition 2

The Elf token swarm: Sindarin Liege’s landfall makes a 1/1 Elf per land; Bard, King of Dale doubles every token. Thranduil’s Company stacks +1/+1 counters and vigilance; Mirkwood Pathmaker is as big as your land count; Boughside Wanderers pumps +2/+2 per landfall. Restless Vinestalk joins as a 5/5 trample Plant. This plan beats midrange boards by outnumbering and out-sizing them, and beats control by forcing multiple sweepers — Thranduil draws two per legendary Elf after each wipe.

Mulligan guide

Keep any hand with two or three lands and a two-drop, or three lands and a four-drop engine. Hands with Thranduil, the Elvenking but no fixing for black are fine if Multiversal Passage or Tarnation Vista is present. Ship one-lander openers and hands with three top-end six-drops. Against aggressive decks, keep the hand with Wood Elves or the Apothecary rather than the greedier draw cards.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Thranduil, the Elvenking’s draw triggers on every other legendary Elf entering — including a second copy of himself, Elrond, Galion, Sindarin Liege, and The Queen of Dale (a legendary Human Noble, so it also draws).
  • Bard, King of Dale doubles only draws beyond the first card of your draw step, but it doubles every draw from Thranduil, Elrond, Ravenhill triggers, and the adventures. It also doubles all token creation — Sindarin Liege landfall tokens become two Elves.
  • Bard the Bowman triggers on your second draw each turn, so a burst of four cards still counts as one trigger; the counters accumulate from multiple turns and multiple legendary Elves.
  • Elrond’s draw triggers once per turn, on any creature-ability activation — Thranduil’s stolen Elf abilities (e.g. Woodland Weavemaster’s tap-for-mana if it is in the graveyard) count.
  • Silvan Reveler’s discard: if you discard a land, it enters the battlefield tapped — that triggers landfall twice (the discard land and the landfall pay {1}{G}{U} recursion).
  • Gone Fishing (Lake-town Mariners adventure) exiles two of your own permanents and returns them — re-triggering Wood Elves, Sindarin Liege landfall, and Silvan Reveler; it can also dodge a removal spell or bounce a tapped land for a double landfall.
  • Thranduil’s Decree exiles countered permanent spells; you may cast them for free later — steal the opponent’s win condition.
  • Mirkwood Meditator’s base 4/2 replacement only lasts until end of turn; it does not permanently shrink.
  • Restless Vinestalk’s attack trigger sets another creature’s base stats to 3/3 — use it to fix Galion’s pump target or to shrink an opposing-adjacent threat only if it is your creature.

Matchups

  • Aggro (fliers, lifegain, cats, landfall): Bard the Bowman’s lifelink, the 1/1 Elf wall, and Ravenhill Flock as a flying blocker stabilize; Thranduil’s Company gives vigilance to hold back and attack. Mirkwood Meditator blocks as a 4/2.
  • Midrange: out-value with the draw engine; Galion plus Rogue’s Passage ends games through blockers; Troll Negotiations removes the biggest threat.
  • Control: the engine rebuilds after every sweeper because every legendary Elf draws two; Thranduil’s Decree and Mistrise Village’s uncounterable spell fight the counter war; Bard, King of Dale’s doubled draws outpace one-for-one answers.
  • Combo race: draw-engine speed wins the race; Gone Fishing can blink a key piece to dodge removal, and the token army provides instant chumpers.
