The Hobbit set releases on Arena 2026-08-14 — every Hobbit card in this list has an empty arena_id until that date, so the import list works from release day.

Overview

Mirkwood Menagerie is the ninth dedicated daily Hobbit combo deck. Eight earlier builds claimed the set’s headline engines — the storied dwarves, the goblin amass raid, the elvenking’s court, Smaug’s hoard, the wilderland pack, the Lake-town recruits, the dwarf bards and the thieves of the crooked way — so today’s deck takes the wild remnants of the set and makes them cohere: wolves, bears, a rabbit, a bee, Mirkwood elves, an eagle and a border garrison. The unused Hobbit pool is thin (18 cards, all common/uncommon), so the deck is fused around the two mechanics those leftovers share: Ferocious (Rabbit and Warg pay off power 4 or greater) and Landfall (Raft-Steerer and the Mirkwood druids trigger off every land), held together by a +1/+1 counter subtheme and a token-swarm finish.

The engine chain is simple but deep: small creatures (and the tokens they make) draw cards through Enduring Innocence; big creatures (Ordinary Bear, a pumped Guardian of the Halls, a grown Elvish Refueler, Ojer Taq) draw cards through Outcaster Trailblazer; Mirkwood Nurturer bounces a creature or land to re-trigger entries and landfall; and every creature on the board feeds Esgaroth Garrison’s power. Then Ojer Taq, Deepest Foundation triples every token — soldiers, heroes and roles — and Zanarkand’s adventure lands a Hero token with a +1/+1 counter for each land you control.

Approach

Game plan by phase

  1. Develop the burrow (turns 1–3). Nasty Little Rabbit on turn 1; Ravening Warg, Guardian of the Halls or Duskwatch Hunter on turn 2. Small-creature entries trigger Enduring Innocence for cards. The ETB-tapped duals (Foggy Bottom Swamp, Meditation Pools, Kyoshi Village) trigger landfall on the way in.
  2. Turn ferocious on (turns 3–5). Ordinary Bear (4/5) and Guardian of the Halls’ {5}{G}{G} pump are the reliable enablers; Elvish Refueler grows past 4 power with spare mana; every big entry draws via Outcaster Trailblazer. Elven Raft-Steerer taps blockers on landfall, buying time.
  3. Stampede (turns 5+). Esgaroth Garrison’s power equals your creature count; Eagle of the Great Shelf and a solved Case of the Gateway Express anthem the board; Rabbit Response is a +2/+1 finisher with scry 2 (you control a Rabbit). Ojer Taq triples tokens for the final swing, and Banner of Kinship turns the seven wolves into a +7/+7 stampede.

Curve and mana

Four colors from 23 lands: green is the spine (13 sources), white 8, black 5, blue 4 — the hybrid cards (Duskwatch Hunter {2}{B/G}, Mirkwood Nurturer {2}{G/U}) and the triple ETB-tapped duals smooth the early turns, and rainbow fixing is not needed at this curve. The deck is creature-dense (31 creatures) with 18 cards at mana value 2; Bushwhack and Outcaster Trailblazer provide light ramp toward the 5–6 mana finishers. Mulligan aggressively toward green sources and a two-drop.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Turn 1 Nasty Little Rabbit, turn 2 any two-drop. Prefer Guardian of the Halls and Ravening Warg — they win the ground while Duskwatch Hunter’s counter and token-evasion apply pressure. Enduring Innocence on turn 3 turns every subsequent small creature into a card. The duals enter tapped, so sequence basics first on turns 1–2.

Mid game

Establish ferocious: Ordinary Bear or a {5}{G}{G} pump on Guardian of the Halls. From here Outcaster Trailblazer draws on every big entry, and Mirkwood Nurturer can bounce a spent creature (or an ETB-tapped land) to re-trigger entries and landfall. Elven Raft-Steerer taps the biggest blocker each landfall, letting wolves and bears through.

The combo turn

  1. Enduring Innocence + Outcaster Trailblazer convert every creature you cast into a card — the swarm refuels itself.
  2. Mirkwood Nurturer bounces and replays a creature (or a land) for extra counters, draws and landfall taps.
  3. Ojer Taq, Deepest Foundation triples every token: Esgaroth’s recruit Soldier, Castle Guard’s Hero, Royal Treatment’s Role — each token maker now makes three bodies.
  4. Swing with the full board: Esgaroth Garrison is huge from the creature count, Eagle of the Great Shelf anthems per attacker, Case of the Gateway Express is solved (+1/+0), and Rabbit Response gives +2/+1 and scry 2.

Win condition 2 — the giant Hero

Cast Lasting Fayth from Zanarkand, Ancient Metropolis when you control a dozen or more lands: the Hero token enters with that many +1/+1 counters. Royal Treatment and Duskwatch Hunter counters stack on it. The token wins through sheer size — protect it with Royal Treatment and finish over two turns.

Win condition 3 — the Wolf stampede

Banner of Kinship enters naming Wolf with a fellowship counter for each Wolf you control — up to seven (three Ravening Warg, three Duskwatch Hunter, one Desolation Prowler) — turning every Wolf into a 9/9-plus threat. It is a one-card finisher that also works as a giant pump for the wolf half of the board.

Mulligan guide

  • Keep: 2–3 lands with a green source, a two-drop, and ideally a Rabbit or Guardian.
  • Ship: one-landers, five-plus-land hands, and hands with double-white or double-black spells but no matching source.
  • Keep Outcaster Trailblazer or Enduring Innocence hands on a playable curve — the engines beat raw stats.
  • The ETB-tapped duals slow the first two turns: an opening of three tapped duals is a mulligan.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Enduring Innocence triggers only once per turn — time your token bursts (Esgaroth recruit, Castle Guard death) to your own turn.
  • Outcaster Trailblazer draws only on power 4 or greater entries — the ferocious enablers (Ordinary Bear, Guardian pump, Refueler growth) are what turn it on.
  • Banner of Kinship chooses a type at entry — name Wolf; the fellowship counter count is set when it enters, so cast it when your wolf count is highest.
  • Ojer Taq triples tokens, not counters — the +1/+1 counters from Duskwatch Hunter, Troll Negotiations and Refueler are unaffected (a good thing: they stack on tripled bodies).
  • Zanarkand is a land and a spell — cast Lasting Fayth from hand, then play Zanarkand from exile later; the land face enters tapped.
  • Ojer Taq’s death returns it transformed as Temple of Civilization, a land; {2}{W}, {T} transforms it back after attacking with three or more creatures — the win condition loops itself.
  • Liminal Hold exiles until it leaves — it handles any nonland permanent, including enchantments and artifacts.
  • Mirkwood Nurturer can bounce a land — replay it for an extra landfall trigger (Raft-Steerer tap-down).
  • Case of the Gateway Express solves at your end step after three or more creatures attacked — the +1/+0 is permanent once solved.

Matchups

  • Aggro: Ravening Warg deathtouch and Raft-Steerer landfall taps hold the ground; Great Fierce Bee and Velvetwing Butterflies block flyers; Liminal Hold exiles the biggest threat.
  • Midrange: Outcaster Trailblazer’s card advantage out-draws them; Troll Negotiations and Bushwhack fights trade up; Banner of Kinship and Ojer Taq outclass their top end.
  • Control: Enduring Innocence and Trailblazer refill after sweepers; Royal Treatment protects Ojer Taq; Zanarkand’s giant Hero token is a single-card clock they can’t answer cheaply.
