Loose Change Leviathan

Five-color Treasure x Increment x Void combo — crack the coin, feed the counters

Overview

Three mechanics that look unrelated turn out to be one machine, because a Treasure token is a nonland permanent that leaves the battlefield, and the mana it produces is still mana spent.

  • Void turns on whenever “a nonland permanent left the battlefield this turn.” Cracking a single Treasure satisfies it. That makes every Void card in the deck live from turn two onward instead of depending on a sacrifice theme.
  • Increment triggers whenever you cast a spell and the mana you spent is greater than the creature’s power or toughness. A 0/2 like Pensive Professor increments off any spell — including one paid for entirely with Treasure. Mana source is irrelevant; only the amount matters.
  • Treasure is therefore both the Void switch and the Increment fuel, and it fixes five colors on a basic-land manabase.

The result is a deck that spends its early turns making and cracking coin, its middle turns drawing two cards a turn off Pensive Professor, and its final turns swinging with a Fractal the size of a house that cannot be blocked.

Two cards carry the counters engine. Berta, Wise Extrapolator is a 1/4, so any two-mana spell increments her, and every counter she receives adds one mana of any color — she is the deck’s five-color glue. Pensive Professor at 0/2 draws a card every single time she increments, which is every spell.

Void supplies the alternate clock. Alpharael, Stonechosen makes a defending player lose half their life, rounded up, on attack while Void is live — two attacks and the game is over regardless of blockers. Elegy Acolyte turns the same condition into a Robot every end step and draws on combat damage.

Approach

Game plan in three phases

  1. Build the coin engine (turns 1-3). Land Ambitious Augmenter, Magda, the Hoardmaster, Discerning Financier, or Goldvein Hydra on two. Discerning Financier is the most reliable Treasure in the deck because an opponent almost always has more lands than a five-color deck on turn three. Seize the Spoils and Redcap Thief keep the coin flowing while digging for the counter creatures.
  2. Turn coin into cards and counters (turns 3-5). Resolve Pensive Professor, Berta, Wise Extrapolator, and Topiary Lecturer. From here every spell is a cantrip off the Professor, and every Treasure cracked is a Void trigger. Hold mana up: cracking Treasure is an instant-speed action, so you can make Void live during your own combat step.
  3. Convert (turns 5+). Grow a Fractal with Berta, or land Fractal Tender for a free 3/3 Fractal every end step, then clear a path with Rogue's Passage. If the board stalls, attack with Alpharael instead — half of a life total does not care about blockers.

Basics (5 Mountain, 5 Forest, 4 Island, 4 Swamp, 2 Plains) 20 The five-color base, deliberately basic-heavy: Treasure and Berta do the fixing

Pip count across the deck is 15 blue, 13 green, 12 black, 7 red, 4 white. Blue is the heaviest because Pensive Professor wants double blue, but every non-basic source in the deck is colorless, so the manabase leans on Treasure for the second blue and for black’s double pips.

Curve and mana

Eighteen of the thirty-six nonland cards cost three, which is exactly what Increment wants: three mana beats the power or toughness of a 1/1, a 2/2, a 0/2, and a 0/3, so the entire counter suite grows off a normal curve. The deck does not need to reach five mana to function — five-drops are three cards, all of them finishers.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Lead with lands and a two-drop. Magda, the Hoardmaster and Discerning Financier are the best turn-two plays because they generate coin without asking anything of you. Do not crack Treasure just because you can — hold it until either you need the mana for a spell that increments something, or you need Void live in the same turn you attack with Alpharael or end the turn with Elegy Acolyte.

Mid game

The priority order is Pensive Professor, then Berta, then everything else. A resolved Professor turns Seize the Spoils into draw three and turns every future spell into a cantrip; it is the card that wins long games. Berta is the answer to color screw: she taps for one mana of any color per counter, and since she increments on any two-mana spell she usually offers two or three colors by turn six.

Topiary Lecturer is the quiet ramp piece. It is a 1/2, so it increments off three-mana spells, and it taps for green equal to its power — a Lecturer that has grown twice is a three-mana land that also attacks.

The combo turn

The deck’s “combo” is a loop rather than a single turn:

  1. Have Pensive Professor or Berta and at least two Treasures.
  2. Cast a three-mana spell using Treasure mana. Increment triggers, Professor draws you a card, and two Treasures have left the battlefield, so Void is live.
  3. At end of turn, Elegy Acolyte makes a Robot and Insatiable Skittermaw grows. If you have Fractal Tender out and incremented it, you also get a 3/3 Fractal.
  4. Next turn the board is wider, the mana is bigger, and Berta can dump X counters into a Fractal.

Win condition 2

Alpharael, Stonechosen is a 3/3 with ward, and its Void attack trigger makes the defending player lose half their life, rounded up. Twenty life becomes ten, then five, then three. Because it is life loss and not damage, it ignores blockers, fog effects, and indestructible creatures — the only answer is removing Alpharael or keeping Void offline, and keeping Void offline means never cracking a Treasure, which the deck simply does not do. Cracking a Treasure during your own combat step is the cleanest way to turn the trigger on after blockers are declared.

Mulligan guide

  • Keep any hand with two lands, a two-drop, and a three-drop.
  • Keep one-land hands only with Ambitious Augmenter or Discerning Financier and a second land on top.
  • Ship hands with no blue and no Treasure: the deck cannot function without a Professor or Berta.
  • Ship five-land hands with no engine. The deck draws well once it has a Professor, but it has no way to convert raw land into action on its own.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Void checks the whole turn, not the stack. A Treasure cracked in your first main phase keeps Void live through combat and the end step, so one coin can pay for a spell, enable Alpharael’s attack trigger, and turn on Elegy Acolyte in the same turn.
  • Increment counts mana spent, not mana in the cost. Treasure mana, Berta’s any-color mana, and Topiary Lecturer’s green all count. Casting a spell for free does not.
  • Pensive Professor’s threshold is zero. At 0 power, any spell with at least one mana spent increments her. She never stops drawing.
  • Berta’s counter trigger is not optional and not once a turn. Two spells that both increment her in one turn means two mana of any color.
  • Ambitious Augmenter wants to die with counters on it. Attack into a bad block on purpose late in the game: the Fractal arrives with the Augmenter’s counters already on it, which is often a straight upgrade.
  • Goldvein Hydra is the deck’s best topdeck. Cast it for four or five, it increments the whole team, and when it dies it refunds the mana as Treasures, which then turns Void on for the rest of that turn.
  • Magda needs a crime. Temporal Intervention targeting an opponent is a crime; so is any removal pointed at their board. The three-Treasure Scorpion Dragon is a real clock against decks that wall the ground.

Matchups

Opponent Plan
Aggro Lean on Elegy Acolyte lifelink and Robot chump blockers; race with Alpharael’s half-life trigger, which does not need a profitable attack
Control Do not overextend into sweepers. Keep one Professor plus Treasures, rebuild after the wipe, and win with Rogue’s Passage on a single large Fractal
Midrange grinders Out-card them with Professor plus Fountainport; Fractal Tender wins any board stall where both sides have creatures
Combo Race. Alpharael halves life faster than most combos assemble, and Temporal Intervention for two mana with Void is the deck’s only real disruption
Ramp Their lands trigger Discerning Financier every upkeep, so you get free coin all game; convert it into counters before they land a haymaker
