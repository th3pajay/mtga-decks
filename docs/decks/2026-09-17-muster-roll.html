Overview

Muster Roll is the recruit build of The Hobbit set. Recruit is a keyword action written on a large chunk of the set’s cheap creatures: draw a card, then discard a card; if the card you discarded was not a land, you also get a 1/1 white Human Soldier. Twenty-one of the deck’s thirty-seven nonlands can recruit, and eight separate cards carry the ability, so the deck’s clock and its card flow come from the same action.

That is what the rest of the list is built to convert. Every recruit is a draw, so Ravenhill Flock gets a +1/+1 counter and Lakeshore Apothecary gets a +1/+1 counter on the second draw of the turn — a single recruit with an Apothecary on the board is a 2/3 for {1}{U} that keeps growing. Lake-town Toymaker checks for two cards drawn this turn at the beginning of combat and pays that off with +3/+0 and first strike on an attacker, which is how a 1/1 Soldier token or a Bird token becomes a real threat. Esgaroth Garrison turns the whole board into one number: its power is the number of creatures you control, so every Soldier token made by recruit makes the Garrison itself bigger.

The black half of the deck is a second, independent damage source. Gollum, Riddle Master is a 3/1 for {1}{B} that locks in a mana-value parity as it enters and then pays you a +1/+1 counter, a 2-life drain, or a card every time the opponent casts a spell of that parity — once per mode. Gollum the Abandoned is a 2/2 for {1}{B} that drains 2 on entry and can be bought back for {2} by sacrificing an artifact or a creature, which is exactly what a board full of expendable Soldier tokens is for. Two Gollums, a few 1/1 Soldiers and a spare Great Gilded Boat are a slow but very hard to stop drain.

The deck is not a two-card combo; it is a loop. Recruit makes tokens, tokens pay for Gollum, Gollum drains, the drains and the fliers finish what the swarm starts, and Rogue’s Passage makes the largest body unblockable when the ground stalls.

Approach

Game plan phases

Phase Turns What the deck is doing
Open 1 to 2 Lake-town Lookout on one, then The Queen of Dale, Gollum, Riddle Master or Lakeshore Apothecary on two. The Queen of Dale turns the opponent’s first noncreature spell every turn into a recruit, so turn two is when the engine switches on even if you never attack
Build 3 to 4 Patient Instructor arrives with vigilance and recruits on entry, Great Fierce Bee adds an evasive body that scries on every trade, and Sound the Trumpets counts a cheap spell while making another token. Great Gilded Boat and Celebrate the Mountain-king come down as the first non-creature value pieces
Engine 4 to 5 Lake-town Toymaker, Ravenhill Flock and Long Lake Nuisance arrive. From here each turn normally includes two draws (a recruit plus a Flock trigger or a Gollum draw), which turns on the Toymaker every combat and keeps the Flock growing
Close 5 plus Esgaroth Garrison lands as a large attacker that also recruits, Rogue’s Passage makes it unblockable, and the Gollum loop plus the fliers close from the air if the ground is blocked

Lands as engines

Land Count Why it is in the deck
Rogue’s Passage 1 The win-condition land. {4}, {T} makes a target creature unblockable for the turn, which is how a 6-power Esgaroth Garrison or a counter-loaded Ravenhill Flock gets through a stalled board
Temple of Enlightenment 3 White-blue dual that enters tapped and scries 1. The scry is real selection in a deck that discards to recruit every turn
Temple of Deceit 3 Blue-black dual, enters tapped, scries 1. It is the only land that produces black and blue together
Plains, Island, Swamp 6 / 5 / 5 Basics. White carries the recruit half, blue carries the draw payoffs and the Vehicle, black carries the Gollum pair and Great Fierce Bee

Coloured sources: nine white, eleven blue and eight black, before the two hybrid casting costs on Patient Instructor, which can be paid with either white or blue. Twenty-three lands is deliberate: the deck wants four mana on time for Lake-town Toymaker and Ravenhill Flock and then stops, because excess mana goes into Rogue’s Passage activations and the Gollum loop.

Curve and mana

Mana value Count Cards
One 4 Lake-town Lookout
Two 12 The Queen of Dale, Gollum, Riddle Master, Gollum the Abandoned, Lakeshore Apothecary, The Eagles Are Coming!
Three 10 Patient Instructor, Great Fierce Bee, Great Gilded Boat, Sound the Trumpets
Four 8 Lake-town Toymaker, Ravenhill Flock, Long Lake Nuisance, Celebrate the Mountain-king
Five 3 Esgaroth Garrison, Magnificent End

The deck curves out at five and lives at two and three. Twenty-two of the thirty-seven nonlands cost three or less, and the two five-mana cards are both effective at less: Magnificent End usually costs {W} because its target is a creature that attacked, and Esgaroth Garrison recruits on entry so it never enters empty-handed.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Turns one and two are about turning on the engine. Lake-town Lookout is the best one-drop because it recruits on death, so it is a safe blocker that replaces itself. The Queen of Dale is the strongest turn-two play: from then on, every first noncreature spell the opponent casts gives you a card and, on a nonland discard, a Soldier token. Gollum, Riddle Master on turn two is the other option, and against a deck full of two-mana spells you name even — zero counts as even, so the parity covers the most common cheap spells and most double-faced cards.

Lakeshore Apothecary wants to be played before the first recruit, not after: its counter triggers on the second draw each turn, and a recruit is frequently that second draw.

Mid game

Patient Instructor on three is the deck’s best value play: a vigilant 2/2 that recruits as it enters, which is a card and often a token. Sound the Trumpets is the tempo card — counter a two-mana spell and you get the recruit, so it is a counterspell that replaces itself.

Draw two or more cards in a turn whenever you can. Two full draws turn on Lake-town Toymaker’s combat pump, and the deck has redundant ways to get there: a recruit, a Gollum draw mode, a Flock trigger, or simply your draw step plus any cantrip. Hold The Eagles Are Coming! until it returns an ETB creature — Patient Instructor and Long Lake Nuisance both recruit again when you replay them, so the card is a 4/4 flier plus a second recruit trigger.

The combo turn

The engine turn needs The Queen of Dale or any two recruit sources, a second-draw payoff, and at least three creatures.

  1. Play the turn’s first creature with recruit — Patient Instructor, Long Lake Nuisance or Celebrate the Mountain-king. Draw a card, discard a nonland, make a 1/1 white Human Soldier.
  2. Attack with everything, including Great Gilded Boat if it is crewed. The Boat recruits on attack whether or not it is crewed, so attacking with anything at all gives you the second recruit and, with it, the second draw.
  3. That second draw triggers Ravenhill Flock’s +1/+1 counter and Lakeshore Apothecary’s counter, and turns on Lake-town Toymaker for +3/+0 and first strike. Put the Toymaker trigger on the largest flier or on Esgaroth Garrison.
  4. Before the end of the turn, if a creature would die anyway, sacrifice it to Gollum the Abandoned to return him to hand, then replay him for another 2 life from each opponent.

A typical turn four: Queen of Dale and Patient Instructor in play, a second-draw payoff, two recruit triggers, four or more tokens, and a Garrison coming down as a 7/5 — with Rogue’s Passage one activation away from being unblockable.

Win condition 2

The drain and flier plan does not need Esgaroth Garrison. Gollum, Riddle Master offers three separate one-time payoffs and the drain mode plus Gollum the Abandoned’s entry trigger is 4 life a turn, doubled by a return and replay. Support that with the air force: Great Fierce Bee, Long Lake Nuisance, Ravenhill Flock, and the 4/4 Bird Soldiers from The Eagles Are Coming!.

Against a deck that answers the board, stop committing to the ground. Keep one creature holding Rogue’s Passage duty, keep draining with Gollum, and hold The Eagles Are Coming! for the turn after a sweeper, when returning two or three creatures that already recruited gives you a fresh board of fliers.

Mulligan guide

Hand shape Verdict
Two lands, The Queen of Dale, one other two-drop Keep. This is the ideal opening — the engine starts on turn two without needing to attack
Two lands, Lake-town Lookout, any three-drop Keep. A one-drop that recruits on death plus a three-mana value creature is enough to start drawing
Two lands, Patient Instructor and Great Gilded Boat, no black source Keep. Two ways to recruit and two ways to draw is the engine, and black is only needed for the Gollum pair
One land, no one-drop Mulligan. The deck needs three mana on turn three to keep the recruit sources coming
One land, Lake-town Lookout and Lakeshore Apothecary Borderline keep. Play the Lookout first and use the Apothecary as the follow-up, but this hand goes wrong if it misses the second land
Five lands, two four-drops Mulligan. Excess lands are the worst cards in a deck that discards every turn anyway
Two lands, Gollum, Riddle Master and Gollum the Abandoned Keep. It is not the fast draw, but the drain pair wins slower games on its own

Key interactions and edge cases

Interaction Ruling detail
Recruit is one trigger with two outcomes Draw, then discard, then if the discarded card was a nonland, create the Soldier. Discarding a land is not a failure — with 23 lands, recruit is also how the deck filters floods, and Lakeshore Apothecary and Ravenhill Flock count the draw either way
The Queen of Dale triggers once per turn Only the opponent’s first noncreature spell each turn recruits. Extra noncreature spells later in the same turn do nothing, so do not hold the Queen expecting a second trigger
Gollum, Riddle Master’s parity is locked in Odd or even is chosen as he enters and never changes, zero counts as even, and each of the three outcomes can be chosen only once. Name even against decks built on two-mana interaction, odd against decks built on one- and three-mana spells
Gollum the Abandoned’s recursion is sorcery speed The {2} plus a sacrifice can only be activated on your turn, and it returns him to hand rather than the battlefield, so he has to be replayed. Sacrificing a Soldier token or a spent Great Gilded Boat costs nothing the deck misses
Lake-town Toymaker checks before combat The two-card condition is checked at the beginning of combat. Trigger a recruit in your first main phase, or hold Sound the Trumpets, rather than relying on the draw step alone
Great Gilded Boat recruits on any attack The trigger is on attacking, not on the Boat attacking, and it does not require the Boat to be crewed. The 4/4 body is a bonus; the card and the token are the point, and the Boat is also the cheapest sacrifice for Gollum’s recursion once it has done its job
Esgaroth Garrison counts itself Its power is a characteristic-defining ability equal to the number of creatures you control, so it enters as at least a 2/5 even on an empty board and grows with every Soldier token
Magnificent End’s discount It costs {3} less only if the target is already tapped when you cast it, which makes it a one-mana answer to anything that attacked you and a five-mana answer to a blocker
Sound the Trumpets recruits conditionally The recruit only happens if the countered spell had mana value 2 or less. It is a tempo play against cheap interaction, not a hard counter for a five-drop
The Eagles Are Coming! timing The Bird tokens are created at the beginning of the next upkeep, so they attack on your following turn. Returning a creature is a real cost, which is why it should return ETB creatures such as Patient Instructor or Long Lake Nuisance that recruit again on replay
Rogue’s Passage has no timing restriction {4}, {T} can be activated in the declare-blockers step, after the opponent has committed blockers, and the {4} can be paid with any colour

Matchups

Opponent Plan
Aggro, including fliers and lifegain Race in the air and use Magnificent End as a one-mana answer to whatever attacked. Block with Lake-town Lookout, which recruits on death, and keep the Queen of Dale back as a blocker rather than attacking into a bigger board. The Gollum drain is the way to win a race that stays close
Midrange The strong matchup. The deck draws two or more cards on most turns, so it refills faster than one-for-one midrange decks, while Esgaroth Garrison grows past any single blocker and Rogue’s Passage ignores the rest. Return and replay Gollum the Abandoned every turn to keep draining
Control The tokens are the sweeper’s target, so keep a recruit source in hand and rebuild after the wipe with The Eagles Are Coming! and the fliers. Sound the Trumpets answers the sweeper if it is cast for two or less; otherwise hold the Boat and the Queen of Dale back as the post-wipe engine
Combo The clock is turn five or six, so every flier matters. Magnificent End kills the first combo creature, Sound the Trumpets counters the cheap enabler, and Celebrate the Mountain-king exiles a permanent outright. Do not over-invest in tokens against a deck that ignores the board
