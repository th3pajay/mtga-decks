Overview

Haggle the Horizon is a Temur tempo-combo deck that fuses three mechanics around a single resource: the Map token.

  • Explore is the engine. Cenote Scout, River Herald Guide and Jadelight Spelunker explore on entry, Sentinel of the Nameless City explores through Map tokens, and Over the Edge explores a creature twice. Every explore either puts a land into hand (fixing and ramping) or puts a +1/+1 counter on the explorer.
  • Bargain is the value conversion. A Map token is an artifact, which makes every Map a free sacrifice for Torch the Tower and Brave the Wilds. The same token can instead be spent on its own ability to make a creature explore — so each Map is a choice between a bigger spell now and a counter or land later.
  • Exhaust is the payoff. Mindspring Merfolk exhausts to draw cards and put a +1/+1 counter on every Merfolk, Sita Varma, Masked Racer exhausts to set the whole team’s power to hers, and Loot, the Pathfinder offers three exhaust abilities (mana, cards, damage) on a double-striking body. Boommobile and Boom Scholar exist to make those exhausts cheap and repeatable: the Vehicle adds four mana that can only be spent on abilities, and the Goblin discounts every other exhaust ability by two.

The fusion is what makes it work. Explore produces the tokens that Bargain eats, Bargain spells protect the board that Exhaust then converts into a lethal swing, and the exhaust enablers (a Vehicle and an Advisor) also give the deck its mid-game body. Because the deck is full of Merfolk, Mindspring Merfolk is a team pump as well as a draw spell, and Nicanzil, Current Conductor turns every land off an explore into a free land drop and every nonland into a counter.

The manabase is a weapon, not just fixing. Restless Ridgeline attacks as a 3/4 Dinosaur and untaps another attacker, Restless Spire attacks as a first-striking 2/1, Bitter Work Earthbends a land into a hasty 4/4 that keeps drawing cards, and Brave the Wilds turns a land into a hasty 3/3 Elemental while fetching the next one.

Approach

Game plan in three phases

  1. Turns 1–3, build the board and the tokens. Deploy one-drops on curve — Cenote Scout, Spyglass Siren, Mindspring Merfolk, Jadelight Spelunker. Siren and Sentinel hand you Map tokens; hold them as Bargain fodder or crack them to push an explorer into a land. Nicanzil, Current Conductor converts every land-card explore into a free land drop.
  2. Turns 3–5, convert. Use Torch the Tower and Over the Edge to clear blockers, using a Map as the Bargain sacrifice for Torch’s scry-and-exile mode. Bitter Work starts drawing a card a turn as soon as any 4-power creature attacks, and Boommobile arrives as a 5/5 crew 2 that also banks four ability-only mana.
  3. Turns 5+, close. Spend the banked mana on exhaust abilities. Mindspring Merfolk for X (draw and counter every Merfolk), then Sita Varma, Masked Racer for X to make the whole team’s power equal to hers. Attack with everything, including whichever lands animate. Loot, the Pathfinder is the top-end that keeps the engine running: double strike and haste, plus exhaust draw three and exhaust three damage.

Curve and mana

23 lands: 4 manlands (2 Restless Ridgeline, 2 Restless Spire), 6 fetch lands (3 Terramorphic Expanse, 2 Evolving Wilds, 1 Fabled Passage), 2 Fountainport, and 11 basics (4 Forest, 4 Island, 3 Mountain). The curve is deliberately low: 18 one-drops and 7 two-drops mean the deck rarely stumbles on colour, and the fetch lands let you pick the colour you are missing. Green is the primary colour (Merfolk, ramp, Brave the Wilds), blue pays for the exhaust payoffs and Over the Edge, red covers Torch the Tower and Boommobile.

Gameplay strategy

Early game (turns 1–3)

  • Turn 1: Cenote Scout is the best play — it explores immediately and becomes a 2/2 or hands you a land. Spyglass Siren is the alternative when you want a flying body and a Map token to hold.
  • Turn 2: Nicanzil, Current Conductor or a second one-drop plus a held-up Torch the Tower with a Map ready to bargain. If your hand has Jadelight Spelunker, cast it for X = 2 or 3 once you have lands in hand, so each explore has a real chance of finding a land.
  • Do not crack Maps early by reflex. A Map is an artifact first: it turns Torch the Tower into three damage plus scry, and Brave the Wilds into a 3/3 hasty Elemental. Crack it only when you need the explore trigger or a colour.

Mid game (turns 4–6)

  • Sentinel of the Nameless City is the best mid-game body: vigilance means it blocks and still triggers an explore on the attack, and each trigger makes another Map.
  • Crew Boommobile and attack — its four ability-only mana is worthless until you have an exhaust to spend it on, so treat it as a mana battery that also blocks.
  • Bitter Work converts a wide board into cards: any attack with a 4-power creature draws. That is usually Sentinel with a counter, an enlarged explorer, or an animated land.

The combo turn

  1. Build to five or more lands with at least two Merfolk on board (Cenote Scout, Sentinel of the Nameless City, River Herald Guide, Jadelight Spelunker, Mindspring Merfolk and Nicanzil all count).
  2. Cast or crew Boommobile for four mana that can only be spent activating abilities, and land Boom Scholar if you have it to discount every other exhaust by two.
  3. Mindspring Merfolk exhaust, {X}{U}{U}: draw X cards and put a +1/+1 counter on each Merfolk you control. This is both the refill and the setup.
  4. Sita Varma, Masked Racer exhaust, {X}{G}{G}{U}: put X +1/+1 counters on her, then the base power and toughness of every other creature you control becomes equal to her power until end of turn. With three counters she is a 5/6 and the rest of the team swings as 5/5s.
  5. Attack with everything. Restless Ridgeline joins as a 3/4 and untaps your best other attacker, Restless Spire as a first-striking 2/1.

Win condition 2: the animated land

The manabase closes games on its own. Bitter Work’s exhaust is {4} for Earthbend 4 — a land you control becomes a 0/0 creature with haste that is still a land, with four +1/+1 counters, and it returns to the battlefield tapped if it dies. That 4/4 haste attacker also satisfies Bitter Work’s own trigger, so attacking with it draws a card. Brave the Wilds in Bargain mode does the same job a turn earlier at one mana for a 3/3 hasty Elemental, and both Restless Ridgeline and Restless Spire attack as creatures without any help at all. Against a deck that has stabilised behind ground blockers, this is the plan that gets there.

Mulligan guide

  • Keep: two or three lands in the right colours, at least one one-drop explorer, and either a Map-maker (Spyglass Siren, Sentinel of the Nameless City) or a removal spell with a bargain outlet.
  • Keep aggressive: Cenote Scout plus Nicanzil, Current Conductor plus lands is a real hand; the deck wins off a wide, pumped board rather than a single haymaker.
  • Trim: hands with no turn-one or turn-two play; hands where all three colours are required before turn three; five-land hands with only Boommobile and Loot, the Pathfinder as action.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • A Map token is both a Bargain cost and an explore. Bargain is paid as you cast the spell, before it resolves; the Map’s own explore ability costs {1} and a tap and can only be activated as a sorcery. Decide which one you need before you commit.
  • Nicanzil triggers off the explore, not the explorer. It sees any creature you control exploring, so a Map cracked on your best attacker still adds a land or a counter.
  • Jadelight Spelunker’s X is locked in on cast. It explores X times on entry; if a land shows up, it goes to hand and the next explore can find another.
  • Boommobile’s mana and Boom Scholar’s discount only pay for abilities. They cannot cast spells, so plan the exhaust turn rather than the spell turn.
  • Sita Varma sets base power and toughness. Counters and other bonuses on your other creatures are overwritten for the turn; put counters on Sita Varma instead, and attack with the Merfolk whose counters you cashed in on the previous turn.
  • Exhaust abilities can only be activated once each. With no repeat-enabler in the deck, spend each exhaust on the turn it actually wins or stabilises.
  • Torch the Tower exiles the permanent it kills. Do not expect to remove something and leave it in a graveyard.

Matchups

  • Aggro: the deck’s early bodies are small, so block with Sentinel of the Nameless City (3/4 vigilance) and trade up with Torch the Tower. Boommobile is a 5/5 blocker for four mana. Race only when you already have a pump in hand.
  • Control: hold Map tokens to force Torch the Tower and Brave the Wilds through as two-for-ones, and use manlands and Bitter Work Earthbend so your threats are lands that sweepers miss. Loot, the Pathfinder’s exhaust draw three replaces a wiped board.
  • Midrange: your counters accumulate faster than theirs because explore triggers are free. Keep Over the Edge for an artifact or enchantment that matters and win the long game with Bitter Work card draw.
  • Combo: you rarely have interaction beyond Torch the Tower and Over the Edge, so apply pressure early and hold Boommobile mana to threaten an exhaust that ends the game first.
