Overview

Gilded Horde fuses the two black-red engines of The Hobbit set: the Amass Goblins counter machine and Smaug’s Treasure economy. The core is the same one the Goblin-town raiders rely on — The Great Goblin, who deals 2 damage to the opponent every time you put one or more counters on a Goblin, Orc, or Army you control. Since an amassed Army is also a Goblin, every amass spell in the deck is a burn spell that also grows a creature.

The Smaug half converts that pressure into gold. Orcrist, Goblin-cleaver turns combat damage into a Treasure per Goblin you control; Smaug the Magnificent mints a Treasure every upkeep and deals its damage on attack equal to your Treasure count; The Misty Mountains Cold banks four Treasures and pays out a 6/6 Dragon. Crime Novelist spends those Treasures on +1/+1 counters — which, of course, re-trigger The Great Goblin. The result is a deck where the burn plan and the gold plan feed each other: amass to grow, grow to swing, swing to mint, mint to ping.

Curve and mana

21 lands: 4 Goblin-town, 3 Mirkwood, 2 Jagged Barrens, 1 each of Restless Vents, Razortrap Gorge, Strangled Cemetery, Wastewood Verge, Thornspire Verge, Cavern of Souls, Rogue’s Passage, plus 2 Mountain, 2 Swamp, 1 Forest. Red sources 11, black 15, green 7 — green is a light splash for Warg Tactics only.

Nonland curve: 1-drop x2, 2-drop x19, 3-drop x14, 4-drop x3, 5-drop x1. Average CMC 2.54. The deck wants black and red early: Flunkies or Noisemaker on turn 2 into a turn-3 Great Goblin, with Smaug the Magnificent arriving turn 4 and Orcrist turn 5.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Lead with Goblin-town Flunkies on turn 2 — a 2/1 haste body that starts the Army. Bothersome Noisemaker is the better turn-2 against removal-light decks because it turns every later spell into extra damage. Rage into the Valley is fine to fire off early if it keeps your curve rolling; Tidings of War should be held for the turn The Great Goblin resolves, where it becomes one mana for 2 damage. Azog on turn 3 doubles as removal and amass: killing a 4/4 is four Army counters and a ping.

Mid game

Land The Great Goblin as early as possible — it is the entire burn engine. From there, every amass spell is a Lightning Bolt that also grows the Army. Develop the treasure side once the Army is big: Smaug the Magnificent on turn 4 starts banking Treasures every upkeep, and Orcrist makes combat damage pay in gold. Crime Novelist turns those Treasures into counters — and counters into pings.

The combo turn

With The Great Goblin and Bothersome Noisemaker on board, each spell is worth two pings:

  • Rage into the Valley: amass 2 (2 damage) + Noisemaker amass 1 (2 damage) = 4 damage and a card.
  • Tidings of War: 4 damage, then flashback for amass 3 — another 4.
  • Clap! Snap! (adventure): 4 damage.
  • Goblin Plate Mail: 4 damage and the Army gains menace.
  • Gathering of Darkness: 4 damage plus a creature back.

A typical kill turn: Great Goblin + Noisemaker + Rage (4) + Tidings (4) + Tidings flashback (4) + Clap! Snap! (4) + Plate Mail (4) = 20 damage from spells alone, with the Army attacking as a 15/15+. Crime Novelist adds a counter-ping for every Treasure sacrificed, and Goblin-town’s land activation is a repeatable 2-damage ping from the mana base.

Win condition 2

If the burn gets answered, the gold plan wins: equip the Army with Orcrist (+2/+2, trample) and My Precious (hexproof, can’t be blocked) or activate Rogue’s Passage, then swing. Combat damage from Orcrist mints a Treasure per Goblin you control — with four Goblins on board that is four Treasures, which Smaug the Magnificent converts into damage on its own attack. The Misty Mountains Cold banks four Treasures and pays out a 6/6 flying Dragon, and Restless Vents provides a repeatable menace attacker. The Army usually ends the game in one or two swings; Smaug’s attack trigger closes out games where the Army is chump-blocked.

Mulligan guide

Keep: 2-3 lands with a turn-3 Great Goblin or early amass pieces (Flunkies, Rage, Noisemaker). The Great Goblin is the engine — hands without it need a fast Army plan or Azog interaction. Mulligan 4+ land hands with no action and 0-1 land hands. A black/red-heavy hand is fine; green is only needed for Warg Tactics.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Amass X puts X counters on the Army in one event — The Great Goblin pings once per amass event, not once per counter.
  • The Army token is a Goblin, so it triggers The Great Goblin, counts for Orcrist’s Treasure generation, and can be named with Cavern of Souls.
  • Goblin Plate Mail amasses 1 and attaches to the Army automatically — the ping and the menace happen on entry.
  • Azog destroys a creature and amasses X equal to its power: killing a 5/5 is 5 counters plus a ping; if the creature was yours, you draw a card.
  • Bolg of the North converts a sacrificed Fearsome Goblin Pair into power-damage, with the excess becoming amass — and the Pair’s death trigger amasses 4 on top.
  • My Precious’s adventure, Allure of Power, sacrifices a Goblin for draw 2 — sac a Fearsome Goblin Pair for amass 4 plus the draw.
  • Crime Novelist triggers on any sacrificed artifact — Treasures from Orcrist, Smaug, and The Misty Mountains Cold all convert to counters and red mana.
  • Smaug, Wicked Worm’s draw trigger checks Treasure mana specifically — sequence your Treasure sacs into your next spell’s cost.
  • Cavern of Souls mana only casts creature spells of the chosen type — don’t use it for Rage or Tidings.
