Overview

Rekindle the Lineage is a Jeskai Elemental deck that turns one card into an army. Champion of the Path is a 7/3 for four mana whose second line reads: whenever another Elemental you control enters, it deals damage equal to its power to each opponent. A token copy of the Champion is itself a 7/3 Elemental, so a single two-mana Molten Duplication is seven damage to each opponent, and Twinflame Travelers doubles that trigger to fourteen.

The three rolled mechanics carry the shell. Behold is the cost structure: Champion of the Path exiles an Elemental as an additional cost to be cast, Soulbright Seeker and Kinsbaile Aspirant use the softer “behold or pay” wording, and the flashback cost of Kindle the Inner Flame is literally behold three Elementals, which rewards the deck for going wide with the same creature type that the payoffs care about. Flashback is the second copy: Electroduplicate, Kindle the Inner Flame and Twinned Vision all cast a second time out of the graveyard, so the combo turn can extend past the cards in hand. Vivid counts colors among permanents, which is why the deck plays three colors and multicolor permanents such as Twinflame Travelers, Saheeli and the animated Restless Spire: three colors on board means Squawkroaster is a 3/4 double striker and Explosive Prodigy kills a three-toughness creature on entry.

Every nonland card is either an Elemental, a copy effect, or a payoff that counts Elementals or colors, so nothing is filler.

Approach

Game plan

  1. Turns 1-2, stack the board. Soulbright Seeker and Kinsbaile Aspirant are one-mana Elementals that also turn on the behold costs. Flamebraider ramps two mana for Elemental spells, and Saheeli comes down early as a two-mana body that becomes a repeatable copy engine.
  2. Turns 3-4, land the Champion. Cast Champion of the Path by exiling a spare Elemental, then immediately point Molten Duplication or Electroduplicate at it. Seven or fourteen damage to each opponent the turn the Champion arrives.
  3. Turns 5+, cash the graveyard. Kindle the Inner Flame from hand copies a Champion; its flashback (one red plus behold three Elementals) copies another. Caretaker’s Talent draws on every token and its level 2 turns one Champion token into two.
  4. Close. Squawkroaster with three colors on board is a 3/4 double striker, and Secret Tunnel sends the Champion and a Squawkroaster through unblocked for thirteen.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Lead with a one-drop whenever the hand has one, because the deck needs an Elemental on the battlefield before Champion of the Path is castable. Soulbright Seeker is the better turn one play: three activations of its trample ability in one turn add four red mana, which is exactly the mana for a second copy effect. Flamebraider on turn two into a four-drop on turn three is the fastest start. Hold Molten Duplication until the Champion resolves rather than copying a one-drop.

The combo turn

Champion of the Path in play, then in order of efficiency: Molten Duplication (two mana, seven damage), Electroduplicate (three mana, seven damage, and again later from the graveyard for five), Kindle the Inner Flame (four mana, seven damage, and its flashback only costs one red plus beholding three Elementals), Saheeli’s tap ability (two mana, once per turn, repeatable), Caretaker’s Talent level 2 targeting a Champion token (two mana plus the level cost, seven damage, and it draws a card). With Twinflame Travelers on board every one of those is fourteen instead of seven, so two copies in a turn is twenty-eight: lethal from twenty against any deck that has not gained life.

Win condition 2

Squawkroaster is the fair half of the deck. With a white, a blue and a red permanent on board it is a 3/4 double striker for four mana, which races most midrange decks on its own. Secret Tunnel turns it and the Champion into an unblockable pair, and Caretaker’s Talent at level 3 makes every Elemental token two power larger. Explosive Prodigy clears the one blocker that would otherwise eat a Squawkroaster attack.

Mulligan guide

Keep any hand with two lands, a one-drop Elemental and a copy effect, or any hand with three lands, Champion of the Path and Molten Duplication. Ship hands with no Elemental at one or two mana: the Champion cannot be cast without one. Ship one-land hands even with a cantrip, since Twinned Vision is the only cheap draw and it does not find lands. Five-card hands are acceptable if they contain Champion plus a copy spell, because that is a two-card kill.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Champion of the Path exiles the beheld Elemental, so casting it costs a creature or a card from hand. Keep a spare one-drop specifically for this.
  • When the Champion leaves the battlefield the exiled card returns to its owner’s hand, which makes the Champion a soft loop with a one-mana Elemental and a sacrifice outlet.
  • A token copy of the Champion never exiled anything, so its leaves-the-battlefield trigger returns nothing.
  • Twinflame Travelers only doubles triggered abilities of other Elementals, and it is itself an Elemental, so it does not double its own entry.
  • Kindle the Inner Flame is a Kindred Sorcery, an Elemental spell, so Flamebraider’s mana pays for it and the copy enters with haste.
  • Squawkroaster’s power is a characteristic-defining ability, so it is checked continuously: bouncing a white permanent shrinks it in the middle of combat.
  • Explosive Prodigy must target a creature an opponent controls; with only red permanents the damage is one.
  • Animated Restless Spire counts as a blue and red permanent for Vivid while it is a creature, and it is a legal Elemental to behold or to copy.

Control Do not commit the Champion into open countermagic; use Saheeli and Molten Duplication in the same turn as the Champion, and keep Twinned Vision for cards
