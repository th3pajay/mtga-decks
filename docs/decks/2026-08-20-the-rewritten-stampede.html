Overview

The Rewritten Stampede fuses three mechanics into one engine: Saddle mounts attack for value, Convoke spells are cast by tapping the same creatures, and Plan enchantments count every tap. The heart of the deck is Rewrite History: whenever one or more creatures you control become tapped, you draw a card, discard a card, and advance the plan. Attacking, saddling, convoking, and even Station all tap creatures — so every axis of the deck draws cards.

The other two Plans turn the same actions into resources. Death to Our Enemies makes a Treasure for every noncreature spell you cast (all your Convoke spells), which ramps the deck into its 5-6 mana finishers, and caps at 7 damage. Political Triumph turns every creature that enters into scry 1 plus a plan counter, ending in a global +1/+1 anthem.

The payoff is a go-wide Mount army. Seraphic Steed mints 3/3 flying Angels each saddled attack, Bridled Bighorn adds Sheep, Bounding Felidar pumps the whole team, Drover Grizzly gives trample, and Collective Inferno doubles all damage dealt by Mounts. Dracosaur Auxiliary swings in the air with haste and shocks anything. Games end in one massive, doubled, trampling stampede — or a 7-point burn finish.

Approach

Game plan phases

  1. Setup (T1-3): Play Political Triumph or a cheap Mount (Gilded Ghoda, Seraphic Steed). Untap the Roads lands with a Mount in play and land Rewrite History.
  2. Engine (T3-5): Resolve Rewrite History and Death to Our Enemies. Every saddle tap draws a card; every Convoke spell makes a Treasure. Mounts attack while saddled to generate tokens and Treasures.
  3. Stampede (T5-7): Slam Collective Inferno (choose Mount), saddle Dracosaur Auxiliary / Bounding Felidar / Guardian Sunmare with Pilots and tokens, and attack for doubled, trampling, lifelink damage.
  4. Cleanup: Burn with Death to Our Enemies (7 split), or bury the opponent in cards with Harmonized Crescendo (draw per Mount) and Rewrite History recursion.

Curve and mana

Average nonland CMC is 3.5. The curve peaks at 3-5; the two 6-drops (Dracosaur Auxiliary, Harmonized Crescendo) are effectively cheaper thanks to Convoke, Treasures, and Bucolic Ranch. The deck runs 23 lands but plays like 26: Treasures from Gilded Ghoda and Death to Our Enemies, plus creature-tapping for Convoke, generate the extra mana.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Mulligan for Rewrite History plus any 1-2 drop Mount or Political Triumph. A turn-1 Political Triumph followed by creatures scries every draw step. Gilded Ghoda is the best early Mount: 2/2, saddle 1, and every saddled attack makes a Treasure — ramp and card draw on one body. Seraphic Steed (2/2 first strike, lifelink) races aggro while it waits for saddle 4.

Mid game

Land Death to Our Enemies and start casting Convoke spells — each one makes a Treasure. Unswerving Sloth is the engine-breaker: saddle it (tap 4 power), attack, and it untaps every creature you control, setting up a free main-phase Convoke turn. Guardian Sunmare tutors any nonland permanent with mana value 3 or less on a saddled attack — fetch a second copy of a Plan, another Mount, or a Convoke spell.

The combo turn

  1. Have a wide board of Mounts and at least Collective Inferno (Mount).
  2. Main phase 1: tap Pilots and tokens to saddle Dracosaur Auxiliary (saddle 3), Bounding Felidar (saddle 2) and Guardian Sunmare (saddle 4). Every tap draws a card off Rewrite History and every noncreature spell cast so far made a Treasure.
  3. Attack: Dracosaur flies over for 4 (8 with Inferno) plus 2-4 shock damage; Felidar puts a +1/+1 counter on every other creature and gains life; Drover Grizzly grants trample; Seraphic Steed mints 3/3 Angels; lifelink keeps you ahead.
  4. Main phase 2: Harmonized Crescendo (choose Mount) draws a card per Mount permanent — routinely 8-12 cards — or Unexpected Assistance digs deeper. Rewrite History then returns the best instants/sorceries from the graveyard to hand for the next loop.

A fully developed board deals 25-40 doubled, trampling damage in one swing.

Win condition 2: burn and grind

Death to Our Enemies reaches four plan counters after four noncreature spells (Plans, Convoke spells, and lands’ activated abilities do not count — spells only). That is easy with this shell: the payoff is 7 damage split as you choose, usually 7 to the face to finish after one stampede. In grindy games, Rewrite History recurs Temporal Cleansing, Appeal to Eirdu, and Unexpected Assistance, while Harmonized Crescendo refills the hand off the Mount count.

Mulligan guide

Keep: any hand with Rewrite History or Political Triumph plus 2-3 lands and a creature. Keep 2-land hands only with a 1-2 drop Mount (Gilded Ghoda, Seraphic Steed). Ship: hands without a Mount or a Plan, and hands flooded with 5-6 mana spells. Bucolic Ranch is a strong keep — it fixes and finds the finishers.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Rewrite History triggers on any tap: attacking, saddling, convoking, and Station taps all draw. Plan the order — saddle first, then convoke, so each tap draws.
  • Pilots saddle as power 3: one Pilot covers saddle 1-3; two Pilots cover saddle 4 (Seraphic Steed, Guardian Sunmare, Unswerving Sloth).
  • The Wandering Rescuer protects tapped creatures with hexproof and blocks with flash, double strike — cast it with Convoke at instant speed.
  • Temporal Cleansing answers anything nonland, including indestructible threats (it tucks instead of destroying).
  • Adagia copies an enchantment at 12+ charge counters: copying Collective Inferno quadruples Mount damage; copying a Plan re-runs its payoff.
  • Death to Our Enemies cares about spells, not abilities: saddling and Station do not advance it, Convoke spells and Plans do.

Matchups

  • Aggro: Seraphic Steed’s lifelink, The Wandering Rescuer’s flash blocks, and Bounding Felidar’s lifegain stabilize; stall until the stampede.
  • Control: Death to Our Enemies ramps you through counterspells (Treasures are instant-speed mana), Rewrite History restocks after board wipes, and hexproof from The Wandering Rescuer protects tapped attackers.
  • Midrange: out-muscle with Angels, trample, and doubled damage; Temporal Cleansing removes the single biggest blocker.
