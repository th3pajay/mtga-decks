Blight the Levy

Mardu blight and mobilize combo — conscript the tokens, blight the conscripts, drain the table

Overview

Blight the Levy is a Mardu deck built on one observation: mobilize hands you free creatures, and blight wants creatures it can afford to ruin. Every mobilize attacker creates tapped and attacking 1/1 red Warrior tokens that are sacrificed at the beginning of the next end step. Those tokens are the perfect blight target — putting a -1/-1 counter on a body that dies at end of combat costs you nothing — and their deaths are the trigger that every payoff in the deck is waiting for.

The engine has three moving parts:

  • Conscripts. Dalkovan Packbeasts (mobilize 3), Voice of Victory (mobilize 2), Avenger of the Fallen (mobilize X, where X is the number of creature cards in your graveyard), plus the one-mana Stadium Headliner and Shock Brigade. Each attack widens the board without spending a card.
  • Blight as the outlet. Scuzzback Scrounger blights 1 in your first main phase for a Treasure, Shadow Urchin blights 1 on every attack and then exiles cards equal to the counters on a dying body, Boggart Mischief blights 1 for two Goblin tokens, Warren Torchmaster blights 1 to grant haste. All of these cost only a counter on a token that was already condemned.
  • Payoffs on death. Venerated Stormsinger drains each opponent for 1 and gains you 1 every time any creature you control dies; Marauding Blight-Priest converts that lifegain into another point of drain; Enduring Tenacity converts it into drain equal to the amount gained. Mobilize turns each combat step into a small drain loop.

The finisher is a genuine unbounded combo: with Bloodthirsty Conqueror and Marauding Blight-Priest on the battlefield, each point an opponent loses is life you gain, and each point you gain drains every opponent for 1 again. Enduring Tenacity plays the same role as the Blight-Priest, and any single lifegain — a Stormsinger death trigger, an attacking Restless Fortress — starts the loop.

Zurgo, Thunder's Decree flips the whole engine into beatdown: during your end step, Warrior tokens you control cannot be sacrificed, so mobilize tokens stop evaporating and pile up as a permanent army. Zurgo's Vanguard’s power is equal to the number of creatures you control, and Rogue's Passage makes it unblockable.

Approach

Game plan in three phases

  1. Deploy and attack (turns 1–3). Land a one- or two-drop mobilize creature and start attacking immediately. Scuzzback Scrounger is the ideal turn-two play: it ramps you with Treasure while teaching you to blight your own expendable bodies. Avenger of the Fallen is fine on turn three even with an empty graveyard — it grows every turn the tokens die.
  2. Build the drain engine (turns 4–6). Land Venerated Stormsinger and Marauding Blight-Priest, then keep attacking. Each mobilize token that dies is 1 drain from the Stormsinger and a second point from the Blight-Priest; a mobilized Dalkovan Packbeasts alone drains for 6 across the table over two turns.
  3. Close. Either assemble Bloodthirsty Conqueror plus Marauding Blight-Priest / Enduring Tenacity for the unbounded loop, or drop Zurgo, Thunder's Decree, stop sacrificing tokens, and swing with a Zurgo's Vanguard that is as large as your board.

Curve and mana

Thirty-six nonland cards: two one-drops, six two-drops, seventeen three-drops, nine four-drops and two five-drops, for an average mana value of 3.1. The deck wants to attack from turn two, so the manabase is built around untapped sources: nine shock lands, four fast lands and six verges. Black is the most demanded color (Blight-Priest, Stormsinger, Conqueror, Avenger), so the shock split is biased toward Blood Crypt and Godless Shrine. Scuzzback Scrounger Treasures pay for the heavier Mardu cards, including the {1}{R}{W}{B} cost of Bone-Cairn Butcher.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Lead with a one- or two-drop that attacks. Stadium Headliner into Scuzzback Scrounger is the fastest opening: the Scrounger’s first-main blight 1 turns a spare body into a Treasure, and the Treasure casts your three-drop a turn early. Do not be precious about the -1/-1 counters. The correct blight target is almost always the smallest token you control, preferably one that will be sacrificed at end of combat anyway.

Mid game and the combo turn

The deck does not need to hold back. Attack every turn to keep generating tokens, and stack drain triggers. The combo turn is simple once both pieces are down: play Bloodthirsty Conqueror, then any lifegain starts the loop. Venerated Stormsinger plus a dying token is the most common ignition; an attacking Restless Fortress is the most stylish. With Marauding Blight-Priest and Bloodthirsty Conqueror both on the battlefield, each iteration drains 1 and gains 1, so the loop repeats until the table is dead. Enduring Tenacity is the backup half of the combo, and it recurs itself, which makes it the harder piece for opponents to answer permanently.

Win condition two

Zurgo, Thunder's Decree converts the mobilize engine into a permanent army. Once Zurgo is down, your Warrior tokens survive your end step, so a single Dalkovan Packbeasts attack leaves three bodies behind. From there Zurgo's Vanguard is a creature whose power equals your board, and Rogue's Passage for {4} makes it unblockable. Bone-Cairn Butcher gives all attacking tokens deathtouch, so even a chump-blocked swarm trades up.

Mulligan guide

Keep hands with two to three lands, at least one two- or three-drop mobilize creature, and one blight outlet or drain payoff. A hand of only blight enablers with no mobilize is a mulligan. Voice of Victory is a strong keep against blue, because opponents cannot cast spells during your turn while it is on the battlefield.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Blight on a doomed token is free. Mobilize tokens are sacrificed at the beginning of the next end step, so a -1/-1 counter on one costs nothing. Blight first, attack second where possible.
  • Shadow Urchin counts counters, not just tokens. When a creature you control with one or more counters dies, you exile that many cards and may play them until your next end step. Counters placed by Restless Bivouac or Bone-Cairn Butcher count too.
  • Kill triggers are not optional value. Venerated Stormsinger triggers on its own death as well, so it drains even through a sweeper.
  • Zurgo changes the clock, not the trigger. With Zurgo out, the mobilize sacrifice is prevented during your end step, so tokens persist; they are still 1/1 Warriors and still fuel the next attack.

Matchups

  • Aggro: chump with tokens and drain them out. Bone-Cairn Butcher’s deathtouch tokens are the key card.
  • Control: Voice of Victory is your best card, blanking their turn. Hold it until you are ready to commit the combo, and keep a second threat in hand because sweepers still cost you the board.
  • Midrange: your drain engine grinds better than theirs. Enduring Tenacity recurs, so their removal is a tempo play rather than a real answer.
  • Combo: attack pressure forces them to answer the board instead of assembling.
