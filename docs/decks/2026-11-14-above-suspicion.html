Above Suspicion

Dimir (blue / black) surveil-reanimator combo

Primary win Zombify returns Valgavoth, Terror Eater, a 9/9 flying lifelink Demon, on turn four or five

Overview

The deck is built on one loop: look at the top of the library, throw the bad half into the graveyard, keep the good half, and get paid for both halves.

Surveil and scry do the work. Opt, Dreams of Laguna and Wreckage Wickerfolk surveil or scry on entry, Undercity Sewers surveils as it enters, and Umbral Collar Zealot and Joo Dee, One of Many turn spare permanents into repeated surveil. Every one of those triggers is a graveyard card, which is the fuel Abhorrent Oculus needs (it asks for six cards exiled from the graveyard as an additional cost) and the reason Zombify always has a target.

Matoya, Archon Elder is the hinge. It reads “whenever you scry or surveil, draw a card,” so each one-mana Opt becomes scry one and draw two, Dreams of Laguna becomes surveil one and draw two with a flashback copy later, and Joo Dee becomes a repeatable draw engine that also copies itself. Matoya is the reason a deck with 23 lands and no card-advantage sorcery can chain through half its library by turn five.

The payoff is deliberately blunt. Zombify costs {3}{B} and puts any creature card from the graveyard onto the battlefield, so a Chart a Course discard or a Bitter Triumph additional cost can turn into a turn-four 9/9 flier with lifelink. Valgavoth, Terror Eater also exiles everything the opponent loses and lets you cast their cards for life, which means the Demon is a card-advantage engine as well as a clock. When the Demon is not available, Abhorrent Oculus is a 5/5 flier for three mana that manifests dread on every opponent’s upkeep.

The second half of the deck is the grind: Umbral Collar Zealot and Joo Dee generate and eat tokens, Restless Reef attacks as a 4/4 deathtouch Shark, and Rogue's Passage makes the biggest body unblockable. Nothing in the list is a standalone body; every card either digs, surveils, discards, or converts a full graveyard into a threat.

Approach

Game plan in three phases

  1. Turns 1-2, load the graveyard and shape the hand. Opt, Sleight of Hand and Wreckage Wickerfolk are the openers. Wreckage Wickerfolk is the best turn-two play: a 1/3 flier that surveils two, which is two cards in the graveyard and two cards closer to Zombify. Undercity Sewers on turn one is worth the tapped land, because the surveil is a real card. Joo Dee and Umbral Collar Zealot come down here as bodies that will become repeatable surveil later.
  2. Turns 3-4, turn on the engine. Matoya, Archon Elder converts every scry and surveil into a card, so from here every cheap spell is a two-for-one. Stock Up finds the missing half of the combo (Demon or Zombify), Chart a Course draws two and discards the Demon or a spare land, Bitter Triumph kills a blocker while discarding the Demon as its additional cost. Joo Dee plus Zealot gives a free surveil every turn without spending a card.
  3. Turns 4-6, convert. Zombify on Valgavoth is the primary line; Abhorrent Oculus for three mana is the backup. Once a 9/9 or 5/5 flier is on the board, Rogue's Passage and Restless Reef end the game through blockers, and Matoya keeps the hand full so removal does not matter.

Curve and mana

Six one-drops, sixteen two-drops, ten three-drops and three four-drops, with the two Demons as the only expensive cards in the list and both of them meant to be cheated in. Average mana value sits under 2.5, so the deck functions on three lands and does not need a fourth to operate.

Twenty-three lands with seventeen blue sources and thirteen black sources. Blue is the heavier requirement (Opt, Sleight of Hand, Matoya, Stock Up, Chart a Course, Dreams of Laguna, Oculus), while black is concentrated at two and four mana. The four Undercity Sewers are the only lands that enter tapped unconditionally; Watery Grave can come in untapped for 2 life, which matters on turn four when Zombify has to be cast on curve.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Keep hands with two lands and a one-mana look spell. The ideal sequence is Undercity Sewers into Wreckage Wickerfolk: the surveil land fixes the draw, the Wickerfolk surveils two more, and by turn three the graveyard has enough cards that Abhorrent Oculus is castable. Do not be afraid to discard Valgavoth to Chart a Course on turn three; that is the plan, not a cost.

Joo Dee is not an attacker in the early game. Play it, wait a turn, then activate it on a turn where the copy and the sacrifice both matter: keep the token, sacrifice the original, and the board is unchanged while the graveyard grows and Matoya draws.

The combo turn

The turn the deck wins usually looks like this:

  1. Zombify with {3}{B}, returning Valgavoth, Terror Eater after a Chart a Course or Bitter Triumph put it in the graveyard.
  2. Attack for 9 with flying and lifelink. The Demon’s ward makes the usual removal trade cost three nonland permanents, so most decks cannot answer it.
  3. From that point the Demon exiles everything the opponent loses and lets you cast their spells for life, so the game becomes a race the opponent cannot win.

If the Demon is not in the graveyard yet, Abhorrent Oculus is the same turn for three mana: exile six cards from the graveyard, resolve a 5/5 flier, and every opponent upkeep afterwards manifests dread, which is another two cards of library selection per turn cycle.

Win condition 2

Without the Demon the deck still closes. Restless Reef activates for {2}{U}{B} into a 4/4 deathtouch Shark that mills four whenever it attacks, which is both a clock and a way to dig deeper. Umbral Collar Zealot and Joo Dee tokens chip in, and Rogue's Passage turns any one of them into an unblockable attacker for {4}. Against decks with ground blockers this is the plan: two Reefs plus a Passage is eight unblockable damage a turn from lands alone.

Mulligan guide

Keep: two or three lands plus any two of Opt, Sleight of Hand, Wreckage Wickerfolk, Matoya. A hand with Chart a Course or Bitter Triumph and a Zombify is a keep even without the Demon, because Stock Up and Opt find it.

Mulligan: one-land hands, hands with no blue source, and hands that are all three-drops with no look spell. Two Demons in the opener is a mulligan unless a Chart a Course is also there.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Abhorrent Oculus needs six cards exiled. Check the graveyard count before casting; with Dreams of Laguna and Wickerfolk it is normally reached by turn four. The exile is a cost, so it cannot be responded to by removing the Oculus.
  • Matoya triggers on scry as well as surveil. Opt is a scry, so it draws two cards with Matoya out. Stock Up does not scry and does not trigger.
  • Joo Dee copies itself, then you sacrifice. The copy is created before the sacrifice, so you always keep exactly one Joo Dee and the trigger repeats every turn. Sacrifice the token and keep the original to preserve the engine.
  • Umbral Collar Zealot needs fodder. It sacrifices another creature or artifact, not itself. Pair it with Joo Dee tokens, spare Wreckage Wickerfolk, or a Clue-style artifact.
  • Bitter Triumph can bin the Demon. The additional cost is “discard a card or pay 3 life,” so it is both removal and a way to put Valgavoth where Zombify can reach it.
  • Chart a Course discard happens after the draw, so sequence it before Zombify and not after.
  • Rogue's Passage adds colorless mana. It cannot cast Opt or Zombify on its own; count it as a spell-land, not a source.

Matchups

  • Aggro: the deck’s cheap interaction is thin, so race with lifelink. Valgavoth ends aggro races on its own because every attack gains 9. Bitter Triumph on their best attacker plus Umbral Collar Zealot blockers is usually enough to reach turn five.
  • Control: Matoya is the whole matchup. Play it before the Demon, force them to answer the draw engine, and let Joo Dee and Zealot grind out card advantage. Rogue's Passage beats their blockers.
  • Midrange: the reanimation line is faster than their curve. Restless Reef trades with anything on the ground and the 9/9 flier ignores their board.
  • Combo: no counterspells in the main deck, so the plan is speed: Zombify on turn four with the Demon plus Rogue's Passage the following turn is the fastest clock available.
