Size 60 cards
Primary win Smaug the Magnificent burn: treasures make his attack trigger deal N damage to any target, and he has flying and haste — one swing with a full hoard is 10+ to the face

Overview

Wing and Coin is the Smaug deck of The Hobbit set: a dragon engine that converts gold into damage and damage into a milled library. The engine is Treasure — made five different ways (Smaug’s upkeep trigger, Wicked Worm’s entry burst, the Misty Mountains saga, Gleaming Splendor, Dori, the Grey Dog) — and the payoff is Smaug the Magnificent, whose attack trigger deals damage equal to the number of Treasures you control to any target. A hoard of six tokens turns every attack into a 6-damage fireball plus his 4 flying haste damage.

The second half of the sentence is written on The Master of Lake-town: whenever any player loses life, that player mills that many cards. Damage causes loss of life, so Smaug’s burn becomes mass-mill and Inside Information exiles the library directly. The deck wins by burning, by milling, or by burying the opponent under dragon beats and Dwarf tokens — three kill lines out of one engine.

The set is scheduled for Arena on 2026-08-14 (arena_id is still empty in the database, so the import list works from that date).

Approach

Game plan phases

Turns 1-3 Develop the hoard: Sackville-Bagginses, Gleaming Splendor, Last Light, Dori, or the Grey Dog; Stir Up Trouble and Deadly Slice hold the board
Turns 3-5 Engines online: The Misty Mountains Cold makes a Treasure every chapter and pops a 6/6 Dragon; Smaug, Wicked Worm enters for a burst of tapped gold
Turns 5-7 The combo turn: Smaug the Magnificent lands with a hoard behind him; Desolation clears non-Dragons and pays for the next dragon
Late game Win condition 2: The Master of Lake-town turns every Smaug trigger into mill; Were-Worm’s extra combat doubles the burn

Curve and mana

24 lands: 4 The Lonely Mountain, 2 Hobbit Hole, 2 Sundown Pass, 1 Elegant Parlor, 2 Inspiring Vantage, 2 Temple of Malice, 1 Temple of Triumph, 1 Temple of Silence, 1 Restless Fortress, 1 Razortrap Gorge, 2 Mountain, 5 Swamp. Red sources 15, black 10, white 8 — red is the workhorse, white a light splash.

Nonland curve: 1-drop x2, 2-drop x13, 3-drop x11, 4-drop x4, 5-drop x3, 6-drop x2, 7-drop x1. Average CMC 3.08. The deck wants a Treasure maker on turn 2, a saga or Dori on turn 3, and Smaug the Magnificent on turn 4-5 with a hoard already forming.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Turn 1 is Stir Up Trouble (holding up {B} to eat a Treasure and kill a threat) or a tapped Temple. Turn 2: The Sackville-Bagginses, Gleaming Splendor, Last Light, or removal. Sequence tapped lands first — Temples, Elegant Parlor, and Restless Fortress enter tapped, so keep Sundown Pass and Inspiring Vantage for the turns you need untapped red. Fetching a Mountain with Hobbit Hole also advances the Last Light quest.

Mid game

Resolve The Misty Mountains Cold and let the chapters stack Treasures; it turns itself into a 6/6 Dragon at four tokens. Smaug, Wicked Worm is the mid-game haymaker: against artifact decks it enters with four or five tapped Treasures, and every spell cast from that gold draws a card. Gleaming Splendor turns the opponent’s card draw into more gold, and its {2}{W} mode makes two players draw — forcing the trigger and cantripping you at once.

The combo turn

  1. Accumulate a hoard: four to eight Treasures from the saga, Splendor, Wicked Worm, Dori, and the Grey Dog.
  2. Cast Smaug the Magnificent ({2}{R}{R}, haste, flying). If the board is crowded, Desolation of Smaug first — it deals 3 to every non-Dragon and its four mana can pay for Smaug himself.
  3. Attack: Smaug’s trigger deals damage equal to your Treasure count to any target — face, planeswalker, or blocker — plus his 4 combat damage.
  4. Repeat: every upkeep makes another Treasure, so each swing is bigger. With Desert Were-Worm (growing off every Mountain) attacking alongside, 12+ total power grants an extra combat and Smaug’s burn fires twice.

A typical kill: six Treasures and Smaug attacking alone is 10 damage; the next turn is 11, and the turn after that is 12. Wicked Worm’s draw-on-Treasure-cast keeps the hand full through the sequence.

Win condition 2

The Master of Lake-town turns the same engine into mill. Every point of life an opponent loses — Smaug’s attack trigger, his combat damage, Restless Fortress’s drain — mills that many cards off the top; a 6-Treasure Smaug swing mills 10. Inside Information finishes: with a hoard of Treasures paying any color, X = 10 or more exiles a huge chunk of the library outright, and the exiled cards are playable (paying life instead of mana, which conveniently mills more). The Arkenstone’s adventure tutors either Smaug or the Master, whichever the board needs. Backup: The Lonely Mountain’s Dwarf tokens, the saga’s 6/6 Dragon, and Restless Fortress beats.

Mulligan guide

Keep: two to three lands with a Treasure maker or removal — Sackville-Bagginses, Gleaming Splendor, Dori, or Stir Up Trouble openers are strong; any hand with The Misty Mountains Cold or Smaug, Wicked Worm plus interaction is excellent. Mulligan one-land hands and hands with five or more lands and no engine. White is a splash: a hand with only red and black is keepable, but a hand that cannot cast a two-drop by turn 2 is not.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Smaug the Magnificent’s attack trigger targets — it can hit creatures and planeswalkers as well as the opponent; point it at a blocker and Smaug’s own combat damage still connects.
  • The Misty Mountains Cold checks “four or more Treasures” after each chapter — with Gleaming Splendor and the Grey Dog it can pop its Dragon as early as chapter II.
  • Smaug, Wicked Worm counts artifacts the opponent controls at entry — Bilbo’s Gambit’s gifted Treasure feeds it, so hold Wicked Worm for when they have an artifact board.
  • Last Light of Durin’s Day counts Mountains entering, not lands — fetch Mountains specifically with Hobbit Hole and Thrór’s Map; The Lonely Mountain is a Mountain.
  • Desert Were-Worm’s extra combat triggers “the first time each turn” you attack with 12+ total power — attack with it and Smaug together so both get the second combat. Gleaming Splendor’s {2}{W} targets two players: choose an opponent and yourself to force the Treasure trigger and draw your own card.
