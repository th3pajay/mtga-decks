Dragon’s Ledger

Jund (B/R/G) Smaug Treasure-Dragon — The Hobbit set identity deck

hobbit
smaug
Published

August 30, 2026

At a glance

Attribute Value
Format Standard (MTGA)
Colors Black / Red / Green (Jund)
Archetype Combo-midrange (avg CMC 3.1)
Core mechanic Treasure tokens x Mountain landfall x Dragons
Primary win Smaug the Magnificent pings for damage equal to your Treasure count, doubled by extra combat
Secondary win Dragon swarm — 6/6 Dragon from The Misty Mountains Cold, Smaug, the Great Calamity, Desert Were-Worm
Land win condition The Lonely Mountain 2/2 Dwarf army, {1} cheaper per Equipment

Overview

Dragon’s Ledger is the Jund Smaug deck: it treats Treasure tokens as both ramp and ammunition. Every nonland card either makes a Treasure, puts Mountains into play, is a Dragon, or discounts The Lonely Mountain — the land that doubles as the third win condition.

The engine is two loops that feed one payoff. The Treasure loop: Bejeweled Warg, Dori, The Sackville-Bagginses, Long-Bodied Grey Dog, Smaug’s own upkeep trigger and The Misty Mountains Cold all mint Treasures, which fix Jund’s three colors, accelerate into Dragons, and make Smaug the Magnificent’s attack trigger lethal. The Mountain loop: Wood Elves, Troop of Ponies and Through the Forest Gate put lands into play, stacking quest counters on Last Light of Durin’s Day (six Mountains tutors any Dragon), pumping Desert Were-Worm by +2/+0 per Mountain, and growing Mirkwood Pathmaker. When both loops converge, Smaug attacks, the Worm grants a second combat, and the opponent takes two treasure-counted pings in one turn.

Curve and mana

Twenty-six lands with fourteen basics and eight duals; Treasures are the real acceleration, so the deck plays like a 24-land deck with ritual-style bursts. The curve peaks at four (Smaug the Magnificent) with a top end of seven (Smaug, the Great Calamity, Through the Forest Gate). Last Light of Durin’s Day’s Mountaincycling and Troop of Ponies smooth the colored sources.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Play a two-drop Treasure engine or Last Light of Durin’s Day on turn two. The Sackville-Bagginses wants a sacrifice available — a Treasure token works. Do not hold Wood Elves; the land count is the deck’s clock. Against aggressive decks, Long-Bodied Grey Dog (reach, flash) and Desert Were-Worm’s 0/5 body stabilize while the engine builds.

Mid game

Resolve Smaug the Magnificent and start banking Treasures. The Misty Mountains Cold doubles as both engine (four Treasures) and finisher (6/6 Dragon). Use Smaug’s ping as removal on problem creatures instead of racing life totals if needed — it hits any target.

The combo turn

The signature sequence: attack with Desert Were-Worm plus at least 12 total power, untap all attackers, get the extra combat, and have Smaug the Magnificent deal treasure-count damage twice. A typical mid-game count of six to eight Treasures becomes 12-16 damage from Smaug alone, plus the attackers’ damage. Rogue’s Passage makes the first Smaug swing unblockable.

Win condition 2

The Dragon swarm: The Misty Mountains Cold’s 6/6 Dragon token, Smaug, the Great Calamity, and a pumped Desert Were-Worm. Desolation of Smaug clears everything non-Dragon and floats four mana to cast a Dragon the same turn. Last Light of Durin’s Day tutors whichever Dragon the board is missing — it can also grab a Dragon already in hand for free.

Land win condition

With four Equipment on the battlefield (Ragged Short Spear, Well-Worn Spatula, Orcrist), The Lonely Mountain activates for {0}{R} and floods the board with 2/2 Dwarves. Orcrist turns those Dwarves into Treasures: an equipped attacker choosing Dwarf makes one Treasure per Dwarf you control, which feeds Smaug’s next ping.

Mulligan guide

Keep any hand with a two-drop engine or Last Light plus three lands. Hands with Smaug the Magnificent and no Treasure source are too slow — the first ping needs a Treasure count. Mirkwood/Goblin-town are acceptable duals but enter tapped, so keep basics-heavy openers against aggro. Through the Forest Gate is a late-game card; ship it in the opener.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Smaug’s attack trigger hits any target — planeswalkers, players, or creatures — and resolves on each combat, so the extra combat from Desert Were-Worm means two triggers per turn.
  • Smaug, Wicked Worm draws only when mana from a Treasure pays for the spell; sacrifice Treasures on the cast, not before.
  • Last Light of Durin’s Day checks Mountain ETB, and its own Mountaincycling plus Troop of Ponies count. It sacrifices itself at six quest counters and tutors (hand and/or library) — with six Mountains and a Dragon in hand it simply puts it onto the battlefield.
  • Desert Were-Worm’s extra combat triggers only the first time each turn you attack with 12+ total power; it untaps all attackers, so Smaug, the Worm and the Dwarf tokens all swing twice.
  • The Lonely Mountain enters tapped unless you control an Equipment — one Equipment on board keeps all four copies untapped.
  • Desolation of Smaug’s four mana is Dragon-only; use it for Wicked Worm or Great Calamity, never for Gnashing of Teeth.
  • Through the Forest Gate can put any number of lands from the top twenty into play — with several Mountains that is multiple Last Light quest counters and a big Were-Worm in a single spell.
  • Gnashing of Teeth’s -5/-5 mode exiles the creature if it would die, denying recursion.

Matchups

  • Aggro (fliers, lifegain, cats, landfall): Desolation of Smaug and Gnashing of Teeth reset the board; Grey Dog and Were-Worm block; Smaug’s ping removes the biggest threat. The deck’s life buffer is Through the Forest Gate and Well-Worn Spatula.
  • Midrange: trade resources with Smaug pings, then win the value war — Wicked Worm turns every Treasure into a card.
  • Control: sagas are sticky, Treasure mana makes it easy to hold up interaction, and Desolation punishes creature-light boards. Last Light’s tutor forces answers.
  • Combo race: the extra-combat turn ends the game faster than most opposing engines; Rogue’s Passage ignores chump blockers.
