Overview

Paid in Blood is a five-color engine that turns dying into damage. Every creature that dies pays the opponent: Vein Ripper drains 2 on any creature death anywhere, Cauldron of Essence drains 1 and gains 1 on each of your deaths, and the life gained feeds Marauding Blight-Priest, which drains 1 more per trigger. Scavenger’s Talent converts deaths into Food tokens, Demand Answers and Eaten Alive spend those tokens on cards and removal, and Ruthless Lawbringer trades any creature for any nonland permanent.

The fodder never runs out. Bonebind Orator and Unstoppable Slasher return from the graveyard, Cauldron of Essence reanimates one creature per activation, Raise the Past floods back every 2-mana-value creature, and Hop to It, Kambal (token copies via Three Steps Ahead) and Great Arashin City keep tokens flowing. Bloodletter of Aclazotz doubles the entire drain package on your turn, turning a modest loop into a kill.

Approach

Game plan phases

  1. Seeding (turns 1-3). Land a two-drop: Marauding Blight-Priest, Ruthless Lawbringer, Kambal, Profiteering Mayor or Bonebind Orator. Use Eaten Alive on early threats and let Scavenger’s Talent start banking Food.
  2. Assembly (turns 4-5). Resolve Cauldron of Essence or Bloodthirsty Conqueror. Spend tokens on Demand Answers and Witherbloom Charm for card advantage. Saddle The Gitrog to draw into the engine.
  3. Drain clock (turn 6 onward). With Vein Ripper, Bloodletter of Aclazotz and Cauldron of Essence online, every sacrifice drains 3 or more and gains life. Finish with flying attackers, manlands, or a Raise the Past flood.

Curve and mana. 26 lands: 7 basics, 19 fixing and utility lands. Average mana value is just under 3. Black is the core color, white and green support it, and red and blue are splashed for Demand Answers, Choreographed Sparks and Three Steps Ahead.

Gameplay strategy

Early game. Prioritize a two-drop over a tapped land when the colors allow. Play Scoured Barrens early for the life-gain trigger. Spend Eaten Alive freely on aggressive starts; the sacrifice cost feeds the engine either way.

Mid game. Assemble Cauldron of Essence plus one drain payoff before grinding. Sacrifice Food tokens to Demand Answers rather than creatures, and let Ruthless Lawbringer convert expendable creatures into premium removal. The Gitrog wants a saddle creature ready when it attacks.

The combo turn. With Cauldron of Essence and Vein Ripper on the battlefield, each sacrifice drains 3 and gains 3 (1 from Cauldron, 2 from Vein Ripper). Recur Bonebind Orator or Unstoppable Slasher and repeat every turn. Bloodletter of Aclazotz doubles the drain to 6 per sacrifice. A single Food token becomes 3 life and 3 damage per cycle.

Win condition 2. Attack with Bloodthirsty Conqueror, Vein Ripper and Vampire Nighthawk over the top. Amonkhet Raceway grants haste at max speed, which the drain package reaches quickly. Soulstone Sanctuary and Crawling Barrens are repeatable attackers, and Raise the Past turns a board wipe into a swarm.

Mulligan guide. Keep any hand with two or three lands including early black or white. A two-drop plus removal is a strong keep. Mulligan hands with mostly tapped lands or no early plays. Scavenger’s Talent plus a sacrifice outlet is the best opener.

Key interactions and edge cases.

  • Cauldron of Essence reanimation is sorcery-speed only; plan the recursion around your main phase.
  • Vein Ripper’s ward cost is a sacrifice, so it protects itself from removal by draining the opponent.
  • Eaten Alive exiles, which bypasses opposing death triggers but still triggers your own via the sacrifice cost.
  • Unstoppable Slasher returns tapped with stun counters, so it is a slow but repeatable clock.
  • The Gitrog only draws when it deals combat damage while saddled; do not attack it into obvious blockers without a trick.
  • Three Steps Ahead can copy any of your creatures into a token, which then triggers Kambal and feeds the sacrifice engine.

Matchups. Against aggro, early removal and the life-gain drain package stabilize and out-race. Against control, Three Steps Ahead protects key pieces and the recursion engine grinds through sweepers. Against other midrange decks, the drain clock is faster than conventional damage because it ignores blockers.
