Hoardfire

Jund (Black / Red / Green) — Smaug treasure-hoard burn from The Hobbit set

hobbit
smaug

At a glance

Attribute Value
Format Standard (MTGA)
Colors Black / Red / Green (Jund)
Archetype Smaug treasure-hoard burn
Size 60 cards (24 creatures, 12 noncreature spells, 24 lands)
Primary win Smaug the Magnificent attacks, dealing damage equal to your Treasure count to any target
Secondary win The Misty Mountains Cold converts four Treasures into a 6/6 flying Dragon; Smaug, the Great Calamity and Desert Were-Worm close in the air
Land win con The Lonely Mountain is a Mountain that makes 2/2 Dwarf tokens (one mana cheaper per Equipment) and drives Last Light of Durin’s Day’s quest counters

Overview

Hoardfire is a treasure engine attached to a Dragon. Every permanent in the deck either makes a Treasure or spends one, and Smaug the Magnificent is the meter that turns the hoard into damage: he has flying and haste, and whenever he attacks he deals damage equal to the number of Treasures you control to any target. Because the count is read at attack time, the deck wants to bank Treasures, not cash them — Smaug’s own upkeep trigger adds one every turn he lives.

The Hobbit set supplies the whole core fresh: Smaug the Magnificent and Smaug, Wicked Worm, the four-chapter saga The Misty Mountains Cold, Desolation of Smaug, Last Light of Durin’s Day, Dori, Bearer of Friends, Bejeweled Warg, Noggle Robber and The Lonely Mountain. Black and green only brace the engine — Treasure Dredger and The Sackville-Bagginses keep the bank growing, Bejeweled Warg and Noggle Robber are efficient bodies that pay in Treasures, and black removal keeps Smaug’s attack lane clear.

The Hobbit set lands on Arena 2026-08-14; the import list works from that date.

Approach

Game plan phases

Phase What happens
Turns 1-3 Bank: Noggle Robber, Bejeweled Warg and Dori each add a Treasure; Treasure Dredger repeats; removal clears blockers
Turns 4-5 Convert: hasted Smaug swings for your Treasure count; Smaug, Wicked Worm draws a card off every Treasure-paid spell
Turns 6+ Multiply: The Misty Mountains Cold makes a 6/6 flying Dragon, Desolation of Smaug sweeps non-Dragons and refunds four Dragon-only mana, Rapacious Dragon and Desert Were-Worm finish

Curve and mana

Average mana value across the 36 nonland cards is 3.44, spread as two mana: 11, three mana: 12, four mana: 5, five mana: 4, six mana: 2, seven mana: 2. The deck plays 24 lands with 14 red, 10 black and 9 green sources, so the early turns are always red or black and green arrives through Wastewood Verge or a Forest. Treasures pay for anything, which is what makes a three-colour deck with a seven-drop castable: Rapacious Dragon and Desolation of Smaug both hand back mana in the same turn they are played.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Lead with lands and a two-drop that banks a Treasure. Noggle Robber is the best turn-three play when there is nothing on board, because it is a 3/3 that leaves a Treasure behind on either side of the board. Bejeweled Warg wants to attack immediately — trample damage to a player is what turns it into a Treasure. Hold Pinecone Strike and Bilbo’s Deadly Slice for the first threat that would otherwise block Smaug; the deck wins by keeping its attacker’s lane clear, not by trading.

Mid game

Smaug the Magnificent on turn four is the pivot. If he lands with three or more Treasures, the attack is already a third of the opponent’s life total and the upkeep trigger makes the next one larger. From there the hoard is read twice per turn: Smaug’s attack uses it, Smaug, Wicked Worm converts spent Treasure mana into cards. Cast with Treasure mana whenever possible — Wicked Worm’s draw triggers on spent Treasure mana, not on the Treasure being sacrificed, so a spell paid with a Treasure and one land still triggers it.

The combo turn

The kill turn is Smaug the Magnificent plus a hoard of five or more Treasures. Attack, and the trigger deals five-plus damage to any target — face for the win, or a blocker that would otherwise trade. Because the damage is dealt by the attack trigger and not by combat damage, Smaug does not need to connect: an unblocked Smaug, a chump-blocked Smaug and a Smaug attacking into a bigger flyer all deal the same damage. Desert Were-Worm on the following turn is usually lethal, since attacking with 12 or more total power untaps the attackers and grants a second combat phase.

Win condition 2

The Misty Mountains Cold is the independent plan. Chapters one through three add a Treasure each; if you control four or more Treasures when a chapter resolves, the saga sacrifices itself and leaves a 6/6 red Dragon with flying. With two Treasures already banked the saga pays off on its second chapter — turn five on a normal curve — and the card is never dead, because the Treasures it makes are exactly what Smaug and Desolation of Smaug want. Rapacious Dragon and Smaug, the Great Calamity are the air force behind it, and Last Light of Durin’s Day can put a Dragon straight onto the battlefield once six Mountains have entered.

Mulligan guide

Keep any hand with two or three lands, one of them red, and a two- or three-mana Treasure source (Bejeweled Warg, Noggle Robber, Dori, Treasure Dredger or The Sackville-Bagginses). Keep a hand with The Misty Mountains Cold and four lands even if the rest is filler — the saga is a self-contained clock. Ship hands that need both black and green before turn four with only one land of either, and ship one-land hands even with a two-drop, because the deck has no one-mana play. Hobbit Hole is not a mana source: never count it as one of the two lands you need to keep.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Smaug the Magnificent’s trigger counts Treasures at the moment of attack. Sacrificing Treasures for mana during the attack step shrinks the trigger, so crack them after damage or in the second main phase.
  • The Misty Mountains Cold checks the Treasure count as each chapter resolves. Order matters if you are playing a Treasure-maker in the same turn: play the maker first, then let the chapter resolve.
  • Desolation of Smaug deals 3 to each non-Dragon, including your own Bejeweled Warg and Noggle Robber. Cast it after Smaug is out, or accept the trade when your Dragons are still lethal through the air.
  • Orcrist, Goblin-cleaver’s Treasure count is per creature you control of the chosen type. Naming Rogue or Warrior in a board of Rogues and Warriors is usually more Treasures than naming Dragon.
  • The Lonely Mountain enters tapped unless an Equipment is on the battlefield, and Orcrist is the only Equipment in the deck — deploy Orcrist before the Mountain if the untapped land matters.
  • Treasure Dredger, Treasure Dredger and similar activated abilities can be used at instant speed, which means you can hold a Treasure back and activate it in response to a removal spell that targets Smaug, then swing for the larger count.

Matchups

Against aggro, the plan is to trade early and let Smaug’s attack trigger act as removal; Pinecone Strike and Desolation of Smaug carry the mid-game, and The Lonely Mountain’s Dwarf tokens are free blockers. Against midrange, bank Treasures and hold removal for the turn Smaug attacks — a five-Treasure trigger plus a 4/3 flyer ends games in two swings. Against control, lead with The Sackville-Bagginses and Treasure Dredger to force interaction, keep Smaug for a turn where the opponent is tapped out, and remember that the saga and Rapacious Dragon are a second, independent clock that does not need the commander-style payoff to survive.
