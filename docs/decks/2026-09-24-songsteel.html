Red / White with a blue splash — Storied Dwarves, Equipment, and the enduring story

Overview

Songsteel is today’s The Hobbit set identity deck, and it takes the Storied Dwarves axis rather than the amass Goblins, the Smaug and Treasure hoard, or the Elf legends. The thesis: the set’s Storied mechanic is a switch, and this deck flips it on turn three and never looks back.

Storied reads “if you control three or more artifacts, legendaries, and/or Sagas, you have an enduring story for the rest of the game.” The deck carries twenty-six legendary cards and two Equipment, plus Axe Equipment tokens from Iron Hills Blacksmith and Dáin Ironfoot, so two Dwarves and one Axe is enough to turn the story on permanently. Once it is on, four cheap Dwarves become finishers: Fíli the Pathfinder gives every creature +1/+1 and mints a 2/2 Dwarf token on each nontoken Dwarf entry, Thorin Oakenshield gives your whole board ward {1}, Dáin, Lord of the Iron Hills taxes every attacker for {1}, Kíli the Resourceful makes the first equip of each turn free, and Óin the Brave gains haste.

The kill comes from volume plus Equipment. Dáin Ironfoot makes an Axe on entry and grants double strike to every equipped attacker; Dwalin, Weaponmaster puts a hone counter on each Equipment whenever he enters or attacks, and each counter is +1/+0 to the equipped creature; The Black Arrow is a three-mana flash Equipment with equip {1} that also pings for 1 on entry. An Unexpected Party named Dwarf adds +2/+2 on top. Five or six Dwarves with two Axes and an anthem is 20-plus damage no single blocker can stop.

The second line is a burn kill. Balin, Loremaster, with the story active, makes every Dwarf entry drain each opponent for your hand size, and Bifur, Melodic Rider doubles every Dwarf triggered ability — including Fíli’s tokens, Kíli’s draw, and the Axe-making of Dáin Ironfoot and Iron Hills Blacksmith. Bifur plus Balin and two more Dwarves is often lethal without a single attack step.

The Hobbit set (hob, 193 cards) landed on Arena on 2026-08-14; the import list below works from that date.

Curve and mana

24 lands: 4 The Lonely Mountain, 4 Mystic Monastery, 2 Temple of Epiphany, 9 Plains, 5 Mountain. White sources 13, red sources 15, blue sources 6. Six of the lands enter tapped, which is why the deck wants nothing cheaper than two mana: turn one is a tapped dual or a basic, and every real play starts on turn two.

Nonland curve: 2-drop x22, 3-drop x6, 4-drop x5, 5-drop x2, 6-drop x1. Average nonland CMC 2.72. The deck is nineteen-percent six-plus drops by design — the swarm is cheap, the payoffs are legendaries, and Dwarven Provisioner turns surplus mana into +1/+1.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

The deck has no one-drop, so turn one is a land and nothing else. Turn two is the identity decision. Iron Hills Blacksmith is the best aggressive start: a 1/1 double strike plus an Axe token gives you two of the three Storied requirements before turn three. Thorin Oakenshield is the best defensive start: 3/2 trample blocks well and makes the board expensive to attack into once the story turns on.

Sequence Kíli the Resourceful after another Dwarf when possible — his draw fires on the first Dwarf or Equipment that enters each turn, so leading with him wastes the trigger on himself. Óin the Brave and Dáin, Lord of the Iron Hills exist to turn the story on early and to make the opponent’s attack step awkward.

Mid game

Turn three to five is where the deck separates. Fíli the Pathfinder converts every subsequent nontoken Dwarf into two permanents — the Dwarf plus a 2/2 red token — and once the story is active, both get +1/+1 from his static ability. Play the cheap Dwarf first and Fíli second: that way Kíli’s once-per-turn draw and Fíli’s token come from the same turn.

The Axe count matters more than it looks. Every Equipment is a Storied requirement, a Dwalin hone counter target, a discount on The Lonely Mountain’s token ability, and an extra double-striking attacker once Dáin Ironfoot is on the board. Iron Hills Stalwart is in the list mainly to move an Axe from a dead Dwarf onto a live one.

The combo turn

  1. Story check. Three or more artifacts, legendaries, and/or Sagas: typically Fíli, Kíli or Óin, and an Axe or The Black Arrow. Once achieved, it lasts the rest of the game.
  2. Assemble Equipment. Dáin Ironfoot or Iron Hills Blacksmith makes an Axe; The Black Arrow and Dwarven Provisioner fill the curve. Kíli makes the first equip free.
  3. Anthem up. An Unexpected Party naming Dwarf gives every Dwarf +2/+2; Dwarven Provisioner’s activated ability adds another +1/+1 for four mana.
  4. Attack with Dáin Ironfoot. Each equipped attacking creature gains double strike until end of turn. A 2/2 token with an Axe and +2/+2 is a 5/5 double strike, so ten damage per token that connects.

A typical kill from an empty board: Fíli the Pathfinder, two Dwarves, and Dáin Ironfoot over two turns is four bodies; add two Axes and An Unexpected Party and the attack step is 20-plus damage across double-striking attackers.

Win condition 2

Balin, Loremaster is a burn spell disguised as a 4/4. Whenever Balin or another Dwarf enters, you may discard your hand, draw that many cards, and, if you have an enduring story, Balin deals that much damage to each opponent. With five cards in hand, that is five damage to each opponent and a fresh five-card hand. Bifur, Melodic Rider makes every Dwarf triggered ability trigger an additional time, and the second drain resolves after the first redraw, so it drains for the new hand size too. Balin plus Bifur plus two cheap Dwarves is often ten to sixteen points without an attack step, and each entry refills your hand, so the loop does not run out of cards.

The land backs this up. The Lonely Mountain needs four red minus one per Equipment, so with three Equipment out the token costs {1}{R}, and the token is a Dwarf: it triggers Fíli and Balin and becomes another drain trigger the following turn. That is the slow, inevitable half of the deck.

Mulligan guide

Keep two to four lands with at least two Dwarves. Because the deck has twenty-two two-drops, almost any two lands plus any two Dwarves is keepable; the ideal opener is a white source, a red source, Iron Hills Blacksmith or Thorin Oakenshield, and a second Dwarf for turn three. Mulligan hands with fewer than two lands, hands with six or more lands and no Dwarf, and hands whose only blue source is Bilbo Baggins — the splash is a bonus, never a plan. With six tapped lands in the mana base, treat a hand of one tapped dual plus one basic as a functional two-land hand only if it casts a two-drop on turn two.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Storied is a state check on permanents you control, not a spell count: Equipment tokens, legendary creatures, and Sagas all count, and an enduring story persists for the rest of the game even if you lose the board. An Unexpected Party is an enchantment — it does not help turn the story on.
  • Kíli the Resourceful draws only once each turn, and his free-equip clause applies only to the first equip ability you activate each turn. Play the permanent you want to draw off first.
  • Equip is sorcery speed, and Dáin Ironfoot’s double strike trigger happens when he attacks, so attach every Axe and The Black Arrow before combat — re-equipping mid-combat does not retroactively grant double strike.
  • Dwalin, Weaponmaster’s hone counters sit on the Equipment, not the creature, so the bonus moves with the Axe if you re-equip it.
  • Balin, Loremaster’s discard is optional and resolves as one effect: you discard the hand you have, then draw that many. Discarding a land-heavy hand is a deliberate gamble on the redraw.
  • Bifur, Melodic Rider doubles triggered abilities of Dwarves only — Fíli’s token, Kíli’s draw, Dáin Ironfoot’s Axe, Balin’s drain, and his own +1/+1 counter trigger, but not Fíli’s static anthem or Thorin’s ward.
  • The Black Arrow has flash and equip {1}: cast it on the opponent’s turn to ping a one-toughness attacker or a key utility creature, and its anti-Dragon clause is a clean answer to Smaug decks.
  • The Lonely Mountain enters tapped unless you control an Equipment, so keep an Axe or The Black Arrow around before playing it if you want the untapped red source on curve; Iron Hills Stalwart’s enter trigger can move an Equipment to blank a removal spell or re-attach after a wipe.
  • Óin the Brave’s loot costs one mana and taps him, and his discard is a real cost when a Balin drain is coming — loot before casting Dwarves, not after.
