Overview

Death by Committee fuses the two rolled mechanics — Sacrifice and Teamwork — into one engine: the committee is the sacrifice fodder. Forsaken Miner, Reassembling Skeleton and Bloodghast refuse to stay dead, so every turn the deck has a fresh set of bodies: tap them to pay Teamwork costs, sacrifice them to draw cards and exile threats, and let their death triggers refill the hand. The Teamwork payoff spells from the Spider-Man set turn that rotating cast of characters into removal, burn and reanimation that does double duty.

The engine loop: a skeleton dies to Fanatical Offering (draw two) or Worthy Cost (exile a threat), the death triggers draw more cards (Midnight Reaper) or make Food (Restless Cottage attacks), the skeleton recurs (crime triggers, {1}{B}, landfall), and the rebuilt board — plus 4/4 Restless Cottage manlands — taps for Teamwork 2 or 4 to cast Cruel Alliance, Repulsor Blast or HULK SMASH! in their doubled modes. The graveyard fills as a side effect, and Too Evil to Stay Dead turns it into a reanimation spell: with Teamwork paid it resurrects any creature card, no mana-value limit.

The finish line is three-pronged. Rune-Scarred Demon and Bloodthirsty Conqueror come back as flying beaters and tutors. Conqueror plus Enduring Tenacity plus any Food sacrifice is a true infinite loop — each life gain drains the opponent, each drain gains life. And the lands themselves kill: Restless Cottage is a 4/4 Horror that makes Food on attack, and Rogue’s Passage makes it unblockable, with Team Tactics doubling the damage.

Approach

Game plan by phase

  1. Dig the graves (turns 1–3). Turn 1 Forsaken Miner or Worthy Cost (sacrificing a skeleton); turn 2 Reassembling Skeleton, Bloodghast or Fanatical Offering. Every removal spell is a “crime”, which recurs Forsaken Miner for {B} — the first two skeletons become an endless stream.
  2. Hold the committee meeting (turns 3–5). Corpseberry Cultivator grows on every forage; Midnight Reaper converts each death into a card; Restless Cottage wakes up as a 4/4 to tap for Teamwork 4 and attack for Food. Cast Too Evil to Stay Dead, Cruel Alliance, HULK SMASH! or Repulsor Blast with their doubled modes.
  3. The vote passes (turns 5+). Too Evil to Stay Dead (Teamwork) reanimates the big two: Rune-Scarred Demon tutors the missing combo piece, Bloodthirsty Conqueror starts the drain. Close with the infinite Food loop, or send an unblockable, double-striking Restless Cottage over the top.

Curve and mana

Seventeen creatures, seventeen spells, twenty-six lands. The nonland curve averages about 2.4 mana: nine one- and two-drop recursion bodies, eight three-drops, four four-drops and three reanimation-only finishers (mana value 5 to 7, almost never hard-cast). Black is the spine (roughly twenty sources), green supports it (seventeen), and red is a splash of six cards (five to six sources). The deck is soft to mana flood — Fountainport and Fabled Passage convert spare lands into cards and recursion triggers.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Prioritize a black source and a one-drop: Forsaken Miner on turn 1 threatens a free 2/2 every time you later cast removal, which is a crime. Reassembling Skeleton on turn 2 blocks the format’s early attackers and sets up turn 3 Fanatical Offering. Do not hold skeletons — they are meant to die; the whole deck is built around them coming back. Sequence the untapped fast lands early and the tapped duals (Mortuary, Commercial District, Overgrown Tomb) later.

Mid game

Start sacrificing on your own terms. Fanatical Offering turns any skeleton into two cards and a Map token; Worthy Cost answers a planeswalker or creature for one mana plus a body that was going to die anyway. Every nontoken death triggers Midnight Reaper, so a single Reassembling Skeleton recursion loop is a repeatable draw spell. Corpseberry Cultivator forages at every combat — exile three graveyard cards (or sacrifice a Food from a Cottage attack) to grow it into a credible Teamwork contributor.

The combo turn

The board is a committee of 2-power skeletons and a Restless Cottage. Tap total power 4 (two 2/2 Miners, or Miner + Bloodghast, or the 4/4 Cottage) to pay Teamwork on Too Evil to Stay Dead and reanimate two of Bloodthirsty Conqueror, Rune-Scarred Demon and Enduring Tenacity from the graveyard you have been filling all game. Demon tutors the missing piece; then the drain loop closes:

  1. Attack with Restless Cottage — it creates a Food token.
  2. Sacrifice the Food: gain 3 life.
  3. Enduring Tenacity: target opponent loses 3 life.
  4. Bloodthirsty Conqueror: you gain 3 life — loop back to step 3. The opponent dies.

The loop needs only Conqueror + Tenacity + any lifegain, and both halves are reanimation targets, so the graveyard is the hand. If the loop is disrupted, the reanimated pair still attacks for 10-11 flying damage with the Demon’s tutor finding whatever is missing.

Win condition 2

The lands kill. Activate Restless Cottage ({2}{B}{G}) into a 4/4, cast Team Tactics tapping any creature (Teamwork 1 is nearly free) for double strike and trample, then activate Rogue’s Passage ({4}, {T}) — 8 unblockable damage from a land. In long games the Cottage also grinds: attack, make Food, exile a graveyard card (shutting down opposing reanimation), then sacrifice the Food to grow Corpseberry Cultivator or to feed the drain loop.

Mulligan guide

Keep any hand with two or three lands and a one- or two-drop — the deck functions on skeletons alone. Ship hands with no black source or with only tapped lands and no early play. Keep Too Evil to Stay Dead plus a sacrifice spell plus a body; the reanimation plan only needs a graveyard, which sacrifice provides. Against aggressive decks, favor removal (Worthy Cost, HULK SMASH!) over the Teamwork spells.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Crime recursion: Worthy Cost, Cruel Alliance, Repulsor Blast and HULK SMASH!’s fight mode all target opponents or their permanents, so each one recurs Forsaken Miner for {B} — sequence removal before combat to rebuild the board for Teamwork.
  • Teamwork is optional: every Teamwork spell can be cast without tapping — a doubled mode is a bonus, not a requirement; tap only when the payoff is worth the tapped creatures.
  • Enduring Tenacity returns as an enchantment when it dies as a creature — it stops being sacrifice fodder but keeps draining, so the combo survives a board wipe.
  • Too Evil to Stay Dead without Teamwork is still a two-for-one enabler at mana value 4 or less — Solemn Simulacrum is the ideal cheap target (ramps, then draws on death).
  • Fountainport tokens count: a 1/1 Fish is a creature for Teamwork 1 and a sacrifice for Fanatical Offering, and sacrificing it draws a card.
  • Don’t over-sacrifice: the team is also the Teamwork payment; keep at least one body untapped to pay Teamwork 2/4 next turn.

Matchups

  • Aggro: the skeletons block forever and Worthy Cost / HULK SMASH! / Cruel Alliance clear the path; Conqueror’s lifegain ends the race.
  • Midrange: grind them out — every sacrifice replaces itself with cards (Fanatical Offering, Midnight Reaper) while Too Evil reanimates bigger creatures than they can answer.
  • Control: spread threats (manlands, Fountainport Fish) so sweepers never fully reset; the graveyard is a second hand, so wraths that don’t exile are meaningless. Rogue’s Passage closes through blockers.
  • Graveyard hate: the deck still plays a fair midrange game — skeletons and removal — but mulligan aggressively for Cottage plus Team Tactics to win on the land plan instead.
