Overview

Reality Fracture’s prepared creatures are spells wearing a body. Grave Researcher is the cheapest engine in the set: at the beginning of every upkeep it surveils 1, and if there are three or more creature cards in your graveyard it becomes prepared. While prepared, you may cast a copy of its back face, Reanimate — a {B} sorcery that puts any creature card from any graveyard onto the battlefield. Casting it unprepares the Researcher, and the next upkeep begins the loop again.

That is the whole deck: fill the bin, turn the Researcher on, and reanimate every turn for one black mana. The fill is cheap and repeatable — Void Extrapolator enters prepared and hands you a free Omit Variables (mill three), Patient Naturalist mills three and returns a land, Lasyd Prowler mills for each land you control, and Restless Reef mills four every time it attacks. By turn four the graveyard is a resource, not a loss.

The reanimation targets are chosen for what they do after they arrive. Vampire Soulcaller comes back and returns another creature card from the graveyard to hand, so one Reanimate becomes two threats. Proft, Sinister Mastermind is a 5/5 with menace for three mana, but only castable at threshold — which is exactly where this deck lives. Null Summoner exiles a card from the opponent’s hand on entry and, at threshold, lets you cast it with mana of any type. Winter, Tormented Loner makes each opponent sacrifice a creature and grows with every creature card in your graveyard.

Nothing here needs a specific pair to function. Every surveil makes the next reanimation better, and every creature in the bin is another option for the Researcher.

Curve and mana

Twenty-four lands, sixteen of them green sources and twelve blue, with fifteen black. The curve tops out at five:

Cost Count Cards
2 9 Void Extrapolator, Fblthp, Servant of the Stinger
3 14 Grave Researcher, Winter, Proft, Patient Naturalist
4 5 Null Summoner, Hour of Defeat
5 8 Vampire Soulcaller, Defossilize, Lasyd Prowler

The opening hand wants two lands and a mill effect. Double-black is the only real constraint (Proft, Null Summoner, Defossilize); the deck never needs double green before turn four, when Lasyd Prowler asks for it.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Lead with the cheapest mill. Void Extrapolator on turn two, cast the free Omit Variables, and the bin already has three cards. Servant of the Stinger is the other cheap early play: a 1/3 deathtouch that blocks everything relevant and commits a crime whenever it is aimed at an opponent, which turns on its own tutor trigger for a card.

Do not trade the Researcher for a blocker. It is the engine; it survives behind a deathtouch body or a Winter edict.

Mid game and the combo turn

The combo turn is any upkeep from turn four onward:

  1. Upkeep: Grave Researcher surveils 1. If the surveilled card is a creature, the bin count rises; if not, it goes to the graveyard anyway as a threshold card.
  2. If there are three or more creature cards in the graveyard, the Researcher becomes prepared.
  3. Cast the copy of Reanimate for {B}. Choose the best creature in any graveyard.
  4. The Researcher is unprepared and the board has a new threat. Next upkeep, repeat.

Sequencing matters: cast sorcery-speed mill (Patient Naturalist, Lasyd Prowler) before the upkeep trigger resolves when you can, and hold the free Reanimate for the turn you can also attack with the returned creature.

Win condition two

Fblthp, Impossibly Lost is a two-mana 1/1 that draws two cards whenever an opponent is dealt combat damage, and wins the game outright if your library is empty at that moment. Between Restless Reef, Patient Naturalist, Lasyd Prowler and a surveil every upkeep, the library empties in long games. Fblthp shuffles itself back in, so the deck never decks itself out of a win: it is a second, independent axis that costs two slots.

Key interactions

  • Threshold is a switch, not a bonus. Proft cannot be cast with six cards in the graveyard. Surveil and mill are how the deck turns it on; do not exile your own graveyard for value.
  • Reanimate is not restricted to your graveyard. Against a deck that has already traded creatures, the Researcher can take their best creature. That is often better than anything in your own bin.
  • Void Extrapolator’s threshold bonus makes it a 3/3 at seven cards, and its free Omit Variables is a mill three every time a fresh copy enters prepared.
  • Winter wants a sacrifice. Cast it with a body you do not need, or with a creature that already attacked.
