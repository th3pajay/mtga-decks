Overview

Prep Rally is built entirely around Reality Fracture’s flagship mechanic. A creature that is prepared lets you cast a copy of its spell once; doing so unprepares it. Every nonland card here is either a prepared creature with a spell worth casting, a card that prepares a creature again, or a payoff that turns those free spells into damage.

The engine is the small re-prepare loop. Stingerquill Voxmancer and Paradox Shaper prepare themselves during your upkeep, so each turn they hand you a free spell: Vicious Verse for 1 damage to the opponent, or Omit Variables to mill three. Skycoach Waypoint (3 generic, tap: target creature becomes prepared) and Hexhaven Dueling Arena (2 generic, tap: target creature that attacked this turn becomes prepared) extend that loop to every other prepared creature in the deck, including the bigger bodies such as Variable Chaser and Pyre Rhymer. Codie, Ravenous Codex then copies each prepared spell you cast, which doubles the per-turn payload for as long as it lives.

The payoffs convert that trickle into a clock. Tomik, Izzet Sparkmage raises each noncombat damage instance by 1, so a single Vicious Verse copy hits for 2 (4 with Codie out). Master of Barbs turns any noncombat damage into a team-wide +1/+0, and Whiplash Wordsmith gains flying and haste on the same condition, so the drain and the beatdown plan feed each other.

Approach

Plan by phases:

  1. Turns 1 to 2: deploy two prepared bodies (Diviner of Victory, Stingerquill Voxmancer, Pompous Battlemage) and start collecting free spells. Diviner of Victory grows on every scry or surveil, so Thundering Falls, Unwind History and Peer Review all make it bigger.
  2. Turns 3 to 4: land the payoffs. Tomik, Izzet Sparkmage and Master of Barbs convert every free spell into either extra damage or a team pump. This is also when the deck starts winning combat steps with flying prowess creatures.
  3. Turns 4+: settle into the re-prepare loop with Skycoach Waypoint and Hexhaven Dueling Arena, keep drawing with Improvised Act, Hallway Heckler’s tap ability and Cruel Calculations, and let the drain plus evasive damage close the game.

Twenty-four lands with thirteen blue, fourteen black and twelve red sources. Four copies of Watery Grave and Blood Crypt cover the black-heavy early turns, and the six Verge lands are untapped sources of their first colour from turn one, so the deck is not forced to pay life to stay on curve. Molten Tide on the stack turns the seven Mountain-typed lands into extra red mana for a big turn.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Lead on cheap prepared creatures rather than pure attackers. Diviner of Victory and Stingerquill Voxmancer both cost one mana and both hand you a spell: Unwind History clears a small blocker and surveils, Vicious Verse starts the drain. Pompous Battlemage is a prowess body that comes with Improvised Act attached, which filters a land into a real card. Trading early is fine: Bloodline Recollector re-prepares itself at the end step whenever three or more creatures died that turn.

Mid game

Turn three and four are about payoffs, not bodies. Tomik, Izzet Sparkmage and Master of Barbs turn every free spell into progress, and Variable Chaser plus Pyre Rhymer give the deck a real clock while it grinds. Paradox Shaper’s upkeep Omit Variables quietly fills the graveyard, which makes Cruel Calculations a two-mana draw spell that scales with the number of mills you stacked that turn. Once Skycoach Waypoint or Hexhaven Dueling Arena is untapped, every creature with a prepare spell becomes a repeatable resource, and Semester Foreseer and Prudent Fateseer add Cadets to the board while doing it.

The combo turn

The deck’s best turn follows a fixed shape: start with one or more creatures left prepared from the previous upkeep, cast the cheap prepared spells first (Vicious Verse, Improvised Act) to trigger prowess and to switch Whiplash Wordsmith to flying and haste, then attack, then activate Hexhaven Dueling Arena in the second main phase to re-prepare the attacker you want next turn. With Codie out every prepared spell happens twice, so a board of four or five prepared creatures represents eight to ten spell effects, most of them damage. Tomik turns each Vicious Verse copy into 2 damage and each Ancestral Craving into 4, and Master of Barbs converts the whole sequence into a lethal attack step.

Win condition two

The second win is plain evasive damage. Variable Chaser is a 2/3 flier with prowess, Pyre Rhymer is a 3/3 with prowess, Whiplash Wordsmith is a 3/3 that gains flying and haste after any noncombat damage, and Peer Review supplies 2/2 Cadets. Master of Barbs stacks +1/+0 on the team for every turn you dealt noncombat damage, so the drain plan actively builds the combat plan. Codie’s second ability (one of each colour for each creature you control to become prepared) is a late-game dream this manabase cannot actually pay, so treat the copy clause as its real job.

Mulligan guide

Keep hands with one or two cheap prepared creatures plus a re-prepare engine or a payoff. Two or three lands with at least one black and one red source is the sweet spot. Six-card hands are fine when they contain Stingerquill Voxmancer plus any payoff; the deck snowballs from small boards. Ship hands that are all four-drops, hands with only colourless lands, and hands with no way to prepare anything past the first spell.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Casting a prepared spell unprepares that creature, so time the copies. If Skycoach Waypoint is untapped, casting a copy this turn costs you nothing.
  • Prepared spells are real spells, so they trigger prowess on Pompous Battlemage, Variable Chaser, Pyre Rhymer and Tomik, and Codie copies them. Cast the cheap ones first.
  • Prudent Fateseer’s pump triggers only once each turn, so surveil before combat to make the attack step count.
  • Diviner of Victory grows on scry or surveil only. Omit Variables is a mill, not a surveil, but Unwind History, Peer Review and Thundering Falls all trigger it.
  • Cruel Calculations counts cards put into a graveyard from a library this turn, so cast it after Omit Variables (and after Codie has copied it).
  • Ancestral Craving is a drain: aim it at the opponent, who draws three and loses 3 life. With Tomik that is 4. With Codie, twice.
  • Bloodline Recollector only re-prepares when three or more creatures died in the turn, so use it after combat trades or a board wipe.

Matchups

The deck races rather than answers. Against aggro it wins the damage race with drain plus fliers, but it has almost no removal, so Diviner of Victory’s Unwind History and good blocks are the only interaction. Against grindy midrange it out-values most opponents: each upkeep re-prepares a creature and Codie doubles the payoff. Against combo it must be the aggressor, converting Vicious Verse copies and Whiplash Wordsmith’s haste into a fast clock. Against control it leans on Codie’s copy clause, Bloodline Recollector’s free card advantage and Arc of Fortune to rebuild after a wipe.
