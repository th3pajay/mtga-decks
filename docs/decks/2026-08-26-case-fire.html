Overview

Case Fire fuses three mechanics into one engine: Raid (if you attacked this turn), Solved (MKM Cases), and Evoke (Lorwyn Eclipsed Incarnations). The loop: attack every turn to turn on Raid payoffs, those attacks and the burn around them solve Cases, and solved Cases generate tokens, cards, and tutors that make the next attack bigger. Evoke Incarnations are the glue — 2-mana combat tricks and disruption that sacrifice themselves on entry, feeding Case of the Gorgon’s Kiss (three creature cards in graveyards) and emptying your hand for Case of the Crimson Pulse.

The four flagship Cases each convert a raid attack into a resource:

  • Case of the Burning Masks — three damage sources in a turn solves it (Boltwave, Warleader’s Call pings, combat damage); solved, it exiles the top three cards and lets you play one, chaining spells off the top.
  • Case of the Stashed Skeleton — a suspected 2/1 that attacks; once it dies you control no suspected Skeletons and it solves into a one-shot tutor for any card.
  • Case of the Crimson Pulse — loot on entry; when the aggro curve empties your hand it solves into a repeatable “discard hand, draw two” upkeep engine.
  • Case of the Shattered Pact — fetches a basic on entry and solves on five colors among permanents (Mirage Mesa naming green plus the hybrid Incarnations); solved, it grants flying, double strike, and vigilance to a creature every combat.

Evoke completes the circuit: Catharsis evoked for {R}{R} is a two-mana +1/+1 haste anthem for the whole team before dying; Deceit evoked for {B}{B} is a two-mana Thoughtseize; Emptiness evoked for {W}{W} reanimates a 3-drop. Every Incarnation that dies puts a creature card into your graveyard, feeding Gorgon’s Kiss.

Curve and mana

23 lands: 4 Raucous Theater, 2 Restless Bivouac, 2 Restless Spire, 2 Mirage Mesa, 1 Mystic Monastery, 1 Nomad Outpost, 1 Sun-Blessed Peak, 1 Fire Nation Palace, 1 Rogue’s Passage, 4 Mountain, 2 Swamp, 1 Island, 1 Plains. Red sources 16, black 7, white 6, blue 4; Mirage Mesa covers any missing color. Blue is a light splash (Filched Falcon, Deceit’s {U}{U} mode, Restless Spire), so sequence untapped basics and surveil lands first.

Effective curve (evoke costs count as the card’s cost): 1-drop x7, 2-drop x15, 3-drop x11, 4-drop x4. Average CMC 2.32. The deck wants red and black early for Searslicer Goblin and the black Cases, then white for Warleader’s Call and Catharsis from turn 3 on.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Lead with Case of the Filched Falcon, Case of the Stashed Skeleton, or Case of the Gorgon’s Kiss on turn one, Searslicer Goblin on turn two. The suspected 2/1 Skeleton attacks freely (menace usually makes it unblocked or trades up) and dying is part of the plan: it solves its own Case and puts a creature card in the graveyard for Gorgon’s Kiss. Hold Torch the Tower to bargain the Skeleton away if the opponent refuses to block.

Mid game

Attack every turn — Raid is the clock. Order matters: play raid creatures in the second main phase so they enter with raid bonuses, and hold the evoke Incarnations for the combat step. Catharsis for {R}{R} after blockers are declared turns a chump-blockable board into a haste-anthem swing. The raid package then compounds: Searslicer makes a Goblin, Cruel Administrator makes a Soldier with firebending 1, Perforating Artist drains 3, Warleader’s Call pings — all damage sources that solve Burning Masks.

The combo turn

  1. Have Case of the Burning Masks in play and at least two other damage sources online (Warleader’s Call counts, combat damage counts, Boltwave is one).
  2. Attack, then cast Boltwave or evoke Catharsis for {R}{R} — three sources have dealt damage this turn, Burning Masks solves at end step.
  3. Sacrifice it: exile the top three, play the best spell for free this turn — usually a second Catharsis, another raid creature, or Warleader’s Call.
  4. Sacrifice the suspected Skeleton to Torch the Tower (or trade it in combat): Stashed Skeleton solves, then tutors the missing piece — Shattered Pact, Deceit, or a third Burning Masks.
  5. Evoke Deceit for {B}{B} to strip the opponent’s answer, Emptiness for {W}{W} to bring back a dead Searslicer or Perforating Artist, and swing with the full army under Catharsis haste.

A typical kill: five lands, Warleader’s Call, Burning Masks solved. Attack with three creatures plus a Restless Bivouac; the raid drain and pings total 6-8, Boltwave adds 3, and the exiled spell from Burning Masks is a second Catharsis making the board swing for 15+.

Win condition 2

Solve Case of the Shattered Pact: the colors on your permanents are white (Bivouac, Warleader’s Call), red (Theater, Spire, Palace), black (Theater, Emptiness, Gorgon’s Kiss), blue (Spire, Filched Falcon), and green (a Mirage Mesa named green, or Catharsis’s Kithkin tokens). Once solved, every combat give the biggest creature flying, double strike, and vigilance — then Rogue’s Passage makes it unblockable. Deceit full-cast is a 5/5 (10 damage double-struck); Cruel Administrator is 6/5 with its raid counter; the 4/4 deathtouch lifelink Gorgon’s Kiss also works. Case of the Filched Falcon is the backup: solve it with three artifacts (Clues from Fire Nation Raider, Falcon, Fire Nation Warship), then {2}{U} to turn a Clue into a 4/4 flying Bird — repeatable per Case, and the Birds are excellent Raid enablers themselves.

Mulligan guide

Keep: two to three lands with a one- or two-drop Case plus a raid creature; hands with Searslicer Goblin or Filched Falcon openers are strong, and Catharsis plus any red source is a snap keep — the evoke anthem wins races. Mulligan one-land hands and hands with four or more lands and nothing to do. Blue is needed only for Falcon and Deceit’s {U}{U} mode, so a hand with only red, black, and white is keepable; a hand without red or black is not.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Evoke sacrifices the creature when it enters — the ETB trigger and the sacrifice trigger both resolve, and you order them. Catharsis’s anthem and haste persist until end of turn even after the body dies.
  • The Incarnations’ bonus modes check what was actually spent to cast the spell, not the evoke cost: Catharsis evoked with one {R} and one {W} gets neither bonus. Pay {R}{R}, {B}{B}, {U}{U}, or {W}{W} deliberately.
  • Case of the Stashed Skeleton solves only when you control no suspected Skeletons — the initial token must die first. Torch the Tower’s bargain sacrifice is the cleanest way; sacrificing other tokens to bargain does not help solve it.
  • Case of the Gorgon’s Kiss counts creature cards put into graveyards from anywhere this turn — evoke deaths, combat deaths, and Case of the Crimson Pulse’s discard all count; tokens do not.
  • Case of the Burning Masks needs three different sources you controlled to deal damage — one Boltwave is one source, Warleader’s Call pings are one source (no matter how many times it pings), and each attacking creature is its own source.
  • Case of the Crimson Pulse solves at the beginning of your end step if your hand is empty — cast or evoke everything in the second main phase, then loot into a fresh grip on the next upkeep.
  • Suspected creatures have menace and can’t block — the Skeleton attacks but never blocks; do not hold it back expecting defense.
  • Firebending mana from Cruel Administrator’s Soldiers and Fire Nation Palace lasts until end of combat — spend it on instants like Torch the Tower or on the Burning Masks exile chain during or after combat.
  • Shattered Pact’s solved trigger targets at the beginning of combat each turn — combine with Rogue’s Passage in the same combat step for the unblockable double-strike kill.
