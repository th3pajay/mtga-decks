Size 60 cards, 24 lands, 36 spells
Primary win Draconic Visitor replaces each artifact token you would create with a 5/5 red Dragon with flying. The Soul Tether cycle makes those tokens every turn, so the board fills with Dragons
Secondary win Craterclaw Colossus with a Heartwood pile, copied by Hall of Echoes, attacks for trample damage scaled by your artifact count
Land as engine Hall of Echoes copies Craterclaw Colossus or Kwia Vigorbloom for a second enters-trigger pump or a second 6/6 flier, and the legend rule does not apply that turn

Overview

Reality Fracture’s Heartwood cycle is a set of red-green double-faced cards whose back face is Soul Tether ({2}{R/G} sorcery), a spell that creates a Heartwood token: a red and green artifact with {T}: Add {R} or {G}. On its own that is a three-mana mana rock with extra steps. Draconic Visitor ({3}{R}{R}, 5/5 Dragon, flying) changes the maths completely: “If one or more artifact tokens would be created under your control, that many 5/5 red Dragon creature tokens with flying are created instead.” Every Soul Tether you cast is now a 5/5 flier, and every other artifact-token maker in the deck is a Dragon too.

The prepared mechanic is what makes the engine repeatable. Heartwood Crafter ({G}, 1/1) enters prepared, so it is a one-mana body holding a free Soul Tether. Konstrari Improviser ({1}{R/G}, 2/2) also enters prepared. Woodwork Prodigy ({2}{R/G}, 3/3) re-arms itself at the beginning of your upkeep if it is not prepared, which is a fresh Soul Tether — and therefore a fresh Dragon — every single turn for as long as it lives.

The rest of the deck is redundancy for the conversion and for the mana. Pia, Tenured Tethermage, Hungering Puppetbeast and Aerid Konstrari all create artifact tokens on entry, so with Draconic Visitor on the battlefield each of them is a creature plus at least one 5/5 flier. Kwia Vigorbloom turns lifegain into a Lotus artifact token once per turn, so an attacking Vigorbloom — or a single Seed Suture — is another Dragon. Codie, Ravenous Codex copies each prepared spell you cast, which doubles a Soul Tether into two Dragons.

Approach

Game plan phases

Turns 1–3, build the shell. Deploy prepared bodies and the Annex lands. Heartwood Crafter on one, Konstrari Improviser or Vigorbloom Vanguard on two, Woodwork Prodigy, Pia or Tenured Tethermage on three. Casting the Soul Tether copy early is fine — the Heartwood pays for the next spell, and it is the card you most want to convert later.

Turns 4–5, land the converter. Draconic Visitor is the pivot. Once it resolves, every pending and future token becomes a 5/5 flier. Do not tap out for a Dragon-less board if the opponent is holding removal: Visitor plus one open Soul Tether is a two-for-one in your favour even if the Visitor dies.

Turns 5+, convert and swing. Each upkeep Woodwork Prodigy re-arms. Each attack step Hexhaven Dueling Arena can arm an attacker for {2}. Codie doubles the first prepared spell of each turn. Dragons attack in the air while Craterclaw Colossus or Kwia Vigorbloom provide a ground finisher.

Average mana value 3.31 across 36 spells on 24 lands, which is the 40 percent land count that makes four land drops by turn four a better than even proposition. Green is the deepest colour (sixteen sources) because every Soul Tether body and every Heartwood maker wants it; red sits at nine sources for Draconic Visitor, Pia and the Charms; white at nine for Kwia Vigorbloom, Vigorbloom Vanguard and Blossom-Blessed Angel. Hybrid costs in the cycle mean Konstrari Improviser and Woodwork Prodigy can be paid with either colour, which absorbs most colour-screw hands.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Lead on a prepared body, not on the Annex lands. Heartwood Crafter is the best turn one because it is both a blocker and a stored spell. Turn two, Konstrari Improviser or Vigorbloom Vanguard keeps the pressure while holding the Soul Tether. Tenured Tethermage on turn three is the fastest token burst: sacrifice a land you no longer need, make two tapped Heartwood tokens, and if Draconic Visitor is already down those are two 5/5 fliers for one card.

Mid game and the combo turn

The combo turn is any turn where Draconic Visitor sticks and at least one Soul Tether source is available:

  1. Cast Draconic Visitor ({3}{R}{R}). It is a 5/5 flier on its own.
  2. Cast the Soul Tether copy from any prepared creature. With Visitor out, the Heartwood token is replaced by a 5/5 red Dragon with flying.
  3. If Codie, Ravenous Codex is on the battlefield, that prepared spell is copied, so one Soul Tether yields two Dragons.
  4. At end of turn the Woodwork Prodigy is still a 3/3, and on your next upkeep it re-arms for another Dragon.

Two Soul Tether bodies plus Visitor is ten power of evasive creatures over two turns, all of it from cards that were already on the battlefield.

Win condition 2

When Draconic Visitor is answered, the deck pivots to artifact count. The Heartwood tokens stay artifacts in that case, and Craterclaw Colossus gives the team trample and +X/+0 where X is the number of artifacts you control. Hall of Echoes copies the Colossus, and because the copy enters as a new permanent its enters-trigger fires again, so a five-artifact board becomes two pumps of +5/+0 with trample. That is usually lethal through a ground blocker.

Mulligan guide

Keep hands with two to four lands and at least one one- or two-mana prepared creature. A hand with Draconic Visitor but no token maker is a mulligan unless it also has Woodwork Prodigy. Do not keep hands that need two colours of red for Draconic Visitor on curve without a Mountain or Konstrari Annex, and do not keep hands with two or more six-plus drops. Six-card hands with Heartwood Crafter plus any Soul Tether body are keepable, because the engine is the deck.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Draconic Visitor is a replacement effect, not a copy effect: you never get the Heartwood’s mana if the Visitor is on the battlefield. Decide before the trigger whether you want the rock or the Dragon.
  • Because the replacement happens per token, Tenured Tethermage’s two tokens become two Dragons, and Aerid Konstrari’s enters and dies triggers each become a Dragon.
  • Codie’s second ability costs {W}{U}{B}{R}{G}. This deck has no blue or black sources, so treat Codie as a 1/4 body and a prepared-spell copy engine only.
  • Hungering Puppetbeast’s sacrifice ability and Tenured Tethermage’s counter ability both need artifacts you control. With Visitor out you control no artifact tokens, so those lines are for the games where Visitor is absent.
  • Hexhaven Dueling Arena arms a creature at sorcery speed, so the extra Soul Tether is cast in the postcombat main step.
  • Seed Suture from Vigorbloom Vanguard or Blossom-Blessed Angel gains a life, which turns on Kwia Vigorbloom’s Lotus trigger, which is another Dragon while Visitor is out.
  • Hall of Echoes produces only {C}; it is a win-condition land, not a colour source. Do not count it as one.

Matchups

Aggro. Vigorbloom Vanguard and Heartwood Crafter block early, Seed Suture gains life, and Konstrari Charm kills fliers. The deck wins the race the moment one Dragon lands, because a 5/5 flier races almost everything on the ground.

Midrange. Your threats are evasive and come two-for-one once Visitor is down. Save Awaken the Inferno for the creature that actually blocks your Dragons, and use the landcycling mode when you are short on lands.

Control. Do not run Draconic Visitor into open mana with nothing else to do. Deploy two token makers first, then Visitor, so that a single removal spell does not blank the turn. Codie is the best card here because it converts one resolved prepared spell into two Dragons.

Combo. You have no countermagic. Race with fliers, keep Konstrari Charm for a key flier or for the three colourless to power out a Craterclaw, and sideboard into speed rather than interaction.
