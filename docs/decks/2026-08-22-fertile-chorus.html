Sultai Renew x Convoke combo — mill, pump the choir, and tap it for free spells

Overview

Fertile Chorus fuses the two halves of a single idea: Renew (Tarkir) grows creatures out of the graveyard, and Convoke (Lorwyn Eclipsed and friends) spends creatures to cast spells for almost nothing. The deck mills aggressively, turns its own graveyard into +1/+1 counters and keyword counters, then taps the untapped members of the choir to Convoke out spells that cost 8 mana for two or three.

The engine loop:

  1. Mill. Lasyd Prowler mills one card per land you control; Midnight Tilling mills four and grabs a permanent; Midgar, City of Mako’s adventure Reactor Raid sacrifices a spent creature to draw two (the sacrificed creature lands in the yard); creatures that die in combat join the pile.
  2. Renew. Any creature can be sacrificed from the graveyard for a sorcery-speed pump: Sage of the Fang adds a counter and doubles them, Qarsi Revenant grants flying, lifelink and deathtouch, Champion of Dusan grants trample. Kheru Goldkeeper turns every card that leaves the graveyard (every Renew activation!) into a Treasure token — the deck literally pays for itself.
  3. Convoke. With a board full of creatures, cast Web of Life and Destiny (free creature at the start of every combat), Bloodline Bidding (return every Snake from the graveyard), and Vote Out (destroy a creature) for pocket change.

The deck wins by making one creature enormous and then making everything a copy of it — or by wiping the board and milling the opponent out, then reanimating the Snakes to finish.

Approach

Game plan in three phases

  1. Fill the well (turns 1–4): play two-drops, mill with Lasyd Prowler and Midnight Tilling, trade creatures into the yard, and use Reactor Raid to convert a spent body into two cards. Every card in the graveyard is a future counter.
  2. Grow the choir (turns 4–6): activate Renew abilities at sorcery speed — double counters with Sage of the Fang, add flying and lifelink with Qarsi Revenant, trample with Champion of Dusan — while Kheru Goldkeeper banks Treasures for every activation.
  3. Sing (turn 6+): Convoke out Web of Life and Destiny for a free creature every combat, Bloodline Bidding for a full Snake board, or Singularity Rupture to wipe and mill. Swing with a flying, lifelinking, trampling monster and its Naga Fleshcrafter copies.

Curve and mana

21 lands: 3 Treno (U/B), 3 Mutant Town (G/U), 2 Gongaga (R/G), 2 Omashu City (R/G), 2 Midgar (B), plus 4 Forest, 3 Swamp, 2 Island. Green is the critical early color (twelve green two- and three-drops), so fetch and play green first; black comes second for Qarsi Revenant and Vote Out; blue arrives with Naga Fleshcrafter and the Convoke payoffs. Starting hands want two lands including a green source.

The nonland curve is 4 twos, 12 threes, 12 fours, 6 fives, 1 six, 4 eights. The eights are the point: Convoke discounts them by one per creature tapped, so by turn five or six Web of Life and Destiny costs about {2}{G} and Bloodline Bidding about {B}{B}.

Gameplay strategy

Early game (turns 1–3)

  • Turn 2: Champion of Dusan, Sage of the Fang, or Midnight Tilling (mill four, keep a permanent). Prefer the creature against aggressive decks.
  • Turn 3: Qarsi Revenant (3/2 flying deathtouch lifelink holds the sky) or a second two-drop. Keep the ground stalled with trample bodies.
  • Do not be afraid to trade — every creature that dies is Renew fuel. A dead Champion is worth a trample counter on the survivor.

Mid game (turns 4–6)

  • Attack with Lasyd Prowler once to mill; then use its Renew ({1}{G}, sorcery) to put X +1/+1 counters on a creature, where X is the land count in the yard. By turn six that is usually 3–5 counters.
  • The big sequence: Renew Sage of the Fang ({3}{G}) — put a counter, then double. Repeat with a second Sage in the yard to quadruple. Stack Qarsi Revenant’s flying/lifelink/deathtouch counters and Champion of Dusan’s trample counter on the same creature.
  • Kheru Goldkeeper’s Treasure triggers fire on every Renew activation — spend them on the next activation or on a Convoke spell.
  • Cast Vote Out for removal; Disruptor of Currents at flash speed to bounce a blocker or a problem permanent.

The combo turn

  1. Build the monster: Sage of the Fang doubling, Qarsi Revenant counters, Champion of Dusan trample — one creature with 10+ power, flying, lifelink, deathtouch, trample.
  2. Cast Naga Fleshcrafter (or use its Renew from the yard: {2}{U}, sorcery) — “each other creature you control becomes a copy of that creature until end of turn.” Your 2/2s and 3/3s are now a board of 10/10 flying lifelinkers.
  3. Cast Web of Life and Destiny for {2}{G} or so (Convoke with whatever did not attack). From now on, every combat starts with a free creature from your top five.
  4. Swing with the whole choir. If blocked, Reactor Raid or Tarrian’s Journal turns a dead body into cards, and the Tomb of Aclazotz casts the next creature from the graveyard.

Win condition 2: the rupture

Singularity Rupture ({3}{U}{B}{B}) destroys all creatures and then has any number of target players mill half their library, rounded down. Two casts from a 60-card deck remove 45 cards; with the natural mill from Lasyd Prowler and Midnight Tilling, the opponent’s library dies before their life total. The wipe also dumps your own creatures into the yard — where Bloodline Bidding (Convoke, choose Snake) returns Lasyd Prowler, Naga Fleshcrafter, Constrictor Sage and Omni-Changeling all at once for an immediate board.

Mulligan guide

  • Keep: two-plus lands including green, a two-drop, and either a mill source (Lasyd Prowler, Midnight Tilling) or a Renew creature.
  • Trim: five-plus land hands, all-one-color hands, and hands whose only plays are 5+ mana cards.
  • The deck mulligans well: the graveyard smooths draws, and Midnight Tilling finds whatever the mill reveals.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Renew exiles from the graveyard — it is a sorcery-speed activation, so plan it on your turn; it also shrinks the yard, which matters only for Lasyd Prowler’s Renew count (land cards only — lands stay).
  • Kheru Goldkeeper counts every card that leaves — one Renew activation exiles one card and creates one Treasure; two cards leaving creates two Treasures.
  • Bloodline Bidding’s type choice: Snakes cover Lasyd Prowler, Naga Fleshcrafter and Constrictor Sage; Omni-Changeling is every type, so any choice works.
  • The Tomb of Aclazotz casts creatures from the graveyard with a finality counter — they exile on death instead of returning, so use the Tomb for pressure, not for fuel.
  • Singularity Rupture hits your own board — that is the point: the wipe refills the yard for Bidding.
  • Web of Life and Destiny at combat — the free creature enters at the beginning of combat, so it cannot attack that turn but is untapped Convoke fuel for the next spell.
  • Reactor Raid needs a sacrifice to draw — sacrifice a tapped attacker after combat, or an artifact, to keep the yard stocked.

Matchups

  • Aggro: Mutant Town’s life gain and Qarsi Revenant’s lifelink buy turns; Vote Out and Constrictor Sage slow the swarm; the Tomb’s graveyard casting out-values any topdeck war.
  • Control: the deck’s threats are uncounterable-by-accident (Renew activates from the graveyard, the Tomb casts from the graveyard, manlands-style Town lands dodge sorcery-speed sweepers). Singularity Rupture mills them out even if they clear the board.
  • Graveyard hate: hard exile effects hurt. Plan B is real — the creatures are fine on their own, Omni-Changeling and Naga Fleshcrafter copy anything, and Web of Life does not touch the graveyard at all.
