Overview

Palimpsest is a four-color Standard deck built on the fusion of two mechanics. Discover does the digging: it exiles cards from the library until it finds a nonland card cheap enough, then lets you cast it for free from exile. Flashback does the recycling: it lets the deck cast its cheap spells a second time out of the graveyard, so every card spent early is still a card later.

The two halves meet on one card: Quintorius Kand. Its static ability reads “Whenever you cast a spell from exile, Quintorius Kand deals 2 damage to each opponent and you gain 2 life.” Discover, by rule, casts the found card out of exile, so every discover trigger is two points of damage to the opponent plus two life. Flashback is what keeps the deck long enough to fire those triggers over and over, and Archmage’s Newt turns any graveyard instant or sorcery into a free recast.

The result is a deck that never truly runs out of cards: dead spells are re-cast at flashback cost, discovers replace themselves for free, and the graveyard is a second hand.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Lead on lands and hold interaction. Duel Tactics and Lightning Strike trade one mana for one mana against small attackers; Get Lost answers anything larger. Archmage’s Newt is the best turn-two play because it attacks for two, and its combat damage turns a graveyard Duel Tactics or Bulk Up into a fresh spell. Do not rush an Appraiser into open mana if a cheaper discover source is coming: Etali’s Favor at three mana is the safest engine piece because the aura replaces itself through discover 3.

The combo turn

The deck’s “combo turn” is any turn where two or more discover triggers resolve while Quintorius Kand is on the battlefield.

  1. Land Quintorius Kand first, plus one point of loyalty.
  2. Cast Geological Appraiser: it discovers 3, exiling until a card with mana value three or less appears, and casts it for free from exile. Kand deals 2 to the opponent.
  3. Attack with Caparocti Sunborn, tap two creatures or artifacts, and discover 3 again for another free spell and another 2 damage.
  4. At the end of the turn, use Buried Treasure’s graveyard ability, five mana plus exile, for a discover 5 and a fourth trigger.

Four triggers in a turn is 8 damage to the opponent, 8 life gained, and four free spells. Two such turns usually end the game, because Lightning Strike and Tome Blast add reach on top.

Win condition 2

When the burn route stalls, the deck just attacks. Trumpeting Carnosaur is a 7/6 with trample that already discovered 5 on the way in, and Etali’s Favor gives any creature plus one plus one and trample. Bulk Up doubles a creature’s power for one mana, so a Carnosaur that connects through Rogue’s Passage hits for fourteen. Quintorius Kand’s plus one makes a 3/2 Spirit every turn as a second attacker, and the Hidden Cave lands keep the pressure on after the graveyard runs dry.

Mulligan guide

Keep Ship
Two to four lands including at least one red source, plus one engine piece (Appraiser or Etali’s Favor) and one two-mana play Zero or one land, or four-plus lands with no engine and no interaction
One-land hands with only a Duel Tactics, if two of the next three cards are lands Hands with only six-drops and no discover worth casting early

The deck’s low curve means functional five-card and six-card hands often win; flooding is the real loss condition.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Discover casts the found card from exile, so it triggers Quintorius Kand even when the spell was free.
  • Anything the discover whiffs on goes to the bottom of the library, not the graveyard, so discover does not feed flashback. Resentful Revelation and flashback itself are what put cards into the graveyard.
  • Archmage’s Newt’s granted flashback uses the card’s mana cost. Saddling the Newt, three power of other creatures, upgrades the same ability to flashback zero, which makes Duel Tactics a free spell every attack.
  • Bulk Up doubles power after the plus one plus one from Etali’s Favor, so the order matters: pump first, then double.
  • Rogue’s Passage costs four mana to activate, so plan the turn around it if the opponent has a blocker that matters.
  • Buried Treasure’s discover 5 is sorcery speed and needs five mana, but it is one of the few engines that works from the graveyard.
  • Hidden Cave lands enter tapped: they are the slowest cards in the deck, so sacrifice them once the mana is no longer needed.

Midrange The deck wins the long game: flashback gives every spell a second use and Carnosaur outclasses most bodies in combat
Control Do not overextend into a board wipe; keep Lands and Buried Treasure in reserve so the discover plan works from an empty hand
