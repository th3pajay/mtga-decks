Orzhov Endure x Sacrifice — every death makes the choir louder

Overview

Requiem Mass is an Orzhov aristocrats deck built around the fusion of two Tarkir-era mechanics. Endure puts a +1/+1 counter on a creature or creates an N/N white Spirit token whenever it triggers. Sacrifice asks for bodies and pays out in cards, life, and damage. The bridge between the two is death itself: nearly every card in the deck either produces a token to feed an outlet, or cares about a creature dying.

The engine hinges on Anafenza, Unyielding Lineage. Whenever another nontoken creature you control dies, she endures 2 — put two +1/+1 counters on her or create a 2/2 Spirit. That means every sacrifice of a real creature doubles as permanent growth for your best attacker. Bartolomé del Presidio is the free sacrifice outlet that turns those same deaths into counters on itself, Vampiric Rites and Baron Bertram Graywater convert fodder into cards, and Vein Ripper charges 2 life per death from the opponent. Tokens created by endure creatures (Spirits from Fortress Kin-Guard, Descendant of Storms, and Krumar Initiate) are the fuel: they are expendable, so the engine never runs out of bodies.

The deck is a midrange value machine that plays like an aggro deck early — 1/2 and 2/1 endure creatures apply pressure while stockpiling tokens — then turns a mid-game turn with two or three sacrifices into a giant, unblockable threat or a burst of drain.

Approach

Game plan in three phases

  1. Build the choir (turns 1-3). Deploy endure creatures and choose “create Spirit” on their triggers. Descendant of Storms attacks and makes a 1/1 Spirit for {1}{W}. Fortress Kin-Guard endures on entry. Krumar Initiate sits ready as a repeatable spirit factory.
  2. Direct the requiem (turns 3-5). Resolve Anafenza, Unyielding Lineage and a sacrifice outlet. Start sacrificing: nontoken creatures grow Anafenza by +2/+2 (or add a 2/2 Spirit), tokens keep the draw engines fed, and Vein Ripper bleeds the opponent for 2 life per death.
  3. Close the service. Attack with a 10/10-plus Anafenza or Hei Bai, Spirit of Balance through Rogue's Passage, or drain out with repeated sacrifices and Treacherous Greed.

The manabase is 24 lands in exactly White and Black (plus the colorless Rogue's Passage). Fast duals keep the early endure creatures on schedule, while Shadowy Backstreet trades a tempo for a surveil toward the engine pieces.

Curve and mana

Nineteen of the nonland cards cost two or less and only two cost more than three (the Vein Ripper pair), so the deck curves out aggressively and floods the board with bodies before the engine turns on. Bleachbone Verge needs a Plains or Swamp to tap for either color, so the eight basics guarantee it is live from turn one.

Gameplay strategy

Early game (turns 1-3)

Lead with Descendant of Storms or Fortress Kin-Guard. Always take the Spirit token from endure triggers in the first turns — the board presence threatens damage while quietly banking sacrifice fodder. Krumar Initiate is the best turn-two play when you expect a long game: every spare mana becomes an X/X Spirit at sorcery speed, paying life you will win back from Vein Ripper, Baron Bertram Graywater, and Treacherous Greed.

Mid game (turns 4-6)

Land Anafenza, Unyielding Lineage and start the engine. The key sequencing rule: sacrifice nontoken creatures while Anafenza is out (each one makes her +2/+2 or a 2/2 Spirit), and sacrifice tokens when you only need cards or life. Ruthless Lawbringer is removal and an engine in one: it enters, eats a creature, and destroys any nonland permanent — with Anafenza on board the eaten creature grows her at the same time. Social Snub clears a blocker or a hexproof threat from every opponent and gains a life per copy.

The combo turn

With Anafenza, Unyielding Lineage and Bartolomé del Presidio on the battlefield, plus any nontoken creature and a token generator:

  • Sacrifice the nontoken creature to Bartolomé: Bartolomé grows +1/+1, Anafenza endures 2.
  • Repeat with each body: Krumar Initiate can be tapped to make an X/X Spirit first, then sacrificed as a nontoken creature on a later activation, or kept alive to keep generating.
  • Each death also triggers Vein Ripper for 2 damage if he is out.
  • When Anafenza (or Bartolomé, or Hei Bai) has enough counters, activate Rogue's Passage and attack for lethal through any board.

Hei Bai, Spirit of Balance is the second engine that attacks: enter or attack, sacrifice a Spirit token, put two counters on it. A single Hei Bai attacking every turn while churning tokens through the outlet grows by +2/+2 per turn and beats as a 7/7 or larger fly-over blocker-free threat. When it finally leaves, its counters move onto your next attacker, so the growth is never wasted.

Win condition 2: Vein Ripper drain

Vein Ripper turns the whole engine into a life-drain combo. Every creature death — tokens included — makes the opponent lose 2 life and you gain 2. A single engine turn with four or five sacrifices (Spirits from Krumar, an enduring creature, a lawbringer snack) is 8 to 10 damage. The 6/5 flying body with ward-sacrifice also ends games on its own, and Treacherous Greed closes: sacrifice a creature that already dealt combat damage this turn to draw three, drain 3, and gain 3 in one instant.

Mulligan guide

Keep any hand with two or three lands and a two-drop. Fortress Kin-Guard or Bartolomé del Presidio on turn two is the ideal start; Anafenza in the opening seven is excellent but not required. Throw back hands full of three-drops with no early creature, hands with five or more lands, or hands with Vein Ripper and no black sources. Rogue's Passage alone does not count as a colored source in the opener.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Anafenza only sees nontoken deaths. Tokens are fuel for outlets and draw; nontoken creatures are the ones that grow her. Sequence accordingly: make a Spirit, sacrifice the Spirit to Vampiric Rites for a card, then sacrifice a nontoken body to Bartolomé for the endure trigger.
  • Endure is a choice. “Put counters on it or create a Spirit.” You may split decisions per trigger — counters when building a voltron kill, Spirits when you need fodder or blockers.
  • Krumar Initiate’s activation is sorcery-speed and costs life; the X/X Spirit it makes can be sacrificed the same turn, but only after the activation resolves.
  • Sandbender Scavengers reanimates on its own death for a creature with mana value equal to or less than its power. Feed it counters by sacrificing other permanents, then when it dies it returns your Anafenza or Vein Ripper from the graveyard.
  • Ruthless Lawbringer’s destroy is optional but the sacrifice is not: when it enters you may sacrifice a creature, and only if you do does the destroy trigger go on the stack.
  • Vein Ripper’s ward asks for a sacrifice — your opponent pays it by losing a creature, which triggers the drain anyway. Ward here is often a free 2 damage.
  • Social Snub copies if you control a creature when you cast it, so it usually hits each opponent once (or twice in a mirror of two players) for an edict plus a drain.

Matchups

  • Aggro: You are the control deck. Trade endure creatures, use the life gain from Rites and Ripper, and stabilize behind Vein Ripper’s 6/5 flying body. Shadowy Backstreet’s surveil finds answers.
  • Midrange: The engine out-values most fair decks — every removal spell they spend on your creatures is another Anafenza trigger and another drain from Ripper. Press the sacrifice loop every turn.
  • Control: Play around sweepers by keeping the voltron plan in hand until the turn you kill: grow Anafenza with activations, hold Rogue's Passage, and go over the top. Social Snub and Lawbringer answer their planeswalkers; ward on Vein Ripper taxes their spot removal.
