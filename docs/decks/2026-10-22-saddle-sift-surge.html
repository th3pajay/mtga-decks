Overview

Three mechanics, one loop. Mount gives the deck repeatable combat triggers that only exist while a creature is saddled. Mill fills the graveyard, and every mill payoff in the deck grows or recurs off graveyard size. Power-up is the payoff that the Mount and Mill halves make cheaper than they look: its cost shrinks by the creature’s own mana cost if that creature entered this turn, so anything you put onto the battlefield during combat is a discount.

Guardian Sunmare is the card that closes the circle. Whenever it attacks while saddled, you search your library for a nonland permanent with mana value 3 or less and put it directly onto the battlefield. That is a free body every single combat, and because the creature entered this turn, its power-up ability costs its own mana cost less. White Tiger, Ava Ayala enters for {1}{G}, so her power-up drops from {5}{G} to {4}, and it leaves behind The Tiger God, a legendary 4/4 green Cat God that can only be blocked by one creature. Bold Biochemist enters for {1}{U} and its {5}{U} power-up drops to {4} for a counter and two cards.

The mill half is what makes the combat half lethal. Stubborn Burrowfiend mills two cards every time it becomes saddled and gains +X/+X where X is the number of creature cards in your graveyard. Dark Matter Manipulator mills three on entry and gains +2/+0 for every seven cards in the graveyard. Blossoming Tortoise and Hedge Shredder both mill three and two respectively on attack, and Hedge Shredder puts milled land cards onto the battlefield tapped, which turns milling into ramp. A Burrowfiend that has watched ten creatures go to the yard is a 12/12 for two mana.

Ornery Tumblewagg converts all of that into a clock. At the beginning of combat it puts a +1/+1 counter on a creature, and whenever it attacks while saddled it doubles the number of +1/+1 counters on target creature. Counters grow geometrically turn after turn while the mill engine keeps converting library cards into Burrowfiend size.

The alternate kill is a mill-out that costs the opponent their whole library in two activations. Riverchurn Monument’s exhaust ability makes a player mill cards equal to the number of cards in their own graveyard. The Water Crystal adds four to every opposing mill. A player sitting on twenty cards in the graveyard mills twenty four, then the second Monument doubles what remains into the deck’s last card.

Approach

Game plan phases

Phase Turns What you are doing
Seed 1 to 2 Land drops, Dark Matter Manipulator (mills three), then a two-drop Mount or White Tiger
Saddle 3 to 4 Ornery Tumblewagg or Stubborn Burrowfiend, hold up Run Over, first saddled attack converts milling into size
Engine 4 to 6 Guardian Sunmare, Alacrian Armory for free saddles, Blossoming Tortoise and Hedge Shredder widening the graveyard
Convert 6 onward Doubling counters into Rogue’s Passage, or Riverchurn Monument plus The Water Crystal for the mill kill

The deck does not need to win a long game, but it does need one clean attack step per turn from turn three onward. Everything that does not add a body, a saddle, or a mill trigger is cut.

Gameplay strategy

Early game

Turn one is Dark Matter Manipulator when you have it: three cards in the graveyard immediately, and a 1/2 that grows into a real attacker once the yard crosses seven and fourteen. Turn two is White Tiger, Seraphic Steed or Stubborn Burrowfiend. Do not run the Burrowfiend out without a plan to saddle it on turn three, because its mill only happens when it becomes saddled.

Saddling is the cost the deck is built to cheat. Stubborn Burrowfiend and Ornery Tumblewagg both want only two power, so a two-drop plus any token or one-drop pays it. Seraphic Steed and Guardian Sunmare want four, which is why the Angel tokens from the Steed matter: one Angel plus a two-drop saddles the Sunmare every turn afterwards.

Mid game

Guardian Sunmare changes the texture of the deck. From the turn it lands, every combat does three things at once: it puts a permanent onto the battlefield for free, it triggers that permanent’s power-up at a discount because the permanent entered this turn, and it grows your board without spending a card. Fetch in this order:

  1. White Tiger, Ava Ayala when you need a body that cannot be chump blocked forever.
  2. Bold Biochemist when your hand is empty and you need the two cards.
  3. Stubborn Burrowfiend when the graveyard is already deep and you want the biggest possible attacker.
  4. Guidelight Matrix when you have no way to saddle the following turn.

Alacrian Armory is the second half of that plan. At the beginning of combat it saddles a Mount outright, so a Sunmare, Steed or Burrowfiend attack costs you zero creatures tapped. Every spare copy of Guidelight Matrix does the same job for {2} at sorcery speed.

The combo turn

The turn that usually ends the game:

  1. Start combat with Ornery Tumblewagg plus one other creature and a Mount that can be saddled.
  2. Tumblewagg’s beginning-of-combat trigger puts a +1/+1 counter on your best attacker, usually the Sunmare or a Burrowfiend.
  3. Saddle Tumblewagg (two power) and attack. Its saddled trigger doubles the counters on that creature.
  4. Sunmare attacks saddled as well, putting White Tiger onto the battlefield, then the postcombat main pays {4} for her power-up for a counter and The Tiger God.
  5. Activate Rogue’s Passage on whichever creature now has the most counters. Doubling counters plus an unblockable attacker is usually lethal two turns after the first doubling, and the pattern is 1, 4, 10, 22 counters if you keep doubling the same body.

Blossoming Tortoise makes Rogue’s Passage cost three instead of four, which is often the difference between doubling and connecting in the same turn.

Win condition 2

The mill kill does not care about blockers. Riverchurn Monument mills two to any target for {1} and a tap, and its exhaust ability makes a player mill cards equal to the number of cards in their own graveyard. The Water Crystal makes every opposing mill four cards larger. Sequence:

  1. Chip the opponent with Monument activations and any Dark Matter Manipulator mills while you build a board. Twenty cards in their graveyard is the target.
  2. Land The Water Crystal, then exhaust the first Monument: they mill twenty four, going to forty four.
  3. Exhaust the second Monument: they mill forty eight. Any library under ninety two cards is gone.

Against decks that gain life or wall up the ground, this is the plan that actually closes, and it costs two cards plus a Monument you were already tapping.

Mulligan guide

  • Keep: one or two green sources, a one-drop or two-drop creature, and ideally Run Over or Alacrian Armory to interact.
  • Keep: Dark Matter Manipulator plus Stubborn Burrowfiend, the fastest mill start in the deck.
  • Keep: Guardian Sunmare hands with three lands and any two-drop, since Sunmare repays the whole curve on its own.
  • Throw back: hands with no green source, hands with only Island and Swamp, and hands that need both Sunmare and Water Crystal to function.
  • Throw back: one-land hands even with a Manipulator, because the deck cannot afford to miss its third land drop with four colors in the list.

Key interactions and edge cases

  • Power-up discount is evaluated when you activate, not when the creature entered. Guardian Sunmare puts a creature in during combat, so its power-up is still discounted in your second main phase that same turn.
  • Saddle only as a sorcery. Alacrian Armory and Guidelight Matrix get around that by making a Mount saddled at the beginning of combat, which is also why they let you attack with every creature instead of tapping saddlers.
  • Stubborn Burrowfiend’s mill triggers only the first time it becomes saddled each turn, so saddling it twice in a turn does nothing extra.
  • Power-up abilities can be activated once per creature, but a creature that leaves and returns is a new object. Unliving Legionnaire’s power-up returns a creature to hand, and replaying it re-arms its own power-up.
  • Hedge Shredder puts milled lands onto the battlefield tapped. With Blossoming Tortoise out, milling becomes ramp, and the Tortoise’s “activated abilities of lands cost {1} less” reduces Rogue’s Passage and Restless Cottage activation costs.
  • The Water Crystal only adds four when an opponent would mill. Your own self-mill from Burrowfiend, Tortoise and Shredder is unaffected.
  • Riverchurn Monument’s exhaust counts the graveyard at the moment it resolves, so activate it after combat, not before, once you have added anything to their yard.
  • Restless Cottage enters tapped. Do not count it as a green source on turn one.

Matchups

Opponent Plan
Mono-red and other aggro Run Over their best attacker, wall with Seraphic Steed’s lifelink and Angel tokens, and race with doubling counters. The deck has no sweepers, so trade early.
Midrange and typal Mill is your friend. Play around their one big blocker by holding Rogue’s Passage mana, and win the long game with The Water Crystal plus Monuments.
Control Do not overextend into a sweeper. Keep one Mount in hand, use Sunmare on the turn you cast it, and lean on the mill kill since it needs no board.
Combo and ramp You are the faster clock only with a turn three saddle. Keep Run Over for their engine creature and remember that Riverchurn Monument can win through any board stall.
